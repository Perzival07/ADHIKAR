# Ganga Kalyana Scheme (Karnataka)

## Summary
100% free drilling of irrigation borewells, installation of high-power submersible pumps, and energization for small/marginal farmers from SC, ST, OBC, and minority groups.

## Benefit
Fully government-financed individual/community borewell drilling up to ₹4 Lakh per unit (including pump, electrification, and pipeline laying) with zero farmer cost.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a small or marginal farmer belonging to SC, ST, Backward Classes, or Minority community in Karnataka.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **RTC / Pahani Land Record (1.5 to 5 acres)** (Bhoomi Karnataka): Proves dry agricultural land ownership.
- **Integrated Caste and Income Certificate** (Tahsildar / Nadakacheri): Confirms eligible community and income.
- **Certificate of No Existing Borewell on Land** (Village Accountant (VA)): Mandatory requirement.

## How to Apply
### Seva Sindhu Ganga Kalyana Portal (online)
Apply online at sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in under respective corporation.
Link: https://kmdc.karnataka.gov.in

### District Manager, Development Corporation Office (blockOffice)
Submit hard copies for geophysical survey scheduling.

## Caveats
Applicant must own between 1.5 acres and 5 acres of dry land not having existing irrigation facilities.

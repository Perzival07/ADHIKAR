# Kerala Social Security Pension (Direct Monthly DBT)

## Summary
Direct social pension of ₹1,600 per month credited directly to bank accounts or delivered at doorstep by cooperative banks for seniors, widows, and disabled.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,600 directly credited to bank account or hand-delivered in cash by primary agricultural credit societies (PACS).

## Eligibility
You qualify as an elderly person (60+), widow, disabled person (40%+), or agricultural worker residing in Kerala with family income under ₹1 Lakh.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Income Certificate (under ₹1 Lakh)** (Village Officer Kerala): Mandatory economic proof.
- **Age Proof (Aadhaar / School Certificate showing 60+)** (UIDAI / School Board): Required for Old Age Pension.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank / Post Office Account** (Bank Branch / Cooperative Bank): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Grama Panchayat / Municipal Office Sevana Desk (panchayat)
Submit application form with Village Officer income certificate.

### Sevana Pension Software Portal (online)
Track DBT payment history and sanction at welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in.
Link: https://welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in

## Caveats
Family must not own a 4-wheeler (except taxi driven for livelihood) or residential house exceeding 1,200 sq ft.

# Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year from Maharashtra government in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000, providing total ₹12,000 per year when combined with PM-KISAN.

## Benefit
₹6,000 per year DBT credited directly into the farmer’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account in 3 installments (April-July, August-November, December-March).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landholding farmer in Maharashtra actively receiving PM-KISAN benefits.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Tax Exempt)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **PM-KISAN Beneficiary Registration ID** (Agriculture Dept): Mandatory eligibility baseline.
- **7/12 & 8-A Land Record Extract** (Talathi / Mahabhulekh): Proves agricultural land holdings.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Talathi / Krishi Sevak / Gram Panchayat Office (panchayat)
Verify land record integration on e-Kisan portal.

### Maharashtra Agriculture Portal (online)
Check installment payment status at krishi.maharashtra.gov.in.
Link: https://krishi.maharashtra.gov.in

## Caveats
Landholding must be verified on Mahabhulekh (7/12 extract) and bank account seeded with NPCI mapper.

# Vishwakarma Kamgar Kalyan Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
Direct cash assistance of ₹5,000 to purchase modern toolkits for traditional artisans, street vendors, tailors, potters, carpenters, and gig workers in Rajasthan.

## Benefit
₹5,000 direct benefit transfer credited into the artisan’s bank account to purchase professional hand tools, sewing machines, or trade equipment + ₹10,000 exhibition grant.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing artisan, craftsman, or street vendor residing in Rajasthan aged 18 to 40 with family income under ₹3 Lakh.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jan Aadhaar Card** (Jan Aadhaar Authority): Citizen identification in Rajasthan.
- **Artisan Pehchan Card / Udyam Aadhar / Vending Certificate** (MSME / Municipality): Proves traditional artisanal trade.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Rajasthan** (Bank Branch): Required for ₹5,000 direct credit.

## How to Apply
### SSO Rajasthan Industries Portal (online)
Apply online at sso.rajasthan.gov.in under Industries Kamgar scheme.
Link: https://industries.rajasthan.gov.in

### District Industries Centre (DIC) Rajasthan (blockOffice)
Submit verification copy to General Manager DIC.

## Caveats
Applicant must upload photo of trade work and GST/Udyam or municipal vending certificate.

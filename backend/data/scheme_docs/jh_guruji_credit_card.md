# Guruji Student Credit Card Scheme (GSCC Jharkhand)

## Summary
Collateral-free education loan of up to ₹15 Lakhs at nominal 4% simple interest for students from Jharkhand to pursue higher professional education in India.

## Benefit
Loans up to ₹15 Lakh with no collateral or third-party guarantee, with 30% of loan amount usable for personal/hostel expenses (laptop, food, books).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student who passed 10th and 12th from Jharkhand admitted to a recognized higher education institution in India.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 17–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **College Allotment / Admission Confirmation Slip** (Recognized University / Institute): Validates course enrollment.
- **10th and 12th Board Marksheets from Jharkhand schools** (JAC / CBSE / ICSE): Mandatory schooling in Jharkhand.
- **Residential Certificate (Nivas Praman Patra)** (Circle Officer / SDO): Proves state domicile.

## How to Apply
### Guruji Student Credit Card Portal (online)
Apply online at gscc.jharkhand.gov.in.
Link: https://gscc.jharkhand.gov.in

### Designated Lending Bank Branch in Jharkhand (bankBranch)
Sign loan agreement and activate credit card facility.

## Caveats
Repayment starts 1 year after course completion or upon securing employment, whichever is earlier.

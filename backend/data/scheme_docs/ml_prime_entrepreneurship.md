# PRIME Meghalaya Entrepreneurship Promotion Program

## Summary
Incubation support, zero-collateral soft loans up to ₹5 Lakh at zero interest, and prototype seed funding grants up to ₹5 Lakh for local youth enterprises.

## Benefit
Zero-interest enterprise soft loans up to ₹5 Lakh + direct seed grant of ₹1 Lakh to ₹5 Lakh (PRIME Sauramandala Fund) + access to PRIME Startup Hubs in Shillong & Tura.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an aspiring entrepreneur or youth enterprise founder residing in Meghalaya with a viable business plan.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **EPIC Voter ID / Permanent Resident Certificate of Meghalaya** (Election Commission / DC Office): Mandatory residence proof in Meghalaya.
- **PRIME Business Application Pitch and Financial Plan** (Self): Details product, target market, and budget.
- **Proprietorship / Individual Bank Account** (Bank Branch in Meghalaya): Required for loan/grant disbursement.

## How to Apply
### PRIME Meghalaya Portal (online)
Apply online for incubation cohort at primemeghalaya.com.
Link: https://primemeghalaya.com

### PRIME Startup Hub (Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium Complex, Shillong / Tura) (blockOffice)
Attend screening pitch and mentoring sessions.

## Caveats
Applicant must participate in the mandatory PRIME incubation screening and enterprise bootcamp.

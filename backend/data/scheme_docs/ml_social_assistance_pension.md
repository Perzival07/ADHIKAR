# Meghalaya Social Assistance Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly pension of ₹500 per month for destitute senior citizens (60+), widows, and persons with benchmark disabilities residing in Meghalaya.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹500 credited directly into the destitute citizen’s savings bank account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 60+, widow, or disabled person residing in Meghalaya with annual family income below ₹36,000.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Village Headman (Rangbah Shnong / Nokma) Poverty Certificate** (Rangbah Shnong / Nokma): Confirms destitute status.
- **Age Certificate showing 60+ (Aadhaar / Voter ID)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Verifies age criteria.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Meghalaya** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit of ₹500.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) / Block Development Office (blockOffice)
Submit application with village headman certificate.

### Meghalaya Social Welfare Portal (online)
Track pension sanction lists at megsocialwelfare.gov.in.
Link: https://megsocialwelfare.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must not be in receipt of any other regular government or statutory pension.

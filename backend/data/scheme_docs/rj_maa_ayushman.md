# Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya (MAA) Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
Universal cashless hospitalisation coverage of up to ₹25 Lakhs per family per year across 1,800+ empanelled government and private hospitals across Rajasthan.

## Benefit
100% cashless medical care up to ₹25 Lakh per family per year covering kidney, heart, liver transplants, oncology, neurosurgery, ICU, and free post-discharge drugs.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident family of Rajasthan possessing an active Jan Aadhaar card.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jan Aadhaar Card / Jan Aadhaar Enrolment Slip** (DOIT&C / Jan Aadhaar Authority Rajasthan): Primary card for hospital pre-authorization.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for hospital authorization.

## How to Apply
### Hospital Health Guide / Swasthya Margdarshak Desk (csc)
Show Jan Aadhaar card for instant admission approval.

### MAA Rajasthan Health Portal (online)
Check hospital empanelment and package list at health.rajasthan.gov.in.
Link: https://health.rajasthan.gov.in

## Caveats
Free universal cover for NFSA, small farmers, and contractual staff (other families pay nominal premium of ₹850/yr).

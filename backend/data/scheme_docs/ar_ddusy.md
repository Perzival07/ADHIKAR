# Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Swavalamban Yojana (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Front-ended capital investment subsidy of 30% to 40% on project bank loans from ₹10 Lakh up to ₹50 Lakh for youth setting up eco-tourism, agri, or manufacturing ventures.

## Benefit
30% capital subsidy (40% for women entrepreneurs) on bank loans up to ₹50 Lakh + low commercial lending rates.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed educated youth of Arunachal Pradesh establishing a new enterprise.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Arunachal Pradesh Scheduled Tribe (APST) Certificate** (Deputy Commissioner Office): Mandatory for indigenous youth subsidy.
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** (Chartered Accountant / Self): Specifies machinery, land, and budget.
- **In-principle Bank Sanction Letter** (Scheduled Commercial Bank): Confirms bank financing readiness.

## How to Apply
### District Industries Centre (DIC) / Deputy Commissioner Office (blockOffice)
Submit project dossier to General Manager DIC.

### Arunachal Single Window Portal (online)
Track industrial clearance and subsidy release.
Link: https://www.itanagar.nic.in

## Caveats
Applicant must contribute at least 10% promoter equity and possess APST certificate.

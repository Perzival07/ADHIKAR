# Biju Swasthya Kalyan Yojana / Gopabandhu Jan Arogya Yojana (Odisha)

## Summary
Universal cashless hospitalisation coverage of up to ₹5 Lakh for male members and ₹10 Lakh for female family members per year across 950+ empanelled hospitals.

## Benefit
100% cashless medical care up to ₹5 Lakh (males) and ₹10 Lakh (females) per family per year covering critical surgeries, oncology, kidney dialysis, and ICU across network hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident family of Odisha holding an active NFSA or SFSS state ration card.

- Region: Odisha
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **BSKY Smart Card / NFSA Ration Card of Odisha** (Food Supplies & Health Dept): Primary card for hospital admission.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for hospital authorization.

## How to Apply
### Swasthya Mitra Helpdesk at Network Hospital (csc)
Show card for instant biometric admission pre-authorization.

### BSKY Odisha Health Portal (online)
Search empanelled hospitals and package rates at bsky.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://bsky.odisha.gov.in

## Caveats
Card covers all declared family members without any personal copayment.

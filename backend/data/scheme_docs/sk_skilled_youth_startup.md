# Skilled Youth Startup Scheme (SYSS Sikkim)

## Summary
Financial support with up to 50% back-ended capital subsidy on bank loans up to ₹20 Lakhs for educated and skilled unemployed youth to set up manufacturing and service enterprises.

## Benefit
50% project subsidy (up to ₹10 Lakhs) on term loans up to ₹20 Lakhs with soft interest rates for 5 years.

## Eligibility
You are an educated or vocationally skilled unemployed resident youth of Sikkim planning an enterprise.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th / 12th / ITI / Graduate
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Certificate of Identification (COI)** (SDM Office): Proof of state residency.
- **Skill / Technical Qualification Certificate** (ITI / NSDC / University): Proof of technical competence.
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** (Self / Consultant): Business plan and machinery costing.

## How to Apply
### District Industries Centre (DIC) / Directorate of Industries Gangtok (blockOffice)
Submit project proposal for DLC bank linkage screening.

### Industries Department Sikkim Portal (online)
Download application formats at industries.sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://industries.sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Candidate must possess skill training certificate (ITI, Diploma, or Skill India certification).

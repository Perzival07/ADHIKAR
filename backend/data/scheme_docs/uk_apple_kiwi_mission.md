# Uttarakhand Apple & Kiwi Mission (High Density Horticulture)

## Summary
80% subsidy on imported dwarf/semi-dwarf high-density rootstock apple and kiwi saplings, micro-drip irrigation systems, and anti-hail nets to establish high-income commercial orchards in hill blocks.

## Benefit
80% financial subsidy on saplings, trellis wire support, micro-irrigation, and protective anti-hail netting for commercial orchard establishment.

## Eligibility
You are a farmer or landholder in the hill districts of Uttarakhand establishing fruit orchards.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Pre-winter booking cycle)

## Documents Required
- **Land Revenue Record (Khatoni)** (Revenue Department): Proof of suitable farm land holding.
- **Uttarakhand Mool Niwas Certificate** (Tahsildar / SDM): Residency proof.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct subsidy credit.

## How to Apply
### Chief Horticulture Officer (CHO) / Block Horticulture Mobile Team (blockOffice)
Submit planting proposal and soil test report.

### Uttarakhand Horticulture Mission Portal (online)
Book saplings online at shm.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://shm.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Minimum landholding of 0.2 hectares at altitude suitable for target fruit variety.

# Savitribai Phule Kishori Samriddhi Yojana (Jharkhand)

## Summary
Cumulative scholarship of ₹40,000 for adolescent girls from Class 8 to 12 (₹2,500 each in 8th/9th, ₹5,000 each in 10th/11th/12th, and ₹20,000 at age 18-19).

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹40,000 in stages credited directly into the girl’s bank account to encourage school retention and eliminate child marriage.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female student enrolled in Class 8, 9, 10, 11, or 12 in a government or aided school in Jharkhand.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 12–19
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School Enrolment Certificate with Class Details** (School Headmaster): Enrolment proof in Class 8-12.
- **Girl's Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Girl's Individual Bank Account Passbook** (Bank Branch / Pragya Kendra): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### School Principal / Anganwadi Worker Desk (panchayat)
Submit form through school teacher or local Anganwadi center.

### Jharkhand Social Welfare Portal (online)
Track application and disbursement status.
Link: https://jharkhand.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicable to all girl students from eligible households without caste restrictions.

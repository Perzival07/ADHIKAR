# Punjab Ghar Ghar Rozgar Mission (PGRKAM)

## Summary
Statewide job facilitation platform offering free skill training, mega job fairs, foreign study and overseas employment counseling, and enterprise startup loans.

## Benefit
Direct job placements with leading corporations + free certified vocational training at District Bureaus of Employment & Enterprise (DBEE) + foreign study visa guidance.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed youth residing in Punjab holding 10th/12th/ITI/Diploma/Degree qualification.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Educational Marksheet / Degree Certificate** (Recognized University / Board in Punjab): Academic proof.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Updated Resume / CV** (Self): Uploaded on portal for company matching.

## How to Apply
### PGRKAM Official Portal (online)
Register, browse corporate vacancies, and apply at pgrkam.com.
Link: https://pgrkam.com

### District Bureau of Employment & Enterprise (DBEE) (blockOffice)
Attend walk-in interviews and overseas counseling at DBEE.

## Caveats
Candidate must create and verify an active profile on the PGRKAM portal.

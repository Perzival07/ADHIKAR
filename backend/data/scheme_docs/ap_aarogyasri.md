# Dr. YSR Aarogyasri Scheme (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Universal cashless medical and surgical treatment cover of up to ₹25 Lakhs per family per year across 3,250+ procedures in empanelled hospitals.

## Benefit
100% cashless medical care up to ₹25 Lakhs per family per year, free medicines, diagnostics, and post-operative Aarogya Aasara financial allowance of ₹225/day.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Andhra Pradesh holding a Rice Card or with annual family income up to ₹5 Lakhs.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aarogyasri Card / Rice Card** (Civil Supplies Dept AP): Primary card for instant hospital authorization.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for patient e-KYC.

## How to Apply
### Aarogyasri Beneficiary Portal (online)
Search empanelled hospitals and specialty treatments.
Link: https://ysraarogyasri.ap.gov.in

### Aarogyasri Mitra Helpdesk at Network Hospital (csc)
Visit hospital desk directly with Aadhaar or Rice Card for instant pre-authorization.

## Caveats
Covers all BPL/Rice card families and families with landholding up to 12 dry acres / 35 wet acres.

# Mukh Mantri Sehat Bima Yojana (Punjab)

## Summary
Cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalisation coverage up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year across 900+ empanelled government and private hospitals in Punjab and Chandigarh.

## Benefit
100% cashless medical treatment covering 1,600+ medical and surgical packages, cancer treatment, heart surgery, and ICU care across network hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Punjab covered under Smart Ration Card, J-form farmer, construction worker, or registered journalist.

- Region: Punjab
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Punjab Smart Ration Card / J-Form (Farmer)** (Food Dept / Mandi Board Punjab): Primary eligibility document.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for hospital authorization.

## How to Apply
### Hospital Arogyamitra Helpdesk at Empanelled Network Hospital (csc)
Show card to Arogyamitra for instant admission approval.

### State Health Agency Punjab Portal (online)
Search empanelled hospitals and verify card at shapunjab.in.
Link: https://shapunjab.in

## Caveats
Farmers holding J-Forms issued by Mandi Board are automatically eligible for full family coverage.

# Lakhpati Didi Initiative (DAY-NRLM)

## Summary
Empowerment mission enabling rural women SHG members to earn a sustainable income of at least ₹1 Lakh per year through micro-enterprises, value chain integration, and digital tools.

## Benefit
Zero/low interest community investment fund loans up to ₹5 Lakh, skill training in drone piloting (Drone Didi), solar assembly, agri-processing, and guaranteed market linkages via ONDC/GeM.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an active member of a registered Women Self Help Group (SHG) under Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - NRLM.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **SHG Membership Passbook & ID** (Village Organisation / CLF): Proves active membership and savings record.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Individual identity verification.
- **Individual Bank Account Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for direct credit of enterprise loans and subsidies.

## How to Apply
### Village Organisation (VO) / Cluster Level Federation (CLF) (panchayat)
Submit livelihood micro-plan through your SHG animator.
Link: https://aajeevika.gov.in

### Aajeevika NRLM Portal (online)
Track enterprise certification and digital marketplace access.
Link: https://aajeevika.gov.in

## Caveats
Candidate must be an active member of an SHG graded satisfactorily under NRLM panchayat norms.

# Tripura Social Security Pension Scheme (Mukhyamantri Social Pension)

## Summary
Monthly financial pension of ₹2,000 provided to indigent elderly citizens aged 60+, widows, deserted women, and destitute persons not covered under NSAP central pensions.

## Benefit
Monthly direct DBT cash transfer of ₹2,000 credited on the 1st of every month to the beneficiary’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You are a resident of Tripura aged 60+ or a widow with family income below poverty threshold.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 1.0 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRTC)** (SDM Office): Proof of state residence.
- **Aadhaar Card / Voter Card showing Age 60+** (UIDAI / ECI): Confirms minimum age.
- **BPL Ration Card / Income Certificate** (Food Dept / SDM): Proof of indigent economic status.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat Secretary / Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) (panchayat)
Submit physical pension application form with PRTC and BPL card.

### Tripura State Portal (online)
Track scheme notices at tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must not be drawing any regular salary or another state/central pension.

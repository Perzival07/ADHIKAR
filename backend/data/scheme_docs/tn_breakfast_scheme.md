# Chief Minister’s Breakfast Scheme (Mudhalvarin Kaalai Unavu Thittam)

## Summary
Free hot and nutritious cooked breakfast served every school morning to over 17 lakh students studying in Classes 1 to 5 across all 31,000+ government primary schools in Tamil Nadu.

## Benefit
Free wholesome breakfast (Pongal, Khichdi, Upma, Rava Pongal with Sambhar) to eliminate morning hunger and improve learning outcomes.

## Eligibility
You are a child enrolled in Classes 1 to 5 in any Tamil Nadu government primary school.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 5–11
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Primary School (Class 1-5)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Government Primary School Enrollment Record** (Primary School Headmaster): Automatic entitlement upon school enrollment.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary School Campus (blockOffice)
Breakfast is served directly on campus every school working morning.

### Tamil Nadu State Portal (online)
Review program guidelines at tn.gov.in.
Link: https://www.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Universal coverage for all students enrolled in state primary schools.

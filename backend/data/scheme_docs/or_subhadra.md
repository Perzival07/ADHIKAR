# Subhadra Yojana (Odisha)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹50,000 provided across 5 years in annual installments of ₹10,000 (two ₹5,000 installments on Rakhi Purnima & International Women’s Day) for women aged 21 to 60.

## Benefit
₹50,000 total DBT delivered as ₹10,000/year (₹5,000 on Rakhi Purnima and ₹5,000 on March 8 International Women’s Day) + Subhadra ATM Debit Card + digital transaction incentives.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female permanent resident of Odisha aged 21 to 60 covered under NFSA/SFSS or family income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 21–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card with linked mobile number** (UIDAI): Mandatory for biometric/OTP e-KYC.
- **NFSA / SFSS Ration Card of Odisha** (Food Supplies & Consumer Welfare Dept): Validates economic priority.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Single Bank Account Passbook** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct credit of ₹10,000/year.

## How to Apply
### Mo Seva Kendra (MSK) / Anganwadi Centre / Jana Seva Kendra (csc)
Submit paper form or complete biometric registration free of cost.

### Subhadra Odisha Official Portal (online)
Apply online and track DBT installment status at subhadra.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in

## Caveats
Women from families paying income tax or holding regular government employment or owning more than 5 acres of irrigated land are excluded.

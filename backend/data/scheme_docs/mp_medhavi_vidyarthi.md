# Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana (MMVY Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
100% full tuition fee payment by MP government for meritorious students who secured 70%+ in MP Board (or 85%+ in CBSE/ICSE) admitted to Engineering, Medical, Law, or Degree courses.

## Benefit
Full tuition fee reimbursement (even exceeding ₹10-15 Lakhs in government/private medical colleges and IITs) directly paid by MP government to the institution.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student with 70%+ in MP Board (or 85%+ CBSE) residing in MP admitted to an eligible higher education institution with family income under ₹6 Lakh.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 17–26
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Class 12th Board Marksheet (70%+ MP Board or 85%+ CBSE)** (MPBSE / CBSE / ICSE): Mandatory merit threshold.
- **JEE Main / NEET / CLAT Allotment Order and Rank Card** (NTA / Consortium of NLUs / DTE): Proves merit admission in eligible institute.
- **Income Certificate showing family income under ₹6 Lakh** (Tehsildar / Lok Seva Kendra): Income criteria validation.

## How to Apply
### MP State Scholarship Portal 2.0 (MMVY Section) (online)
Apply online at scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/MedhaviChhatra.
Link: https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in

### College Nodal Scholarship Officer Desk (panchayat)
Submit verification copy to college principal.

## Caveats
Students taking benefit in private medical colleges must execute a mandatory rural service bond upon graduation.

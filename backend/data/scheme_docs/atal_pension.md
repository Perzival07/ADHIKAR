# Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

## Summary
Government-guaranteed lifelong pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000 or ₹5,000 per month from age 60 for unorganised sector workers.

## Benefit
Guaranteed minimum monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 for life to subscriber, lifelong pension to spouse upon subscriber death, and return of corpus to nominee.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an Indian citizen aged between 18 and 40 years holding a savings bank account and not paying income tax.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Non-taxpayer)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Primary identification and e-KYC.
- **Savings Bank Account Passbook with auto-debit mandate** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Account must maintain balance on auto-debit date.

## How to Apply
### eNPS APY Online Registration (online)
Register online using net banking or Aadhaar OTP on Protean CRA.
Link: https://enps.nsdl.com

### Any Commercial Bank, Grameen Bank, or Post Office (bankBranch)
Fill one-page APY enrolment form and set monthly debit mandate.

## Caveats
Taxpayers are excluded from joining APY. Contributions are auto-debited monthly/quarterly from savings bank account.

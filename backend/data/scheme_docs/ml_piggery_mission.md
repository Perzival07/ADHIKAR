# Meghalaya Piggery & Livestock Mission

## Summary
Zero-interest bank loans up to ₹5 Lakh and 50% capital subsidies for commercial piggery breeding, fattening farms, and modern hygienic butcheries.

## Benefit
50% capital subsidy on pig sty construction, exotic breeding stock (Hampshire/Yorkshire), and feed machinery + zero percent interest rate on bank loan component.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a livestock farmer or cooperative society member in Meghalaya with suitable land for piggery farming.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Possession Certificate / Village Headman NOC for Piggery** (Village Headman (Rangbah Shnong / Nokma)): Verifies suitable land away from water bodies.
- **Piggery Training Certificate** (Veterinary Officer): Mandatory skill prerequisite.
- **Bank Passbook with Meghalaya Apex Bank / Commercial Bank** (Bank Branch): Direct loan and subsidy disbursement.

## How to Apply
### District Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Officer (DAH&VO) (blockOffice)
Submit application with village headman NOC.

### Meghalaya AH&Veterinary Portal (online)
Download guidelines and forms at megahvd.gov.in.
Link: https://megahvd.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must complete the mandatory 3-day piggery management training at the District Veterinary Farm.

# Chief Minister's Arogya Arunachal Yojana (CMAAY)

## Summary
Cashless health insurance cover up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalization in impanelled hospitals across Arunachal and premier national centers.

## Benefit
Cashless hospitalisation coverage of ₹5 Lakh per family per year, integrated seamlessly with Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous APST resident or regular state government employee/public resident of Arunachal Pradesh.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **APST Certificate or Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC)** (Deputy Commissioner): Confirms domicile.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Mandatory for linking family roster.

## How to Apply
### CMAAY Online Registration Portal (online)
Register online and generate digital CMAAY Card at cmaay.com.
Link: https://cmaay.com

### District Hospital / Empanelled Hospital Kiosk (csc)
Approach CMAAY Arogya Mitra with Aadhaar card.

## Caveats
APST members and non-APST state government employees and permanent resident certificate (PRC) holders are eligible.

# Dayanand Social Security Scheme (DSSS Goa)

## Summary
Direct social pension of ₹2,000 to ₹3,500 per month for senior citizens (60+), widows, disabled persons, and single women residing in Goa.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹2,000 (Senior Citizens & Widows), ₹3,500 (Disabled persons with 90%+ disability), and ₹2,500 (Severe chronic patients).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Goa for 15+ years aged 60+ or widow/divorced/disabled person with family income under ₹1.5 Lakh.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **15-Year Residence Certificate** (Mamlatdar): Mandatory residence proof in Goa.
- **Income Certificate under ₹1.5 Lakh** (Mamlatdar Office): Validates economic criteria.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Goa** (Bank Branch in Goa): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Goa Online Portal (DSSS Section) (online)
Apply online via goaonline.gov.in.
Link: https://goaonline.gov.in

### Taluka Social Welfare Office / Mamlatdar Office (blockOffice)
Submit hard copies for field inquiry.

## Caveats
Applicant must have 15 years continuous residence in Goa and not draw any other statutory pension.

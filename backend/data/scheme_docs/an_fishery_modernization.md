# Andaman Livelihood and Fishery Modernization Scheme

## Summary
50% capital subsidy on motorized fishing crafts, deep-sea safety kits, GPS navigators, and insulated ice-boxes for island fishers.

## Benefit
50% financial subsidy on outboard motors, FRP boats, safety beacons, and subsidized marine fuel for registered traditional fishermen.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a registered traditional fisherman or member of a fisheries cooperative in Andaman & Nicobar.

- Region: Andaman and Nicobar Islands
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Biometric Fisherman ID Card** (Directorate of Fisheries A&N): Proves active traditional fisher status.
- **Islander Identity Card / Local Certificate** (Deputy Commissioner Port Blair / Car Nicobar): Confirms island residency.
- **Bank Passbook** (Commercial Bank): Required for subsidy DBT.

## How to Apply
### Zonal Fisheries Office (Port Blair / Diglipur / Mayabunder) (blockOffice)
Submit application with boat quotations.
Link: https://andaman.gov.in

### A&N Administration Portal (online)
Download scheme application forms and view approved vendor lists.
Link: https://andaman.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must hold a valid Fisherman Biometric ID card issued by the Coastal Security Police / Fisheries Dept.

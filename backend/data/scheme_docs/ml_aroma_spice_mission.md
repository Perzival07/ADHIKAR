# Meghalaya Aroma & Spice Mission (Lakadong Turmeric Scheme)

## Summary
Free distribution of high-curcumin Lakadong turmeric rhizomes, ginger seeds, distillation units, and 50% subsidy on solar spice dryers for hill farmers.

## Benefit
100% free certified Lakadong planting seed material + 50% subsidy up to ₹1.5 Lakh for solar polyhouse dryers and rhizome washing machines + buyback at premium price.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a spice farmer or member of a spice cooperative in Jaintia Hills or across Meghalaya cultivating turmeric or ginger.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Possession Record / Headman Land Allotment** (Rangbah Shnong / Nokma): Proves agricultural cultivation plot.
- **EPIC Voter ID and Aadhaar Card** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook in Meghalaya** (Bank Branch): Required for subsidy DBT.

## How to Apply
### District Horticulture Officer (DHO) / Horticulture Development Officer (HDO) (blockOffice)
Register with block horticulture office for seed allotment.

### Meghalaya Agriculture Portal (online)
Check spice mission targets at megagri.gov.in.
Link: https://megagri.gov.in

## Caveats
Farmers must follow organic protocol to preserve high-curcumin export certification standards.

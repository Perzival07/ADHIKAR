# Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY Odisha)

## Summary
Direct social welfare pension of ₹1,000 per month (aged 60-79) and ₹1,200 per month (aged 80+, or disabled with 60%+ disability) delivered on the 15th of every month.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,000 to ₹1,200 credited directly into the beneficiary’s bank account or hand-delivered on the 15th (Pension Day) at Gram Panchayat.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen (60+), widow, disabled person, or transgender person residing in Odisha with family income under ₹40,000.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Income Certificate showing family income under ₹40,000** (Tahasildar / Revenue Inspector): Mandatory poverty proof.
- **Age Certificate (Aadhaar / Voter ID showing 60+)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Required for Old Age Pension.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Odisha** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### SSEPD Odisha Portal (MBPY Online) (online)
Apply online at ssepd.odisha.gov.in under Social Security services.
Link: https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in

### Block Development Office (BDO) / Gram Panchayat Executive Officer (PEO) (panchayat)
Submit physical application on Pension Day.

## Caveats
Applicant must have resided continuously in Odisha for at least 10 years.

# Mukhyamantri Balak / Balika Protsahan Yojana (Bihar)

## Summary
One-time direct cash incentive of ₹10,000 for students passing Class 10 (Matriculation) Board Examinations in 1st division, and ₹8,000 for 2nd division SC/ST students.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹10,000 (1st division, all categories) and ₹8,000 (2nd division, SC/ST) into student’s personal bank account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student in Bihar who secured 1st division in BSEB Class 10th matriculation board examination.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 14–20
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **BSEB 10th Board Marksheet and Admit Card** (Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB)): Verifies 1st division result.
- **Student Personal Bank Account with IFSC** (Bank Branch in Bihar): Required for DBT.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### MedhaSoft Matric Protsahan Portal (online)
Submit application and verify registration number at medhasoft.bih.nic.in.
Link: https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in

### High School Principal Office (panchayat)
School head verifies student bank details on portal.

## Caveats
Student must hold an individual bank account opened in their own name in Bihar.

# School Fagathansi Mission (Manipur)

## Summary
Free modern digital smart classrooms, science labs, language laboratories, free bilingual textbooks, school bags, and uniforms for students across Manipur schools.

## Benefit
Zero school tuition fee, free uniforms, textbooks, high-tech computer education, and specialized entrance coaching for students in Classes 1 to 12.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student enrolled in a government or local body school across Manipur.

- Region: Manipur
- Age: 6–18
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School Admission Record / Student Bonafide** (School Headmaster): Enrolment proof.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary / Higher Secondary School Principal Desk (panchayat)
Entitlements distributed directly on admission.
Link: https://manipureducation.gov.in

### Manipur Education Portal (online)
Access digital e-textbooks and smart classroom modules.
Link: https://manipureducation.gov.in

## Caveats
Universal entitlement for all enrolled students with zero charges.

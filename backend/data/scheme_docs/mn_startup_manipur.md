# StartUp Manipur (StandUp / IDEA / Revenue Stage Scheme)

## Summary
Direct seed funding grants of ₹3 Lakh (Idea stage) and soft project loans up to ₹1 Crore with 30% capital subsidy for innovative entrepreneurs and local artisans.

## Benefit
Direct grant of ₹3 Lakh (Idea Stage) with zero equity/repayment + ₹10 Lakh to ₹1 Crore commercial loans with 30% state subsidy for scaling ventures.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a young entrepreneur or innovator residing in Manipur with an innovative business concept.

- Region: Manipur
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) of Manipur** (Deputy Commissioner): Mandatory residence proof.
- **Business Concept Proposal and Financial Estimates** (Self): Technical and commercial details.
- **Bank Account Details of Proprietorship / Firm** (Bank Branch in Manipur): Required for grant disbursement.

## How to Apply
### StartUp Manipur Portal (online)
Register online and submit pitch deck at startupmanipur.in.
Link: https://startupmanipur.in

### Planning Department / EDC Manipur (blockOffice)
Present pitch before State Level Screening Committee.

## Caveats
Applicant must be a permanent resident of Manipur and contribute at least 5% promoter equity for scale-up stages.

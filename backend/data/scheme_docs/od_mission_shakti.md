# Mission Shakti Financial Inclusion (Zero Percent Interest Loans, Odisha)

## Summary
Zero percent interest loans up to ₹5 Lakh for Women Self-Help Groups (WSHGs) across Odisha repaying bank loans on time, making micro-enterprise credit 100% interest-free.

## Benefit
Full bank loan interest reimbursed directly into WSHG bank accounts via DBT, resulting in 0% effective interest on loans up to ₹5 Lakh for micro-enterprises and agri-business.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an active member of a registered Women SHG under Mission Shakti in Odisha.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mission Shakti WSHG Unique ID and Passbook** (Block Mission Shakti Federation): Validates group registration.
- **Bank Loan Repayment Passbook** (Commercial Bank in Odisha): Proves timely repayment for 0% interest.

## How to Apply
### Grama Panchayat Level Federation (GPLF) / Block Mission Shakti Desk (panchayat)
Apply through Mission Shakti Animator or Coordinator.

### Mission Shakti Official Portal (online)
Check loan interest reimbursement status at missionshakti.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://missionshakti.odisha.gov.in

## Caveats
WSHG must be graded satisfactorily and maintain regular repayment schedule with zero NPA default.

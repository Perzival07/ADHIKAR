# Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana (MSY Uttarakhand)

## Summary
Subsidized loan scheme providing up to 25% capital subsidy on bank loans up to ₹25 Lakhs for manufacturing units and up to ₹10 Lakhs for service/business enterprises for reverse migrants and local youth.

## Benefit
Up to 25% capital subsidy (up to ₹6.25 Lakhs in Category A hilly districts) credited to margin money account + interest subvention.

## Eligibility
You are a resident of Uttarakhand aged 18+ seeking to establish a micro-enterprise or service unit.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Uttarakhand Domicile Certificate (Mool Niwas)** (Tahsildar / SDM): Residency proof.
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** (Self / Consultant): Business cost and machinery estimate.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank): For margin money linkage.

## How to Apply
### General Manager, District Industries Centre (DIC) (blockOffice)
Appear before DTFC interview for bank loan forwarding.

### MSY Single Window Portal (online)
Apply online at msy.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://msy.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Higher subsidy (25%) provided in Category A hill districts compared to plain districts (15%).

# Mizoram Youth Commission (MYC) Civil Services Coaching Scheme

## Summary
100% fully sponsored professional coaching and monthly living stipends of ₹10,000/month for Mizo youth preparing for UPSC Civil Services in New Delhi.

## Benefit
Full coaching fees (up to ₹2 Lakh) paid directly to empanelled institutes (Vajiram, Next IAS, Vision) + ₹10,000 monthly living stipend for up to 10 months in Delhi.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a graduate Mizo tribal youth who cleared the MYC civil services screening entrance examination.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 21–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Graduation Degree Certificate and Marksheets** (Mizoram University / Recognized University): Mandatory eligibility.
- **Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate of Mizoram** (Deputy Commissioner): Confirms indigenous tribal status.
- **MYC Screening Test Admit Card / Scorecard** (Mizoram Youth Commission): Merit proof.

## How to Apply
### MYC Mizoram Official Portal (online)
Register for entrance test at myc.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://myc.mizoram.gov.in

### MYC Office, New Capital Complex, Khatla, Aizawl (blockOffice)
Attend personal interview and institutional selection.

## Caveats
Selection is strictly based on performance in the MYC Common Screening Test and personal interview.

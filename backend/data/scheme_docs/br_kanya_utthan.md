# Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (Bihar)

## Summary
Cumulative financial support of up to ₹54,100 from birth to graduation: ₹2,000 at birth, ₹10,000 upon passing 10th, ₹25,000 upon passing 12th, and ₹50,000 upon graduation.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹25,000 for unmarried girls passing Intermediate (12th) and ₹50,000 for any girl passing Graduation from recognized Bihar universities.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female student residing in Bihar who has passed Intermediate (12th) or Graduation from a recognized board/college.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 16–30
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Graduation / 12th Final Marksheet and Degree Certificate** (University / BSEB): Mandatory for result verification.
- **Aadhaar Card with Bihar address** (UIDAI): Verifies identity and state domicile.
- **Student Bank Passbook (Branch must be in Bihar)** (Commercial / Grameen Bank): Direct payment transfer.

## How to Apply
### MedhaSoft / e-Kalyan Bihar Portal (online)
Apply online at medhasoft.bih.nic.in with university registration number.
Link: https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in

### College Examination & Verification Cell (panchayat)
University uploads verified result lists to MedhaSoft portal.

## Caveats
Girl must have an individual bank account in a bank branch located in Bihar.

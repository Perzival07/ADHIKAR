# Vhali Dikri Yojana (Gujarat)

## Summary
Cumulative financial assistance of ₹1,10,000 given in 3 stages: ₹4,000 upon Class 1 admission, ₹6,000 upon Class 9 admission, and ₹1,00,000 upon turning 18 unmarried.

## Benefit
Staged financial grants deposited in bank account, culminating in a ₹1 Lakh fixed deposit maturity grant when girl reaches 18 years for college or marriage.

## Eligibility
You qualify as the parent of a first or second girl child born in Gujarat with family income up to ₹2 Lakh.

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 0–18
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Girl Child's Official Birth Certificate** (Talati / Municipal Corp): Must prove birth in Gujarat.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹2 Lakh)** (Mamlatdar Office): Mandatory income limit proof.
- **Mother's and Father's Aadhaar Cards** (UIDAI): Parents identity verification.

## How to Apply
### CDPO (Child Development Project Officer) / Anganwadi Centre (blockOffice)
Submit Form 1 with child birth certificate to Anganwadi worker.

### Digital Gujarat Vhali Dikri Service (online)
Apply online via digitalgujarat.gov.in.
Link: https://wcd.gujarat.gov.in

## Caveats
Application must be submitted within 1 year of the girl child’s birth.

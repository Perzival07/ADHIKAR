# Beti Hai Anmol Yojana (Himachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Fixed deposit of ₹21,000 deposited in post office/bank upon birth of girl child in BPL families + annual school scholarship of ₹450 to ₹5,000 from Class 1 to 12.

## Benefit
₹21,000 post office deposit maturing at age 18 with interest + recurring annual school education stipends for up to two girl children per BPL family.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a girl child born in a registered BPL family residing in Himachal Pradesh.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 0–18
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Girl Child's Birth Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths HP): Mandatory birth proof.
- **BPL Certificate issued by Gram Panchayat / Urban Body** (Panchayat Secretary / Executive Officer): Validates economic status.
- **Himachal Bonafide Certificate of Parents** (Tehsildar): State residence proof.

## How to Apply
### Anganwadi Worker / Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) (panchayat)
Submit application within 1 year of girl child birth.

### e-District Himachal Portal (online)
Apply online via edistrict.hp.gov.in.
Link: https://edistrict.hp.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicable up to maximum two girl children per BPL household.

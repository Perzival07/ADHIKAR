# West Bengal Student Credit Card Scheme (WBSCC)

## Summary
Collateral-free soft education loan up to ₹10,00,000 at a nominal 4% simple interest rate with a 15-year repayment period and 100% state credit guarantee for students pursuing higher education across India and abroad.

## Benefit
Up to ₹10,00,000 collateral-free institutional loan covering course fees, hostel accommodation, books, and laptops with 15-year flexible repayment.

## Eligibility
You are a student residing in West Bengal for at least 10 years enrolled in Class 10/12/Higher Education/Coaching.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 16–40
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Higher Secondary / College / Postgraduate / Professional
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Course Admission Receipt & Fee Structure Brochure** (Recognized University / Institute): Details institutional tuition and hostel fees.
- **Proof of 10-Year Residence in West Bengal** (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Ration Card): Domicile requirement proof.
- **PAN Card of Student and Co-borrower (Parent)** (Income Tax Dept): Mandatory for bank loan underwriting.

## How to Apply
### Institution Nodal Officer / Bank Branch (blockOffice)
Institution verifies admission before forwarding to designated bank.

### WBSCC Official Web Portal (online)
Apply online at wbscc.wb.gov.in.
Link: https://wbscc.wb.gov.in

## Caveats
Loan repayment starts 1 year after completion of the course or upon getting employment.

# Chief Minister's Career Guidance & Scholarship Scheme (Manipur)

## Summary
100% fully funded professional coaching and ₹1 Lakh financial incentive for youth of Manipur preparing for UPSC Civil Services, NDA, CDS, and State PCS examinations.

## Benefit
Full sponsorship of tuition fees at premier national civil services coaching institutes in New Delhi + ₹10,000/month living stipend for up to 1 year.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a graduate youth from Manipur who cleared the state civil services preliminary screening test.

- Region: Manipur
- Age: 21–32
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Graduation Passing Certificate and Marksheet** (Recognized University): Mandatory eligibility.
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) of Manipur** (Deputy Commissioner): Confirms state domicile.
- **State Screening Test Admit Card / Scorecard** (Higher Education Dept): Merit proof.

## How to Apply
### Higher Education Manipur Portal (online)
Apply for screening exam at manipur.gov.in.
Link: https://manipur.gov.in

### Directorate of University & Higher Education, Imphal (blockOffice)
Document verification for selected scholars.

## Caveats
Selection is strictly based on the merit of the State Level Common Screening Entrance Examination.

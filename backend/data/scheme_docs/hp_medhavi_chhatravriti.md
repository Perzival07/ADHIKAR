# Himachal Pradesh Medhavi Chhatravriti Yojana

## Summary
Merit scholarship of ₹1,00,000 for top 100 meritorious students of Class 10th and top 100 students of Class 12th board examinations pursuing higher studies.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹1,00,000 credited directly to the student’s bank account to support collegiate, medical, engineering, or university education.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student who ranked among the top 100 toppers in HP Board of School Education (HPBOSE) exams.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 15–20
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **HPBOSE 10th or 12th Board Marksheet** (HP Board of School Education, Dharamshala): Proves top rank merit standing.
- **College / University Admission Fee Receipt** (Admitting College Principal): Proves continuation of studies.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### National Scholarship Portal (NSP) (online)
Apply online under Himachal Pradesh state schemes section.
Link: https://scholarships.gov.in

### College Nodal Officer Desk (panchayat)
Institutional verification on NSP portal.

## Caveats
Student must enroll in an undergraduate or diploma course immediately following board results.

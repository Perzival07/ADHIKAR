# Atma Nirbhar Bagwani Yojana (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Credit-linked 45% government subsidy for cultivation of high-value commercial fruits such as Kiwi, Apple, Orange, Walnut, and Dragon fruit.

## Benefit
45% capital subsidy for high-density fruit orchards, quality saplings, micro-irrigation systems, and barbed fencing.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landholding farmer or horticulture entrepreneur in Arunachal Pradesh.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Possession Certificate for Horticulture Plot** (Circle Officer): Verifies undisputed land possession.
- **APST Certificate and Aadhaar** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identification proof.

## How to Apply
### Atma Nirbhar Arunachal Portal (online)
Apply online under Bagwani section.
Link: https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in

### District Horticulture Officer (DHO) (blockOffice)
Coordinate field survey with Horticulture Development Officer.

## Caveats
Minimum 0.5 hectare suitable hill slope land required with perennial water source nearby.

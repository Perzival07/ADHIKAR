# Kunwarbai Nu Mameru Scheme (Gujarat)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹12,000 transferred into the bank account of the girl upon marriage for families belonging to SC, ST, SEBC, and EWS categories.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹12,000 paid to the bride to assist with wedding expenses and social dignity.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a bride belonging to SC/ST/SEBC/EWS residing in Gujarat with family income under ₹1.20 Lakh (rural) or ₹1.50 Lakh (urban).

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Marriage Registration Certificate** (Registrar of Marriages / Talati): Legal proof of marriage.
- **Caste / Community Certificate (SC/ST/SEBC)** (Mamlatdar Office): Category verification.
- **Bride's Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch in Gujarat): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### e-Samaj Kalyan Gujarat Portal (online)
Submit digital application at esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in.
Link: https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in

### District Social Welfare Officer / Taluka Development Office (panchayat)
Submit verification copy.

## Caveats
Application must be submitted within 2 years of the solemnization of marriage.

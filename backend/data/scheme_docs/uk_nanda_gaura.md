# Nanda Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana (Uttarakhand)

## Summary
Comprehensive financial incentive providing ₹11,000 at the birth of a girl child and ₹51,000 upon passing Class 12 and enrolling in higher education to eliminate child marriage and encourage girl education.

## Benefit
₹11,000 birth cash transfer + ₹51,000 higher education cash incentive credited directly to the girl’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You are a resident family of Uttarakhand with a newborn girl or a daughter passing Class 12 with annual income under ₹72,000.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 0–20
- Income ceiling: < 72,000
- Gender: Female
- Education level: Class 12 Pass / Newborn
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Within 6 months of birth or Class 12 result)

## Documents Required
- **Uttarakhand Domicile Certificate (Mool Niwas Praman Patra)** (Tahsildar / SDM): Proof of Uttarakhand domicile.
- **Income Certificate (< ₹72,000/year)** (Tahsildar via eDistrict): Income eligibility.
- **Class 12 Marksheet & College Admission Slip (for Stage 2)** (UBSE / CBSE / College): Academic progression proof.
- **Girl Child Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct DBT credit.

## How to Apply
### Local Anganwadi Centre / Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) (panchayat)
Submit application forms through Anganwadi Worker.

### Nanda Gaura Official Web Portal (online)
Apply online at nandagaura.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://nandagaura.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Application for 12th pass benefit must be submitted within 6 months of result declaration.

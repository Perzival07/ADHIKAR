# Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai Thittam (KMUT)

## Summary
Direct universal basic income assistance of ₹1,000 per month provided to 1.15+ crore female heads of families in Tamil Nadu recognizing their unpaid domestic labor.

## Benefit
₹1,000 per month credited directly into bank accounts via DBT on the 15th of every month.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female head of family in Tamil Nadu with annual household income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 21+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Smart Ration Card (Family Card)** (Civil Supplies Department): Proof of family headship and household status.
- **Aadhaar Card of Female Head** (UIDAI): Identity verification and biometric KYC.
- **Electricity Consumer Number / EB Bill** (TANGEDCO): Verifies annual power consumption within limit.

## How to Apply
### Special KMUT Camp at Ward / Village or e-Sevai Centre (csc)
Submit biometric application with Smart Ration Card and Aadhaar.

### KMUT Official Web Portal (online)
Track application and grievance status at kmut.tn.gov.in.
Link: https://kmut.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Family must consume less than 3,600 units of electricity per year and own less than 5 acres wetland or 10 acres dry land.

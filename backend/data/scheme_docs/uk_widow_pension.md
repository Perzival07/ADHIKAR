# Uttarakhand Destitute Widow Pension Scheme

## Summary
Monthly financial pension of ₹1,500 credited directly to widowed and destitute women aged 18 years and above residing in Uttarakhand.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,500 per month credited to the beneficiary’s bank account to support dignified living.

## Eligibility
You are a widowed woman residing in Uttarakhand with family income below poverty threshold.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 48,000 / year (or BPL)
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Death Certificate of Husband** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Proof of widowhood.
- **BPL Card / Income Certificate** (Tahsildar / Food Dept): Proof of income below ceiling.
- **Aadhaar Card of Widow** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### CSC Jan Seva Kendra / Block Social Welfare Office (csc)
Submit application form with husband’s death certificate.

### SSP Uttarakhand Portal (online)
Apply online at ssp.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://ssp.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must not be drawing other state pensions or remarried.

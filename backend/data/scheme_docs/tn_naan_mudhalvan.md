# Naan Mudhalvan Skill Development & Placement Platform

## Summary
State flagship dynamic skilling initiative delivering mandatory industry-certified training in Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, and Robotics across 2,000+ colleges.

## Benefit
Free access to industry-curated courses (Microsoft, IBM, Google, AWS), expert masterclasses, internships, and direct recruitment placement drives.

## Eligibility
You are a student enrolled in engineering, arts, science, polytechnic, or vocational colleges in Tamil Nadu.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 17–26
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: College / ITI / Polytechnic / University
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **College Student ID Card & Roll Number** (Affiliated College / University): Required for portal single sign-on.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### College Placement & Naan Mudhalvan Faculty Coordinator (blockOffice)
Participate in scheduled semester practicals and hackathons.

### Naan Mudhalvan Portal (online)
Register and take certification tests at naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in.
Link: https://naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Students log in using their institutional university roll / registration number.

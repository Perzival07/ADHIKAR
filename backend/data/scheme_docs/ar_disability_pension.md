# Arunachal Pradesh Disability Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct social pension of ₹1,500 per month and free assistive mobility aids for persons with benchmark physical or intellectual disabilities (40%+).

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,500 directly into the disabled beneficiary’s bank account + free tricycles, hearing aids, and calipers.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a person residing in Arunachal Pradesh holding a valid disability certificate showing 40%+ benchmark disability.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 5+
- Income ceiling: < 1.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Disability Certificate (40%+) / UDID Card** (District Medical Board): Mandatory proof of benchmark disability.
- **APST / Domicile Certificate** (DC Office): Confirms state residency.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) / SJETA Desk (blockOffice)
Submit application with medical board certificate.

### Arunachal SJETA Portal (online)
Track pension sanction status.
Link: https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in

## Caveats
UDID (Unique Disability ID) card registration is strongly encouraged for automatic renewal.

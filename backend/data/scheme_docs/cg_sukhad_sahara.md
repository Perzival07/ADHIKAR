# Chhattisgarh Sukhad Sahara Yojana

## Summary
Direct social pension of ₹350 per month for destitute widowed women aged 18 to 39 years living below the poverty line.

## Benefit
Monthly financial pension of ₹350 directly credited to the widow’s bank account until she reaches 40 years (when she transitions to standard widow pension).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a widowed woman aged 18 to 39 years residing in Chhattisgarh belonging to a BPL household.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 18–39
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Husband's Death Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Mandatory proof of widowhood.
- **BPL Survey 2002 / Priority Ration Card** (Food Dept): Confirms below poverty line status.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat / Janpad Panchayat Office (panchayat)
Submit application to Janpad Panchayat Social Welfare desk.

### Social Welfare CG Portal (online)
Track pension sanction.
Link: https://sw.cg.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must not be in receipt of any other pension or family maintenance from government service.

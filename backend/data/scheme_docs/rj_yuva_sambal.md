# Mukhyamantri Yuva Sambal Yojana (Unemployment Allowance Rajasthan)

## Summary
Monthly unemployment allowance of ₹4,000 for male youth, and ₹4,500 for female, transgender, and disabled youth for up to 2 years with 4 hours/day government internship.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹4,000 to ₹4,500 per month credited directly to the youth’s account for up to 24 months upon completing 4 hours of daily skill internship.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed graduate residing in Rajasthan aged 18 to 30 (up to 35 for female/SC/ST) with family income under ₹2 Lakh.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Graduation Degree and Final Marksheet from Rajasthan university** (Recognized University in Rajasthan): Mandatory graduation qualification.
- **Jan Aadhaar Card** (Jan Aadhaar Authority): Matches state citizen database.
- **Family Income Certificate under ₹2 Lakh (Format K & I)** (Executive Magistrate / Tehsildar): Income criteria validation.

## How to Apply
### Department of Employment Rajasthan Portal (online)
Apply online via employment.livelihoods.rajasthan.gov.in through SSO ID.
Link: https://employment.livelihoods.rajasthan.gov.in

### District Employment Office (DEO) Rajasthan (blockOffice)
Verify internship reporting order.

## Caveats
Candidate must undergo 3 months of skill training and 4 hours of daily departmental internship.

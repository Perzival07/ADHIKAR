# Snehapoorvam Scheme (Kerala)

## Summary
Monthly financial scholarship of ₹300 to ₹1,000 per month for orphan children living with extended families to continue school and collegiate education.

## Benefit
Monthly allowance credited directly via DBT: ₹300/mo (Classes 1-5), ₹500/mo (Classes 6-10), ₹750/mo (Classes 11-12), and ₹1,000/mo (Degree/Professional courses).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a child who has lost one or both parents living with surviving parent/guardian with family income under ₹20,000 (rural) or ₹22,375 (urban).

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 5–24
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parent's Death Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Proves orphan/single-parent status.
- **Income Certificate** (Village Officer): Economic eligibility validation.
- **Student's Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch in Kerala): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Headmaster / Principal of School or College (panchayat)
School principal submits verified roll online to KSSM.

### KSSM Snehapoorvam Portal (online)
Track application and annual renewal at kssm.kerala.gov.in.
Link: https://socialjustice.kerala.gov.in

## Caveats
Application must be forwarded through the head of the educational institution where child is studying.

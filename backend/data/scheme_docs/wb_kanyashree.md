# Kanyashree Prakalpa (K1 & K2 Schemes)

## Summary
Internationally recognized conditional cash transfer providing ₹1,000 annual scholarship (K1) for unmarried girls aged 13-18 enrolled in Classes 8-12, and a one-time grant of ₹25,000 (K2) upon reaching 18 years while continuing education.

## Benefit
K1: ₹1,000 per year during school + K2: ₹25,000 one-time financial grant deposited directly into girl’s own bank account.

## Eligibility
You are an unmarried girl student aged 13 to 19 years studying in a recognized West Bengal school or college.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 13–19
- Income ceiling: No Limit (for girls with disability or institutional care)
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School (Class 8-12) / College
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Annual Academic Cycle)

## Documents Required
- **Kanyashree Application Form (Form K1 / K2)** (School Headmaster): Signed and verified by institution head.
- **Unmarried Declaration Certificate** (Guardian / Headmaster): Mandatory condition for eligibility.
- **Girl Child’s Own Savings Bank Account Passbook** (Bank): Account must be exclusively in girl’s name.

## How to Apply
### Recognized School / Madrasah / College Headmaster Desk (blockOffice)
Applications processed directly by the school nodal teacher on Kanyashree portal.

### Kanyashree Prakalpa Portal (online)
Track application status at wbkanyashree.gov.in.
Link: https://www.wbkanyashree.gov.in

## Caveats
Girl must remain unmarried until 18 years of age and continue institutional education.

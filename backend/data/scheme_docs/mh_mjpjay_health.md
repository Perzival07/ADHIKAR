# Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY Maharashtra)

## Summary
Universal cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalization cover up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year across 1,356 medical procedures in empanelled hospitals for all citizens of Maharashtra.

## Benefit
100% cashless medical care up to ₹5 Lakhs per family per year covering surgeries, oncology, kidney dialysis, neurosurgery, burns, and post-hospitalization medicines.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Maharashtra holding an active ration card (Yellow, Orange, or White) or domicile certificate.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Ration Card of Maharashtra (Yellow / Orange / White)** (Food & Civil Supplies Dept): Primary identification for admission.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for hospital e-KYC.

## How to Apply
### Arogyamitra Helpdesk at Empanelled Network Hospital (csc)
Show ration card and Aadhaar to Arogyamitra for instant admission approval.

### MJPJAY Jeevandayee Portal (online)
Search empanelled hospitals and package rates at jeevandayee.gov.in.
Link: https://www.jeevandayee.gov.in

## Caveats
Benefit expanded to cover White Ration Card holders, making health cover 100% universal across Maharashtra.

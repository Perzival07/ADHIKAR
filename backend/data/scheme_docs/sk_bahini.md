# Bahini Scheme (Free Sanitary Napkins for School Girls)

## Summary
Free vending machine-based distribution of 100% biodegradable sanitary napkins to female students from Classes 9 to 12 across all government secondary and senior secondary schools.

## Benefit
Free monthly supply of hygienic sanitary pads and incinerator facilities at all government schools to prevent female student dropouts.

## Eligibility
You are a female student enrolled in Class 9 through 12 in any Sikkim government school.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 13–19
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School (Class 9-12)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School Identity Card** (Govt Secondary School): Verifies student enrollment.

## How to Apply
### School Bahini Nodal Teacher / Vending Machine Kiosk (blockOffice)
Receive token/access to automated dispensing units inside school.

### Sikkim Education Portal (online)
Access program details at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Direct institutional distribution at school premises.

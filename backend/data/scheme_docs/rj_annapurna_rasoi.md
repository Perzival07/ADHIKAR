# Shri Annapurna Rasoi Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
Nutritious, hygienic, hot cooked wholesome full thali meals (chapati, dal, seasonal sabzi, millets/khichdi) provided at a nominal price of only ₹8 per plate across all urban towns.

## Benefit
600-gram wholesome hot cooked meal (300g chapati, 100g dal, 100g vegetable sabzi, 100g khichdi/rice and pickle) served daily across 1,000+ permanent air-conditioned dining centers.

## Eligibility
You qualify as any citizen visiting Shri Annapurna Rasoi centers in any municipality across Rajasthan.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Token at Food Counter (₹8 cash / digital QR payment)** (Shri Annapurna Rasoi Counter): Pay ₹8 directly at dining hall.

## How to Apply
### Any Shri Annapurna Rasoi Centre (near Railway Station, Bus Stand, Hospital) (blockOffice)
Walk into dining hall and purchase meal token for ₹8.
Link: https://food.rajasthan.gov.in

### Local Self Government Portal Rajasthan (online)
Locate nearest Annapurna Rasoi center on map.
Link: https://food.rajasthan.gov.in

## Caveats
No ration card or economic criteria required; universal access for all students, labourers, and citizens.

# Krishak Unnati Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
Agricultural input assistance ensuring farmers receive ₹3,100 per quintal for paddy procurement, with direct difference payout of up to ₹917 per quintal via DBT.

## Benefit
Difference amount between MSP and ₹3,100/quintal credited directly to farmer bank accounts for up to 21 quintals per acre of paddy procured.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a registered paddy cultivator in Chhattisgarh who sold paddy at Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS) mandis.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Tax Exempt)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Integrated Farmer Registration Slip (Kisan Code)** (PACS / Agriculture Dept): Validates paddy cultivation area.
- **B-1 Khasra Land Revenue Record** (Tehsildar / Bhuiyan CG): Proves agricultural land holding.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (District Cooperative Central Bank / Commercial Bank): Direct payment of differential amount.

## How to Apply
### Primary Agricultural Credit Cooperative Society (PACS) Mandi (panchayat)
Sell paddy and verify weighment slip.
Link: https://kisan.cg.nic.in

### Bhuiyan / CG Kisan Portal (online)
Verify land record integration and payment status.
Link: https://kisan.cg.nic.in

## Caveats
Farmer must be registered on the Integrated Kisan Portal before the procurement season commences.

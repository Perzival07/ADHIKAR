# Mukh Mantri Kheti Badi Yantrikaran Yojna (Crop Residue Machinery Punjab)

## Summary
50% individual farmer subsidy and 80% Custom Hiring Centre (CHC) subsidy on crop residue management machines (Super Seeder, Smart Seeder, Happy Seeder, Baler).

## Benefit
50% subsidy for individual farmers and 80% subsidy for cooperative societies and farmer groups to eliminate stubble burning and improve soil fertility.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landholding farmer or registered cooperative society in Punjab cultivating paddy.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jamabandi / Fard Land Record in Punjab** (Revenue Dept / Fard Kendra): Proves agricultural land holdings.
- **Tractor Registration Certificate (RC)** (RTO Punjab): Confirms ownership of tractor to operate machinery.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT of subsidy.

## How to Apply
### Punjab Agri Machinery Portal (online)
Submit application and select machine at agrimachinerypb.com.
Link: https://agrimachinerypb.com

### Chief Agriculture Officer (CAO) / Block Agriculture Officer (blockOffice)
Physical inspection of machine and invoice submission.

## Caveats
Machinery must be purchased from empanelled and approved manufacturers through transparent portal lottery.

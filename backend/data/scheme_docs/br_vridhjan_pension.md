# Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Pension Yojana (MVPY Bihar)

## Summary
Universal monthly old age pension of ₹400 per month (aged 60 to 79) and ₹500 per month (aged 80+) for all senior citizens of Bihar not receiving other pensions.

## Benefit
Direct benefit transfer of ₹400 to ₹500 per month credited directly to the senior citizen’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account for life.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 60 or above residing in Bihar not drawing any other government or retired pension.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Certificate (Voter ID / Aadhaar with exact DOB)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Must confirm age is 60 or older.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook with IFSC** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly pension credit.
- **Aadhaar Consent and Self-Declaration** (Self): Declares no other regular pension is drawn.

## How to Apply
### SSPMIS Bihar Social Security Portal (online)
Apply online directly at sspmis.bihar.gov.in or RTPS service.
Link: https://sspmis.bihar.gov.in

### Block Development Office (BDO) / RTPS Counter (panchayat)
Submit application with biometrics at RTPS desk.

## Caveats
Retired government or PSU employees drawing service pensions are excluded.

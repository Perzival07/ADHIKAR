# Mukhyamantri Vriksh Sampada Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
100% subsidy for commercial plantation on up to 5 acres, and 50% subsidy for more than 5 acres of private land for planting clonal Eucalyptus, Teak, and Sandalwood.

## Benefit
Free high-yield clonal saplings, fencing subsidy, technical maintenance, and assured minimum buyback price by government forest federation.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landowner in Chhattisgarh with at least 0.5 acre to 5+ acres of private cultivable land.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Kisan Rin Pustika / B-1 Khasra Record** (Revenue Dept / Patwari): Proves clear land title ownership.
- **Aadhaar Card and Bank Account** (UIDAI / Bank): Owner identification and DBT.

## How to Apply
### Range Forest Officer (RFO) / Forest Guard Office (blockOffice)
Submit consent form and land details to local RFO.

### CG Forest Department Portal (online)
Track sapling request and timber buyback agreements.
Link: https://forest.cg.gov.in

## Caveats
Minimum survival rate of 80% saplings required to claim year-2 and year-3 maintenance subsidies.

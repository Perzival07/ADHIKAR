# Swanirbhar Naari Scheme (Assam)

## Summary
Direct state procurement of 31 traditional hand-woven indigenous handloom items directly from women weavers at benchmark guaranteed prices with zero middlemen.

## Benefit
Direct bank payment within 7 days of selling traditional Gamosa, Dokhona, Eri silk shawls, and garments at ARTFED/AGMC procurement centers.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous woman handloom weaver registered on the Swanirbhar Naari portal in Assam.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Swanirbhar Naari Registration QR Code / Card** (Handloom Dept Assam): Generated online on portal.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Direct sale proceeds credit.

## How to Apply
### Swanirbhar Naari Procurement Portal (online)
Register loom and book procurement center slot.
Link: https://swanirbharnaari.assam.gov.in

### ARTFED / AGMC Procurement Centre (blockOffice)
Deposit hand-woven textiles for quality tag and payment.

## Caveats
Only traditional hand-woven items on pit-looms or frame-looms without synthetic powerloom adulteration are procured.

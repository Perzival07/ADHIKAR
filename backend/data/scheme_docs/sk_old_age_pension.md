# Sikkim Social Justice Old Age Pension Scheme

## Summary
Monthly financial pension of ₹2,000 provided to senior citizens aged 60 years and above who are permanent residents of Sikkim.

## Benefit
Monthly direct pension of ₹2,000 credited on the 1st of every month directly to the senior citizen’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You are a resident senior citizen of Sikkim aged 60 years or older without other pension support.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Certificate of Identification (COI)** (SDM Office): Proof of domicile.
- **Aadhaar Card / Voter Card showing Age 60+** (UIDAI / ECI): Confirms eligibility age.
- **Individual Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct pension remittance.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat Unit (GPU) / Social Welfare Inspector at Block Office (panchayat)
Submit physical application with age and COI proof.

### Sikkim Social Welfare Portal (online)
View pension beneficiary list at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must submit annual life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan).

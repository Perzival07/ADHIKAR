# National Social Assistance Programme (IGNOAPS - Old Age Pension)

## Summary
Non-contributory monthly social pension for senior citizens aged 60 and above living below the poverty line, supplemented by state contributions.

## Benefit
Monthly direct pension of ₹1,000 to ₹3,000 (Central component + State supplementary contribution) credited directly to bank/post office account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 60 or older belonging to a recognized BPL household.

- Region: All India
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Birth Certificate)** (UIDAI / Election Commission): Must confirm age is 60 or above.
- **BPL Card / Antyodaya Ration Card** (Food Dept): Confirms household is below poverty line.
- **Bank / Post Office Passbook with Aadhaar linkage** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### NSAP National Portal (online)
Submit application and track sanction status at nsap.nic.in.
Link: https://nsap.nic.in

### Gram Panchayat / Block Development Office (BDO) (panchayat)
Submit physical application through Gram Sevak / Social Welfare desk.

## Caveats
Pension amount increases significantly upon reaching 80 years of age.

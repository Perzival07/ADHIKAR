# Prakritik Kheti Khushhal Kisan Yojana (PK3Y Himachal)

## Summary
Complete assistance including ₹25,000 subsidy for purchasing an indigenous desi cow, free drums for Jivamrit preparation, and certification for chemical-free natural farming.

## Benefit
50% subsidy up to ₹25,000 for purchasing indigenous desi cow + 80% subsidy on plastic drums + dedicated farmer training in Subhash Palekar Natural Farming (SPNF).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing farmer in Himachal Pradesh adopting non-chemical natural farming methods.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Himachal Bonafide Certificate / Land Record** (Tehsildar): Proves agricultural holdings in HP.
- **SPNF Natural Farming Training Completion Certificate** (Block Technology Manager (BTM) ATMA): Prerequisite for cow grant.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT of subsidy.

## How to Apply
### Block Technology Manager (BTM) / Agriculture Development Officer (blockOffice)
Register with ATMA at block agriculture office.

### SPNF Himachal Portal (online)
Register for natural farming training and cow subsidy.
Link: https://spnfhimachal.in

## Caveats
Farmer must attend the mandatory 2-day hands-on natural farming workshop before claiming cow purchase subsidy.

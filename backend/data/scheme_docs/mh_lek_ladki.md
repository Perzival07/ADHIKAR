# Lek Ladki Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
Cumulative financial assistance totaling ₹1,01,000 for girl children born in Yellow/Orange ration card families: ₹5k at birth, ₹6k in Class 1, ₹7k in Class 6, ₹8k in Class 11, and ₹75k at age 18.

## Benefit
Direct cash transfers into the girl child’s account at educational milestones + lump sum maturity grant of ₹75,000 when girl attains 18 years of age and remains unmarried.

## Eligibility
You qualify as the parent of a girl child born in Maharashtra in a household holding a Yellow or Orange ration card.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 0–18
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Girl Child's Birth Certificate** (Gram Panchayat / Municipal Corp): Mandatory birth proof in Maharashtra.
- **Yellow or Orange Ration Card** (Food & Civil Supplies Dept): Validates economic priority.
- **Mother's and Father's Aadhaar Cards** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Anganwadi Sevika / Gram Panchayat Office (panchayat)
Submit application within 1 year of girl child birth.

### Maharashtra WCD Portal (online)
Track application status at womenchild.maharashtra.gov.in.
Link: https://womenchild.maharashtra.gov.in

## Caveats
Girl must remain unmarried until 18 years of age to claim the final ₹75,000 grant.

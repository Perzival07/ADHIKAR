# Chirayu Haryana Scheme (Expanded Ayushman Bharat)

## Summary
Cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalization cover of ₹5 Lakh per year for all families having annual income up to ₹3 Lakh (expanded to ₹1.80L - ₹3L via nominal ₹1,500 contribution).

## Benefit
100% cashless hospitalization, critical surgeries, and post-discharge medicines up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year across 1,000+ impanelled hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a Haryana family verified in PPP with annual income under ₹3 Lakh.

- Region: Haryana
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Mandatory for eligibility validation.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Biometric e-KYC.

## How to Apply
### Chirayu Haryana Portal (online)
Register and download Golden Card at chirayu.haryana.gov.in.
Link: https://chirayu.haryana.gov.in

### Atal Seva Kendra / Hospital Ayushman Mitra Kiosk (csc)
Direct biometric verification and card printing.

## Caveats
Families with income between ₹1.80 Lakh and ₹3 Lakh pay nominal annual contribution of ₹1,500.

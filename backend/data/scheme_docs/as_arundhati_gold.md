# Arundhati Gold Scheme (Assam)

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹40,000 transferred to the bride’s bank account to purchase 1 tola (10 grams) of gold upon formal legal registration of marriage.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹40,000 to bride’s bank account to incentivize formal marriage registration under Special Marriage Act.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an adult bride in Assam legally registering marriage under Special Marriage Act with family income below ₹5 Lakh.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Marriage Registration Certificate under Special Marriage Act** (Marriage Registrar / Sub-Registrar): Statutory legal registration proof.
- **Family Income Certificate under ₹5 Lakh** (Circle Officer): Proves economic eligibility.
- **HSLC / 10th Pass Certificate** (SEBA / Recognized Board): Minimum educational qualification.

## How to Apply
### Arundhati Online Registration Portal (online)
Apply online via revenueassam.nic.in/arundhati.
Link: https://revenueassam.nic.in

### Office of Senior Sub-Registrar / Marriage Officer (blockOffice)
Submit physical documents during marriage solemnization.

## Caveats
Bride must have passed minimum Class 10 (relaxed for Tea Tribe and Adivasi communities).

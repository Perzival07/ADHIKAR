# Mukhyamantri Vayoshri Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
One-time direct financial grant of ₹3,000 credited to the bank accounts of senior citizens aged 65 and above to purchase assistive living devices (spectacles, hearing aids, walkers).

## Benefit
₹3,000 one-time direct benefit transfer credited into the senior citizen’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account for purchasing mobility aids and health assistive equipment.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 65 or above residing in Maharashtra with annual family income below ₹2 Lakh.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 65+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Certificate showing 65+ (Aadhaar / Voter ID)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Mandatory age proof.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹2 Lakh)** (Tahsildar / Setu Kendra): Validates economic eligibility.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for ₹3,000 direct credit.

## How to Apply
### MahaDBT Vayoshri Portal (online)
Apply online at mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.
Link: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

### Assistant Commissioner, Social Welfare / Tahsildar Office (blockOffice)
Submit hard copies at social welfare desk.

## Caveats
Applicant must submit a self-undertaking confirming purchase of assistive device/spectacles with the grant.

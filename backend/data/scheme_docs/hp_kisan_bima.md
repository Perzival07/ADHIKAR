# Mukhya Mantri Kisaan Evam Khetihar Mazdoor Bima Yojana (Himachal)

## Summary
Accidental death and permanent disability insurance compensation of up to ₹3 Lakh for farmers and farm labourers injured while operating agricultural machinery or farm activities.

## Benefit
Ex-gratia compensation of ₹3 Lakh for death, ₹2 Lakh for permanent total disability (loss of both limbs/eyes), and ₹1 Lakh for partial disability resulting from farm machinery accidents.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a farmer, tenant, or farm labourer in Himachal Pradesh who suffered an accident during agricultural operations.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 14–70
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Police Daily Diary (Rapat) & Medical Post-Mortem / Disability Certificate** (Police Station / Medical Officer): Proves agricultural machinery accident.
- **Gram Panchayat Pradhan Certificate verifying farm operation** (Gram Panchayat Pradhan): Verifies accident took place during farm work.
- **Bank Account of Victim or Legal Heir** (Bank Branch): Required for compensation transfer.

## How to Apply
### Subject Matter Specialist (SMS Agriculture) / Agriculture Development Officer (blockOffice)
Submit claim form with medical records.

### Himachal Agriculture Portal (online)
Track insurance compensation claim status.
Link: https://hpagriculture.com

## Caveats
Claim must be lodged with the Subject Matter Specialist (SMS Agriculture) within 6 months of the accident.

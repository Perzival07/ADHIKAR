# Illam Thedi Kalvi (Education at Doorstep)

## Summary
Community volunteer-led evening remedial learning centers operating in habitations across Tamil Nadu for students in Classes 1 to 8 to bridge learning gaps and build fundamental reading and math skills.

## Benefit
Free 1.5 to 2 hours of daily evening personalized learning, reading kits, and activity-based tutoring right in the student’s neighborhood.

## Eligibility
You are a school student in Classes 1 to 8 residing in any urban or rural habitation in Tamil Nadu.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 6–14
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School (Class 1-8)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Student EMIS Number** (School Headmaster): Enables progress tracking on state portal.

## How to Apply
### Village Volunteer Center / Community Hall (panchayat)
Attend daily evening session at neighborhood learning center.

### Illam Thedi Kalvi Portal (online)
Volunteer and center info at illamthedikalvi.tnschools.gov.in.
Link: https://illamthedikalvi.tnschools.gov.in

## Caveats
Sessions delivered by trained local community volunteers.

# Jagananna Vasathi Deevena (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Annual financial assistance of ₹10,000 for ITI, ₹15,000 for Polytechnic, and ₹20,000 for Degree/Engineering students towards boarding and hostel food expenses.

## Benefit
Direct cash allowance of ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 per year transferred in two equal installments to the mother’s bank account for student living expenses.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a higher education student residing away from home enrolled in recognized courses in AP.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 16–30
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **College Bonafide and Hostel Certificate** (College Principal / Warden): Proves active course enrollment.
- **Mother's Aadhaar seeded Bank Account** (UIDAI / Bank): Direct credit recipient.
- **Rice Card / Income Certificate** (Civil Supplies Dept): Confirms eligibility.

## How to Apply
### JnanaBhumi Vasathi Deevena Portal (online)
Submit application online through college coordinator.
Link: https://jaganannavasathideevena.ap.gov.in

### Village / Ward Secretariat Social Welfare Desk (panchayat)
Biometric authentication for installment clearance.

## Caveats
Distance education and private deemed university management quota admissions are excluded.

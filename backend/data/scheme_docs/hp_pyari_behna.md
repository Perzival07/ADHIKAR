# Indira Gandhi Pyari Behna Sukh Samman Nidhi (Himachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹1,500 per month (₹18,000 per year) directly credited into the bank accounts of all eligible adult women aged 18 to 59 in Himachal Pradesh.

## Benefit
₹1,500 per month DBT credited directly to the woman’s personal savings account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an adult female permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh aged 18 to 59.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 18–59
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Himachal Bonafide / Domicile Certificate** (Tehsildar / Executive Magistrate): Mandatory permanent resident proof.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Himachal Pradesh** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit of ₹1,500.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat Secretary / Tehsil Welfare Officer (TWO) (panchayat)
Submit physical application form to TWO.

### e-District Himachal Portal (online)
Apply online via edistrict.hp.gov.in.
Link: https://edistrict.hp.gov.in

## Caveats
Women or families having regular government employees, pensioners, or income tax payees are excluded.

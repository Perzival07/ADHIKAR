# Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana (Enhanced ₹25,000)

## Summary
Enhanced conditional cash transfer of ₹25,000 delivered across 6 critical life stages from birth, vaccinations, Class 1, 6, 9 admissions, to undergraduate degree enrollment.

## Benefit
₹25,000 total DBT released in 6 tranches directly into the girl child or mother bank account to promote girl child education and nutrition.

## Eligibility
You are a resident family of Uttar Pradesh with a daughter and annual household income up to ₹3,00,000.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 0–25
- Income ceiling: < 3.0 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: All Stages (Birth to Degree)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Stage-specific timeframes)

## Documents Required
- **Birth Certificate of Girl Child** (Registrar of Births): Proof of birth in UP.
- **Domicile / Niwas Praman Patra of Parents** (Tahsildar / eDistrict): Proof of UP domicile.
- **Income Certificate (< ₹3 Lakh)** (Tahsildar / eDistrict): Proof of family income ceiling.
- **Bank Passbook of Mother / Girl** (Bank): For direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### CSC Jan Seva Kendra / Ward Office (csc)
Upload application and stage documents online.

### MKSY Official Web Portal (online)
Apply directly online at mksy.up.gov.in.
Link: https://mksy.up.gov.in

## Caveats
Maximum two daughters per family eligible (exceptions for twins).

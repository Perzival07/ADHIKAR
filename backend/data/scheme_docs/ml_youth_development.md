# Chief Minister's Youth Development Scheme (Meghalaya)

## Summary
Direct grants of up to ₹1 Lakh for youth clubs, sports associations, and cultural groups for grassroots sports equipment, musical gears, and community initiatives.

## Benefit
Direct financial grants from ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 per registered youth organization to nurture sports talent, football coaching, traditional music, and fitness.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an office bearer of a registered youth club or community sports association in Meghalaya.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Youth Club Registration Certificate / Headman Recommendation** (Registrar of Societies / Rangbah Shnong): Proves active community youth club.
- **Bank Account in the Name of Youth Club** (Bank Branch in Meghalaya): Required for grant transfer.

## How to Apply
### District Sports Officer (DSO) / Sports & Youth Affairs Directorate (blockOffice)
Submit proposal for sports and musical equipment.

### Meghalaya State Portal (online)
View scheme circulars at meghalaya.gov.in.
Link: https://meghalaya.gov.in

## Caveats
Club must be registered under Societies Registration Act or recognized by the local Rangbah Shnong / Syiem.

# Punjab Financial Assistance to Widows and Destitute Women

## Summary
Direct monthly social welfare pension of ₹1,500 per month directly credited to the bank accounts of widowed, deserted, and destitute women residing in Punjab.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,500 directly credited into the widow’s bank account to support basic living needs and family maintenance.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a widowed, unmarried, or deserted woman residing in Punjab with annual family income under ₹60,000.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Husband's Death Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Mandatory proof of widowhood.
- **Income Certificate under ₹60,000** (Tehsildar / Sewa Kendra): Validates economic need.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Punjab** (Bank Branch in Punjab): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Sewa Kendra Punjab / CDPO Office (csc)
Submit application with husband death certificate.

### e-Sewa Punjab Portal (online)
Apply online under Social Security services at connect.punjab.gov.in.
Link: https://socialsecurity.punjab.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must not have remarried and must not be in receipt of any other regular government family pension.

# Ladli Social Security Allowance (Haryana)

## Summary
Direct monthly financial pension of ₹3,000 per month paid to parents who have only daughters upon the father or mother turning 45 years of age.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹3,000 credited to the mother’s (or surviving father’s) bank account until they attain 60 years of age.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a parent residing in Haryana for 10+ years aged 45 to 60 who has only daughters and no living biological sons.

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 45–60
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Validates only daughters in family tree.
- **Birth Certificates of all Daughters** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Confirms number of girl children.
- **Mother's Aadhaar-linked Bank Account** (Bank Branch in Haryana): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Antyodaya Saral Portal (Ladli Pension) (online)
Apply online via saralharyana.gov.in.
Link: https://saralharyana.gov.in

### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) (blockOffice)
Submit physical verification.

## Caveats
Family annual income from all sources must not exceed ₹3 Lakh. Beneficiaries transition to Old Age Samman Bhatta at age 60.

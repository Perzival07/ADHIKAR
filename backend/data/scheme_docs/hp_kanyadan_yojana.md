# Mukhya Mantri Kanyadan Yojana (Himachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct marriage financial grant of ₹51,000 for the marriage of daughters of destitute persons, widows, and disabled parents living below poverty line.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹51,000 transferred into the girl or parent’s bank account to support dignified wedding ceremonies.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an adult bride or guardian residing in Himachal Pradesh with annual family income below ₹50,000 (or destitute/widowed parent).

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Income Certificate showing family income under ₹50,000** (Tehsildar / Naib Tehsildar): Mandatory poverty proof.
- **Himachal Bonafide Certificate** (Tehsildar): Domicile proof.
- **Marriage Card and Age Proof (18+)** (Family / School Certificate): Verifies wedding date and legal age.

## How to Apply
### e-District Himachal / e-Sakhsham Portal (online)
Apply online at edistrict.hp.gov.in.
Link: https://esakhsham.hp.gov.in

### Tehsil Welfare Officer (TWO) Office (blockOffice)
Submit application with marriage invitation.

## Caveats
Bride must be at least 18 years and groom at least 21 years of age.

# Puducherry Financial Assistance to Poor Widows and Destitute Women

## Summary
Direct monthly social welfare pension of ₹1,500 to ₹2,000 per month for widows, destitute women, and deserted wives residing in Puducherry.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹1,500 (age under 60) and ₹2,000 (age 60+) per month + free monthly food grains entitlement.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a widowed, deserted, or destitute woman residing in Puducherry with family income under ₹1 Lakh.

- Region: Puducherry
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **5-Year Puducherry Residence Certificate** (Taluk Office / Tahsildar): Mandatory 5-year stay proof.
- **Husband's Death Certificate** (Municipality / Commune Panchayat): Required for widow pension.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Puducherry Government Portal (online)
Apply online via socwelfare.py.gov.in.
Link: https://socwelfare.py.gov.in

### Taluk Office / Social Welfare Department Puducherry (blockOffice)
Submit application to Welfare Officer.

## Caveats
Applicant must be a permanent resident of Puducherry for at least 5 years.

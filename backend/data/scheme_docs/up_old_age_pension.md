# Uttar Pradesh Mukhyamantri Vridhavastha Pension Yojana

## Summary
Monthly financial pension of ₹1,000 (quarterly payment of ₹3,000) credited directly to elderly citizens aged 60 years and above living below the poverty line.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,000 per month (disbursed as ₹3,000 every quarter) directly to the pensioner’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You are a resident of UP aged 60+ with annual family income under ₹46,080 (rural) or ₹56,460 (urban).

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 46,080 (Rural) / < 56,460 (Urban)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity and age verification.
- **Income Certificate** (Tahsildar via eDistrict): Proof of income below ceiling.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): Mandatory for direct pension deposit.

## How to Apply
### CSC Jan Seva Kendra / Gram Panchayat Sachiv (csc)
Fill application form and complete biometric verification.

### Integrated Pension Portal (SSPY UP) (online)
Apply and track status at sspy-up.gov.in.
Link: https://sspy-up.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must complete annual biometric/facial e-KYC on the SSPY portal.

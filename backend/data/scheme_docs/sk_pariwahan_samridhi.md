# Chief Minister’s Pariwahan Samridhi Yojana

## Summary
Financial subsidy of up to ₹3,00,000 provided to unemployed local youth possessing valid commercial driving licenses to purchase taxis, passenger utility vehicles, and light trucks.

## Benefit
Direct subsidy of ₹3 Lakh credited to the vehicle financing loan account to lower monthly EMI for youth taxi operators.

## Eligibility
You are an unemployed resident youth of Sikkim holding a valid commercial transport driving license.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 21–45
- Income ceiling: < 3.0 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Commercial Driving License** (RTO Sikkim): Mandatory driving credentials.
- **Certificate of Identification (COI)** (SDM Office): Proof of Sikkim residence.
- **Vehicle Proforma Invoice & Bank In-Principle Letter** (Authorized Auto Dealer / Bank): Vehicle cost details.

## How to Apply
### Regional Transport Office (RTO) / Motor Vehicles Secretariat Gangtok (blockOffice)
Submit application with DL and dealer proforma invoice.

### Sikkim Transport Portal (online)
Download guidelines at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must not possess another commercial transport permit in their own name.

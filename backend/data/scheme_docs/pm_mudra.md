# Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

## Summary
Collateral-free business loans up to ₹20 Lakhs for micro and small enterprises across Shishu (up to ₹50k), Kishore (₹50k-₹5L), Tarun (₹5L-₹10L), and Tarun Plus (₹10L-₹20L) categories.

## Benefit
Loans without collateral security up to ₹20 Lakh at competitive commercial bank interest rates with affordable repayment tenure up to 5-7 years.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an entrepreneur or micro-business owner setting up or expanding a non-farm manufacturing, trading, or service enterprise.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card and PAN Card** (UIDAI / IT Dept): Identity and tax identification.
- **Business Registration / Udyam Certificate / Trade License** (MSME Dept / Municipal Corp): Proves enterprise existence or proposed location.
- **Last 6 Months Bank Statement** (Bank Branch): Demonstrates financial track record.

## How to Apply
### Udyamimitra Portal (online)
Submit digital loan application at udyamimitra.in to connect with 100+ lenders.
Link: https://www.udyamimitra.in

### Any Public Sector / Commercial Bank / NBFC Branch (bankBranch)
Submit standard PMMY application form with business proposal.
Link: https://www.mudra.org.in

## Caveats
Enhanced ceiling of ₹20 Lakh applies to entrepreneurs who have successfully repaid earlier Tarun loans.

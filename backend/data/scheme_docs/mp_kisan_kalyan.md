# Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year from MP government in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000, providing total ₹12,000 per year when combined with PM-KISAN.

## Benefit
₹6,000 per year credited directly to the farmer’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account in 3 installments through SAARA portal.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landholding farmer residing in Madhya Pradesh who is an active beneficiary of PM-KISAN.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Tax Exempt)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **PM-KISAN Beneficiary Registration Number** (MoA&FW / PM-KISAN): Mandatory eligibility baseline.
- **Khasra / B-1 Land Revenue Record in MP** (Patwari / MP Bhulekh): Proves agricultural land holdings.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT credit.

## How to Apply
### Patwari Halka / Gram Panchayat Sachiv (panchayat)
Verify land parcel seeding on SAARA portal.

### SAARA MP Revenue Portal (online)
Check installment status at saara.mp.gov.in.
Link: https://saara.mp.gov.in

## Caveats
Farmers excluded from PM-KISAN (income tax payees, constitutional posts) are also excluded here.

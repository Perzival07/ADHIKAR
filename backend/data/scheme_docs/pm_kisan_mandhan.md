# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maan-Dhan Yojana (PM-KMY)

## Summary
Voluntary contributory old age pension scheme guaranteeing ₹3,000 per month from age 60 for small and marginal farmers holding up to 2 hectares of cultivable land.

## Benefit
Guaranteed pension of ₹3,000 per month upon reaching 60 years. Central Government provides 50% matching contribution equivalent to farmer monthly subscription (₹55 to ₹200).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a small or marginal farmer aged 18 to 40 holding cultivable land up to 2 hectares (5 acres).

- Region: All India
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Primary biometric identification.
- **Land Revenue Records (Khasra / Khatauni up to 2 hectares)** (Tehsildar): Proves cultivable land area within 5 acres.
- **Savings Bank Account details with IFSC** (Bank Branch): Required for setting up auto-debit of subscription.

## How to Apply
### Common Service Centre (CSC) Kisan Enrolment (csc)
Enrol with Aadhaar and bank details to generate instant Kisan Pension Card.
Link: https://maandhan.in

### Maan-Dhan Self-Enrolment Portal (online)
Self-register using mobile OTP and bank details.
Link: https://maandhan.in

## Caveats
Farmers already enrolled in PM-KISAN can opt to have their monthly contribution directly deducted from their PM-KISAN ₹2,000 installment.

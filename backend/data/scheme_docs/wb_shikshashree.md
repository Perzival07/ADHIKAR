# Shikshashree Scheme (Pre-Matric Assistance for SC/ST)

## Summary
Annual financial assistance of ₹800 per year directly transferred to Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) students studying in Classes 5 to 8 to curb early dropouts.

## Benefit
Direct financial stipend of ₹800 per academic year credited directly to the student’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You are an SC or ST student studying in Classes 5 to 8 in a recognized school in West Bengal with family income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 10–15
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: School (Class 5-8)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Start of School Session)

## Documents Required
- **SC / ST Community Certificate** (Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO)): Proof of affirmative category.
- **Family Income Certificate (< ₹2.5 Lakh)** (BDO / Municipality): Income eligibility.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct stipend transfer.

## How to Apply
### School Headmaster / Nodal Teacher Desk (blockOffice)
School uploads student roster directly to Shikshashree portal.

### Anagrasar Kalyan Portal (online)
View scheme notifications at anagrasarkalyan.gov.in.
Link: https://anagrasarkalyan.gov.in

## Caveats
Family income must not exceed ₹2,50,000 per annum.

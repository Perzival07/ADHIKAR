# Nagaland Senior Citizen Old Age Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹500 (aged 60-79) and ₹1,000 (aged 80+) per month for vulnerable elderly Naga citizens living in poverty.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹500 to ₹1,000 directly credited into the senior citizen’s savings bank account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous senior citizen aged 60 or older residing in Nagaland holding a BPL card or with income under ₹24,000.

- Region: Nagaland
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Certificate (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Church Baptism Record)** (Election Commission / Church Authority): Must confirm age 60 or older.
- **Village Council Chairman (VCC) Poverty Certificate** (Village Council Chairman): Confirms low income status.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Nagaland** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) / Village Council Office (panchayat)
Submit application form through Village Council Chairman.

### Social Welfare Nagaland Portal (online)
Track pension lists at socialwelfare.nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.nagaland.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must be verified by the Village Council Chairman (VCC) or Ward Authority.

# Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme (BSCCS)

## Summary
Education loan of up to ₹4 Lakhs at highly subsidized interest rates (1% for women, disabled, and transgender; 4% for male students) with complete state guarantee.

## Benefit
Concessional education loan up to ₹4 Lakh covering course tuition fees, hostel expenses, laptop, and books with repayment starting 1 year after course completion.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a 12th pass student from Bihar admitted to a recognized higher education or technical institution.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 17–25
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Admission Letter and Fee Structure from Institute** (College / University): Specifies exact tuition and hostel schedule.
- **10th and 12th Board Marksheets** (BSEB / CBSE / ICSE): Academic proof.
- **Residential Certificate (Nivas Praman Patra)** (Circle Officer): Proves permanent Bihar residence.

## How to Apply
### MNSSBY Portal (online)
Apply online at 7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in.
Link: https://www.7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in

### District Registration and Counseling Centre (DRCC) (blockOffice)
Visit local DRCC with original documents for biometric verification.

## Caveats
Institution must be accredited by NAAC (minimum B grade) or approved by AICTE/UGC/NIRF.

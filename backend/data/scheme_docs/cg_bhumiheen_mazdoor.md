# Rajiv Gandhi Gramin Bhumiheen Krishi Mazdoor Nyay Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
Annual financial assistance of ₹10,000 directly transferred to landless agricultural labourer families, ironsmiths, barbers, washermen, and traditional priests in rural areas.

## Benefit
₹10,000 per year delivered in periodic tranches directly into the head of household’s bank account to support basic livelihood security.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a rural resident of Chhattisgarh who does not own cultivable agricultural land and relies on manual labour or village services.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card of Head of Household** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Patwari Certificate confirming No Agricultural Land** (Tehsil / Patwari): Mandatory condition.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat Secretary / Gram Sevak Office (panchayat)
Submit application to Gram Panchayat for Gram Sabha approval.

### RGGBKMNY Portal (online)
Check name in published village beneficiary list.
Link: https://rggbkmny.cg.nic.in

## Caveats
Family must possess zero agricultural land in Bhuiyan revenue records.

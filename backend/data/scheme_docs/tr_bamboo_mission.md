# Tripura Bamboo & Cane Artisan Support Scheme

## Summary
Distribution of advanced electric bamboo processing machinery, modern toolkits, working capital subsidies, and access to common facility design centers for traditional bamboo and cane artisans.

## Benefit
Free motorized bamboo slicing/cross-cutting toolkits + up to ₹25,000 working capital grant for micro bamboo artisan clusters.

## Eligibility
You are a traditional or skilled bamboo, cane, or handicraft artisan residing in Tripura.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Pehchan Artisan ID Card / TBM Registration** (Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) / TBM): Proof of certified artisan trade.
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRTC)** (SDM Office): Residency verification.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct grant credit.

## How to Apply
### Tripura Bamboo Mission Cluster Office / District Industries Centre (DIC) (blockOffice)
Submit artisan portfolio and group registration.

### Tripura Industries & Commerce Portal (online)
View design catalog at industries.tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://industries.tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Artisan must be registered with the State Handicrafts Board or TBM database.

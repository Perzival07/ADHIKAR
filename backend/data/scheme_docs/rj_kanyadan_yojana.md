# Mukhyamantri Kanyadan / Hathleva Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
Direct marriage financial grant of ₹31,000 to ₹51,000 for daughters of BPL, SC, ST, minority, and destitute families upon solemnization of legal marriage.

## Benefit
₹31,000 cash grant + additional ₹10,000 if bride has passed Class 10th + additional ₹20,000 if bride has passed Graduation (total up to ₹51,000 credited via DBT).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a parent or adult bride residing in Rajasthan from an identified BPL/SC/ST/EWS family.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Marriage Registration Certificate / Invitation Card** (Registrar of Marriages / Gram Panchayat): Mandatory marriage proof.
- **BPL Card / Antyodaya Card / Income Certificate** (Food Dept / Tehsildar): Poverty criteria verification.
- **10th / Graduation Marksheet (for bonus ₹10k-₹20k)** (School / University Board): Required for higher education bonus grant.

## How to Apply
### e-Mitra Kiosk / Social Justice Office (csc)
Submit online form with marriage registration and marksheet.

### SJED Rajasthan Official Portal (online)
Track application status at sje.rajasthan.gov.in.
Link: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in

## Caveats
Bride must have completed 18 years of age. Application can be submitted up to 1 month before or 6 months after marriage.

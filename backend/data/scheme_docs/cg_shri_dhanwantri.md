# Shri Dhanwantri Generic Medical Store Scheme (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
Quality branded generic medicines, surgical items, and baby food supplied at 50% to 71% maximum discount off MRP across 190+ dedicated pharmacy stores in all towns.

## Benefit
Guaranteed 50% to 71% cash discount on all essential medical drugs, antibiotics, diabetes/hypertension medicines, and infant food at municipal Dhanwantri stores.

## Eligibility
You qualify as any citizen purchasing prescribed medical drugs at Shri Dhanwantri stores across Chhattisgarh.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Doctor's Prescription Slip** (Registered Medical Practitioner): Required for dispensing scheduled drugs.

## How to Apply
### Any Shri Dhanwantri Medical Store in your Municipal Area (blockOffice)
Present prescription directly at counter for instant discounted billing.
Link: https://uad.cg.gov.in

### Urban CG Medical Store Locator (online)
Locate nearest Dhanwantri store.
Link: https://uad.cg.gov.in

## Caveats
No ration card or economic criteria required; universal discount for all citizens.

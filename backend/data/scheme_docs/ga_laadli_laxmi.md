# Laadli Laxmi Scheme (Goa)

## Summary
Fixed deposit financial assistance of ₹1 Lakh deposited in the name of eligible Goan girl upon attaining 18 years of age to support marriage or career startup.

## Benefit
₹1 Lakh fixed deposit issued in the name of the girl, redeemable upon formal legal marriage registration or upon reaching 40 years if unmarried.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female resident of Goa aged 18 to 40 who has resided in Goa for at least 15 years.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **15-Year Residence Certificate of Girl or Parent** (Mamlatdar): Mandatory 15-year domicile in Goa.
- **Girl's Birth Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths Goa): Verifies age 18+.
- **School Leaving / Passing Certificate (Class 8+)** (Recognized School): Educational criteria proof.

## How to Apply
### Goa Online Laadli Laxmi Service (online)
Submit digital form at goaonline.gov.in.
Link: https://goaonline.gov.in

### WCD Taluka Office / Child Development Project Office (blockOffice)
Submit original documents for physical scrutiny.

## Caveats
Girl or at least one parent must have resided in Goa for minimum 15 years. Passing Class 8 minimum is required.

# Punjab Old Age Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹1,500 per month directly credited into the bank accounts of senior citizens aged 65+ (males) and 58+ (females) residing in Punjab.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,500 credited directly into the elderly citizen’s savings bank account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen (65+ for male, 58+ for female) residing in Punjab with family income under ₹60,000/yr.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 58+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Certificate showing 65+ male or 58+ female (Aadhaar / Voter ID)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Validates relaxed age threshold.
- **Income Certificate showing family income under ₹60,000** (Tehsildar / Sewa Kendra): Mandatory poverty proof.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Punjab** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit of ₹1,500.

## How to Apply
### Sewa Kendra Punjab / CDPO Office (csc)
Submit application form with age and income certificate.

### e-Sewa Punjab Social Security Portal (online)
Apply online via connect.punjab.gov.in.
Link: https://socialsecurity.punjab.gov.in

## Caveats
Family must not own more than 2.5 acres of agricultural land.

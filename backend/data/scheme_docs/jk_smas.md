# State Marriage Assistance Scheme (SMAS Jammu & Kashmir)

## Summary
One-time financial assistance of ₹50,000 along with 5 grams of gold coin for unmarried poor girls living below poverty line upon attaining marriageable age.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹50,000 to the bride’s bank account plus 5-gram pure gold coin for dignified marriage ceremonies.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unmarried woman aged 18+ resident in Jammu & Kashmir from an identified Antyodaya/BPL family.

- Region: Jammu and Kashmir
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **J&K Domicile Certificate** (Tehsildar): Proves permanent residency in J&K.
- **Priority Household (PHH) / AAY Ration Card** (Food, Civil Supplies & CA Dept J&K): Proves BPL economic criteria.
- **Age Proof showing 18+ years** (School Board / Municipal Corp): Verifies legal marriageable age.

## How to Apply
### JK Service Plus Portal (SMAS Online) (online)
Apply online via jksocialwelfare.nic.in / jk.gov.in/jkeservices.
Link: https://jksocialwelfare.nic.in

### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) J&K (blockOffice)
Submit physical verification form at district office.

## Caveats
Application must be submitted at least one month before solemnization of marriage.

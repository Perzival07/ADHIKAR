# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## Summary
High-interest sovereign savings scheme offering 8.2% annual compounded interest and complete triple tax exemption (EEE) for parents of girl children under 10 years of age.

## Benefit
8.2% annual interest compounded yearly, deposit from ₹250 to ₹1.50 Lakh per financial year, partial withdrawal up to 50% after age 18 for higher education, and complete tax exemption.

## Eligibility
You qualify as the parent or legal guardian of a girl child aged under 10 years.

- Region: All India
- Age: 0–10
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Girl Child's Official Birth Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths / Municipal Corp): Mandatory to verify child age under 10.
- **Parent / Guardian's Aadhaar and PAN Card** (UIDAI / Income Tax Dept): Required for KYC compliance.
- **Passport size photographs of child and guardian** (Self): Required for passbook issue.

## How to Apply
### Any Post Office or Commercial Bank Branch (SBI, PNB, BoB, etc.) (bankBranch)
Submit SSY account opening form with birth certificate and initial deposit of ₹250+.
Link: https://www.indiapost.gov.in

### IPPB Mobile Banking / Bank Net Banking App (online)
Transfer monthly deposits digitally into Sukanya account.
Link: https://www.ippbonline.com

## Caveats
Maximum two accounts per family (or three in case of triplets/twins as first birth). Account matures 21 years from opening date.

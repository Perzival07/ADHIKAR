# Cheyutha Social Security Pension Scheme

## Summary
Enhanced monthly financial pension of ₹4,000 for senior citizens, widows, handloom weavers, toddy tappers, and single women, and ₹6,000 for persons with disabilities.

## Benefit
Monthly direct DBT pension of ₹4,000 (general category pensioners) or ₹6,000 (differently-abled pensioners) delivered directly to bank accounts.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an elderly resident aged 57+, widow, weaver, or person with disability in Telangana.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 57+
- Income ceiling: < 1.5 Lakh (Rural) / < 2.0 Lakh (Urban)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Food Security Card (White Ration Card)** (Civil Supplies Dept): Proof of BPL status.
- **Aadhaar Card showing Age 57+ (or SADAREM cert for PwD)** (UIDAI / Medical Board): Proves age or disability criteria.
- **Post Office / Bank Savings Account Passbook** (Bank / Post Office): For monthly DBT deposit.

## How to Apply
### Mandal Parishad Development Officer (MPDO) / Municipal Commissioner (blockOffice)
Submit application with age and BPL proof.

### Aasara / Cheyutha Pension Portal (online)
Track sanction and monthly payment history at aasara.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://aasara.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiaries must not be central or state government employees or drawing other pensions.

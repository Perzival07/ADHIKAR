# Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Higher Education Assurance (Pudhumai Penn)

## Summary
Monthly financial grant of ₹1,000 directly transferred to girl students who studied in government schools from Classes 6 to 12 until they finish their undergraduate degree, diploma, or ITI course.

## Benefit
₹1,000 per month credited throughout the duration of higher education degree or diploma course.

## Eligibility
You are a female college/diploma student who completed Classes 6 to 12 in Tamil Nadu government schools.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 17–25
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: Undergraduate / Diploma / ITI
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Start of Academic Year)

## Documents Required
- **EMIS School Bonafide Certificate (Class 6-12 Govt School)** (Headmaster / School Education Dept): Proves continuous govt school schooling.
- **College Admission & Bonafide Certificate** (College Principal / Registrar): Confirms current enrollment.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct monthly remittance.

## How to Apply
### College Nodal Officer / Principal Desk (blockOffice)
Colleges upload verified EMIS details onto the state portal.

### Pudhumai Penn State Portal (online)
Apply and check DBT transfer status at pudhumaipenn.tn.gov.in.
Link: https://pudhumaipenn.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Girl must have studied in TN Government schools from Class 6 through Class 12 without private school break.

# Deendayal Antyodaya Rasoi Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Nutritious, hygienic, hot cooked full thali meals (roti, seasonal vegetable, dal, rice) provided at a nominal price of only ₹5 per plate for the urban poor, labourers, and patients.

## Benefit
Subsidized wholesome hot cooked lunch plate (4 rotis, vegetable sabzi, dal, and rice) served daily between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM across 166+ permanent centers and mobile food vans.

## Eligibility
You qualify as any citizen visiting Deendayal Rasoi centers in any urban local body across Madhya Pradesh.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Spot Token at Food Counter (₹5 cash)** (Deendayal Rasoi Counter): Purchase ₹5 meal token directly at center.

## How to Apply
### Any Deendayal Rasoi Centre (Bus Stand / District Hospital / Railway Station area) (blockOffice)
Walk into dining hall and purchase meal token for ₹5.
Link: https://mpurban.gov.in

### MP Urban Administration Portal (online)
Locate nearest Deendayal Rasoi center or mobile van.
Link: https://mpurban.gov.in

## Caveats
No ration card or economic documentation required; universal access for all needy citizens.

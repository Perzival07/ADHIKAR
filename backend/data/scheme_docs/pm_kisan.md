# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## Summary
Income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 to all landholding farmer families across India.

## Benefit
₹6,000 per year directly credited to Aadhaar-seeded bank account in 3 instalments (April-July, August-November, December-March).

## Eligibility
You are eligible because you own cultivable agricultural land and are engaged in farming.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Tax Exempt)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card (Mandatory with linked mobile)** (UIDAI): Must be linked with your bank account for DBT payments.
- **Land Ownership Record (Khatauni / RoR / Khasra)** (State Revenue Dept): Obtain from Tehsil or state Bhulekh portal.
- **Bank Passbook with IFSC** (Bank Branch / Post Office): NPCI Aadhaar mapping must be active.

## How to Apply
### PM-KISAN Self-Registration Portal (online)
Visit Farmers Corner on pmkisan.gov.in and complete e-KYC.
Link: https://pmkisan.gov.in

### Common Service Centre (CSC) / Tehsil Office (csc)
Visit CSC with Aadhaar, bank passbook, and land Khatauni.

## Caveats
Excludes institutional landholders and families where any member pays Income Tax or holds constitutional/gazetted posts.

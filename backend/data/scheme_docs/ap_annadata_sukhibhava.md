# Annadata Sukhibhava / Rythu Bharosa (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct income support of ₹20,000 per year to farmer families across Andhra Pradesh (combining ₹6,000 PM-KISAN + ₹14,000 State DBT).

## Benefit
₹20,000 per year delivered in installments directly to bank account, covering landowning farmers and tenant farmers holding CCRC.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landholding farmer or registered tenant farmer (CCRC) in Andhra Pradesh.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Tax Exempt)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Pattadar Passbook / 1-B Adangal / CCRC Agreement** (Revenue Dept AP): Proof of land ownership or cultivation rights.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Mandatory for DBT linkage.
- **Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch): Must be linked with NPCI mapper.

## How to Apply
### Rythu Bharosa Kendra (RBK) / Village Agriculture Assistant (panchayat)
Verify land data in e-Crop booking at local RBK.
Link: https://karshak.ap.gov.in

### Annadata Sukhibhava Portal (online)
Verify payment status using Aadhaar number.
Link: https://annadathasukhibhava.ap.gov.in

## Caveats
Tenant farmers from SC, ST, BC, and Minority communities are eligible under Crop Cultivator Rights Card (CCRC).

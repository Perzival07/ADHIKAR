# Mission Youth Mumkin Scheme (Jammu & Kashmir)

## Summary
Livelihood generation scheme providing 100% interest-free loans and capital subsidy up to ₹8 Lakh for unemployed youth to purchase commercial vehicles.

## Benefit
Mission Youth provides upfront capital subsidy of ₹80,000 + auto manufacturer provides matching ₹80,000 discount, with balance 80% financed by bank with 100% interest subvention.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed youth aged 18 to 35 residing in Jammu & Kashmir with a valid driving license.

- Region: Jammu and Kashmir
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **J&K Domicile Certificate** (Tehsildar): Mandatory residence proof.
- **Commercial / Light Motor Vehicle Driving License** (RTO J&K): Must be active and valid.
- **Employment Department Registration Card** (District Employment Exchange): Proves unemployed status.

## How to Apply
### Mission Youth Portal J&K (online)
Apply online at missionyouth.jk.gov.in.
Link: https://missionyouth.jk.gov.in

### Office of the Deputy Commissioner / Mission Youth (blockOffice)
Attend district level verification committee.

## Caveats
Applicant must be registered with the J&K Employment Department and not have existing government employment.

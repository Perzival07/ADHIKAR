# Sambal Yojana 2.0 (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Comprehensive social security umbrella for unorganized workers: ₹4 Lakh accidental death grant, ₹2 Lakh normal death grant, ₹16,000 maternity aid, and full fee waiver for children.

## Benefit
Ex-gratia relief of ₹4 Lakh (Accidental death), ₹2 Lakh (Natural death), ₹16,000 (Maternity cash), ₹5,000 (Funeral assistance), and 100% college tuition fee waiver for children.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unorganized worker residing in Madhya Pradesh holding an active Sambal 2.0 digital card.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Samagra ID with Aadhaar e-KYC** (Samagra Mission): Mandatory baseline for Sambal card.
- **Self-Declaration of Unorganized Profession** (Self): Declares occupation (carpenter, labourer, driver, etc.).
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT claim payouts.

## How to Apply
### Sambal 2.0 Portal (online)
Apply online or download smart card at sambal.mp.gov.in.
Link: https://sambal.mp.gov.in

### Gram Panchayat / Ward Office Nodal Officer (panchayat)
Submit claim forms for accidental/maternity ex-gratia.

## Caveats
Worker must not own more than 1 hectare of agricultural land and must not be an income tax payer.

# Subhiksha Keralam Food Security Scheme

## Summary
Integrated food security scheme providing interest-free loans and subsidies for converting fallow lands into paddy, vegetable, banana, and dairy farming clusters.

## Benefit
Fallow land cultivation subsidy up to ₹40,000 per hectare + interest-free agricultural loans up to ₹1 Lakh through cooperative banks.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a farmer, Kudumbashree JLGs, or youth group cultivating fallow land in Kerala.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **AIMS Farmer Registration ID** (Krishi Bhavan Kerala): Must be registered on aims.kerala.gov.in.
- **Land Owner Consent Letter for Fallow Land Cultivation** (Landowner / Village Officer): Confirms right to cultivate.
- **Bank Passbook with Cooperative / Commercial Bank** (Bank Branch): Required for subsidy DBT.

## How to Apply
### Krishi Bhavan at Grama Panchayat (panchayat)
Consult Agricultural Officer (AO) to submit fallow land cultivation plan.

### AIMS Kerala Portal (online)
Apply online at aims.kerala.gov.in.
Link: https://keralaagriculture.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must register the proposed land on the AIMS (Agricultural Information Management System) portal.

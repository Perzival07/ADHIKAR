# Uttar Pradesh BC Sakhi Yojana (Banking Correspondent Sakhi)

## Summary
Deployment of trained rural women as Banking Correspondent Sakhis equipped with micro-ATMs and biometric devices, providing ₹4,000 monthly stipend for 6 months plus ongoing transaction commissions.

## Benefit
₹4,000 per month stipend for first 6 months + ₹50,000 loan for micro-ATM hardware + ongoing commission on all deposits, withdrawals, and DBT transfers.

## Eligibility
You are a woman residing in a UP Gram Panchayat with minimum Class 10 qualification seeking banking agency livelihood.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: 10th Pass
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **High School (10th) Certificate** (Board of Education): Proof of basic literacy.
- **Aadhaar Card with UP Gram Panchayat Address** (UIDAI): Confirms village residence.
- **Self Help Group (SHG) Membership Proof** (UPSRLM / Gram Sachiv): Preference for SHG women members.

## How to Apply
### Block Mission Management Unit (BMMU) / UPSRLM Block Office (blockOffice)
Verification and biometric exam center allocation.

### UP BC Sakhi Mobile App / Portal (online)
Download app from Google Play Store or check upsrlm.org.
Link: https://www.upsrlm.org

## Caveats
Candidate must pass the IIBF (Indian Institute of Banking & Finance) BC certification exam.

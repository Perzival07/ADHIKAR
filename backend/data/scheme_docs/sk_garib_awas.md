# Sikkim Garib Awas Yojana (SGAY)

## Summary
Construction of fully furnished 1-storey pucca houses valued at ₹17.5 Lakhs equipped with living room, bedrooms, kitchen, toilet, and TV set for economically weaker rural families.

## Benefit
Complete pucca house built by the state government with modern sanitation, sofa, bed, and LED television at zero cost to the beneficiary.

## Eligibility
You are a houseless or kuccha house resident family of Sikkim recognized under the rural SECC survey.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 21+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Certificate of Identification (COI)** (Sub-Divisional Magistrate): Residency proof.
- **Land Possession Document (Parcha) or NOC** (Revenue Department): Site where house will be constructed.
- **Rural Income Certificate** (BDO Office): Proof of BPL / EWS status.

## How to Apply
### Block Development Officer (BDO) / Gram Panchayat Unit (GPU) (panchayat)
Submit application with land parcha during Gram Sabha verification.

### Sikkim Rural Development Portal (online)
Check scheme progress at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Family must not already own a concrete pucca house anywhere in India.

# Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi (KASP Kerala)

## Summary
Comprehensive cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalisation coverage up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year across 600+ empanelled government and private hospitals.

## Benefit
100% cashless medical treatment covering 1,600+ surgical and medical packages, diagnostics, critical ICU, and post-hospitalization medication.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Kerala enrolled under the state priority list or possessing a priority (Pink/Yellow) ration card.

- Region: Kerala
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **KASP Card / Electronic Ration Card (Pink / Yellow)** (Civil Supplies Dept Kerala): Primary document for hospital authorization.
- **Aadhaar Card of Patient** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### State Health Agency (SHA) Kerala Portal (online)
Check eligibility and empanelled hospital list at sha.kerala.gov.in.
Link: https://sha.kerala.gov.in

### Hospital KASP Helpdesk (Arogyamitra Desk) (csc)
Show card for instant biometric admission pre-authorization.

## Caveats
Beneficiaries must present their KASP/Ayushman card or priority ration card at the hospital KASP helpdesk.

# YSR Cheyutha Scheme (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹18,750 per year (totaling ₹75,000 across 4 years) for SC, ST, BC, and Minority women aged 45 to 60 for sustainable micro-livelihoods.

## Benefit
₹18,750 per year DBT directly credited to Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the beneficiary woman to set up grocery, dairy, or retail ventures.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a woman aged 45 to 60 residing in Andhra Pradesh belonging to SC, ST, BC, or Minority category.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 45–60
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card with biometric verification** (UIDAI): Must be seeded with active bank account.
- **Integrated Caste Certificate (SC/ST/BC/Minority)** (MeeSeva / Village Secretariat): Validates eligible community status.
- **Rice Card / White Ration Card** (Civil Supplies Dept AP): Confirms household income eligibility.

## How to Apply
### Grama / Ward Sachivalayam (Village / Ward Secretariat) (panchayat)
Contact Village / Ward Volunteer and Digital Assistant.
Link: https://navasakam2.ap.gov.in

### Navasakam Beneficiary Management Portal (online)
Verify inclusion in social audit displayed at secretariat.
Link: https://navasakam.ap.gov.in

## Caveats
Total family income must be under ₹10,000/month in rural areas or ₹12,000/month in urban areas.

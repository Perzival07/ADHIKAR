# Megha Health Insurance Scheme (MHIS Phase VI)

## Summary
Universal cashless hospitalisation cover of up to ₹5.30 Lakh per family per year across 2,400+ medical and surgical packages in empanelled hospitals for all residents of Meghalaya.

## Benefit
100% cashless hospital treatment covering critical surgeries, oncology, cardiology, ICU, diagnostics, and up to ₹30,000 for outpatient diagnostic tests for senior citizens.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Meghalaya holding a valid voter ID or ration card.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **MHIS Smart Card / EPIC (Voter ID) of Meghalaya** (Election Commission / Health Dept): Primary document for registration.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Identity verification and e-KYC.

## How to Apply
### Village Kiosk / Empanelled Network Hospital MHIS Desk (csc)
Show card to MHIS Arogyamitra for instant admission approval.

### MHIS Meghalaya Official Portal (online)
Download digital e-card and search network hospitals at mhis.org.in.
Link: https://mhis.org.in

## Caveats
Covers all citizens residing in Meghalaya without income ceiling (government employees and general public alike).

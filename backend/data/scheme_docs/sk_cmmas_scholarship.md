# Chief Minister’s Meritorious Scholarship Scheme (CMMAS)

## Summary
Full sponsorship covering entire tuition, boarding, lodging, and books for top-performing students from Class 5 in government schools to study in premier boarding schools and universities across India.

## Benefit
100% scholarship covering school tuition fees, hostel, clothing, food, transport, and pocket allowance until school completion.

## Eligibility
You are a meritorious Class 5 student studying in a Sikkim government school who cleared the state selection exam.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 10–13
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School (Class 5)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Annual Selection Cycle)

## Documents Required
- **Certificate of Identification / Sikkim Subject** (SDM Office): Proof of origin.
- **Class 5 Progress Report Card from Govt School** (School Headmaster): Academic merit verification.
- **Birth Certificate** (Municipal / Health Dept): Age verification.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary School Headmaster / Joint Director Education (District) (blockOffice)
Apply through school headmaster for the state competitive exam.

### Sikkim Education Portal (online)
Review exam dates and results at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Students must pass the entrance screening exam conducted by the Education Department.

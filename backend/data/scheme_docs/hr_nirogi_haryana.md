# Nirogi Haryana Scheme

## Summary
Free comprehensive preventive health checkups, including 25+ blood and diagnostic tests, physical screenings, and treatment for all Antyodaya citizens.

## Benefit
Free clinical examination, complete blood count (CBC), kidney/liver function tests, lipid profile, diabetes screening, and free specialist consultations.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an Antyodaya citizen residing in Haryana with annual family income up to ₹1.80 Lakh.

- Region: Haryana
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Mandatory for patient electronic record generation.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Primary Health Centre (PHC) / Community Health Centre (CHC) / Civil Hospital (csc)
Walk in with Family ID for complete free screening.

### Haryana Health Portal (online)
Download digital lab reports using Family ID.
Link: https://haryanahealth.gov.in

## Caveats
Family members are grouped into 6 age brackets with customized test packages for each age group.

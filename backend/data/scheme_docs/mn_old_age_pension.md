# Manipur Old Age Pension Scheme (MOAPS)

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹500 (aged 60-79) and ₹1,000 (aged 80+) per month credited directly to bank accounts of destitute senior citizens of Manipur.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹500 to ₹1,000 directly credited into the elderly citizen’s savings bank account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 60 or older residing in Manipur holding a BPL card or with annual family income under ₹24,000.

- Region: Manipur
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Certificate (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Birth Certificate)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Must verify age 60 or older.
- **Income Certificate (family income under ₹24,000)** (Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO)): Validates economic need.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Manipur** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Office (DSWO) Manipur (blockOffice)
Submit application form with age and income certificate.

### Social Welfare Manipur Portal (online)
Track pension sanction list at socialwelfare.mn.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.mn.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must not be a retired government servant or receiving any other statutory pension.

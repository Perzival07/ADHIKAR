# Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana (Pradhan Mantri TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyan)

## Summary
Direct Benefit Transfer of ₹500 per month for nutritional support to all notified tuberculosis patients throughout their entire treatment duration.

## Benefit
₹500 per month directly credited to the patient’s bank account for each month of active anti-TB treatment + supplementary nutrition kits from Ni-kshay Mitras.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a patient diagnosed with Tuberculosis and notified on the government Ni-kshay national health portal.

- Region: All India
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Ni-kshay Enrolment ID / TB Treatment Card** (DOTS Center / Health Facility): Generated upon medical diagnosis and notification.
- **Bank Account Details with Aadhaar seeding** (Bank Branch): Direct DBT transfer requires active NPCI mapping.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Mandatory for patient deduplication.

## How to Apply
### Government DOTS Centre / Primary Health Centre (panchayat)
Health worker records bank details on Ni-kshay portal upon diagnosis.
Link: https://nikshay.in

### Ni-kshay Citizen Portal (online)
Track DBT installment status and find local Ni-kshay Mitras.
Link: https://nikshay.in

## Caveats
Both public sector and private clinic diagnosed TB patients are entitled to the financial benefit.

# Chief Minister's Samast Shiksha Yojana (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Free school uniforms, modern bilingual learning modules, smart digital classroom infrastructure, and board examination merit awards for students in government schools.

## Benefit
Free textbooks, two sets of uniforms, free school bags, and ₹10,000 cash reward for top achievers in Class 10 and 12 board examinations.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an enrolled student in a government or aided school across Arunachal Pradesh.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 6–18
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School Admission Record / Student Bonafide** (School Headmaster): Enrolment record.
- **APST Certificate of Child or Parent** (DC Office): Eligibility proof.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary / Secondary School Headmaster Office (panchayat)
Entitlements issued on admission.
Link: https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in

### Arunachal Education Portal (online)
View academic circulars and board toppers list.
Link: https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in

## Caveats
Students must maintain 75% attendance in classes.

# UP Divyangjan Pension Yojana (Disability Pension)

## Summary
Monthly financial pension of ₹1,000 (quarterly ₹3,000) credited directly to persons with 40% or more disability living below the poverty ceiling.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,000 per month directly into the pensioner’s bank account to support basic living and healthcare expenses.

## Eligibility
You are a person with 40%+ benchmark disability residing in Uttar Pradesh.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 46,080 (Rural) / < 56,460 (Urban)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **UDID Card / CMO Disability Certificate (40%+)** (Chief Medical Officer (CMO)): Proves disability percentage.
- **Income Certificate** (Tahsildar via eDistrict): Income verification.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct credit.

## How to Apply
### CSC Jan Seva Kendra / District Divyangjan Empowerment Officer (csc)
Submit online form along with UDID card.

### Integrated Pension Portal (SSPY) (online)
Apply online at sspy-up.gov.in.
Link: https://sspy-up.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must have UDID (Unique Disability ID) or CMO disability certificate.

# Mukhyamantri Medhavruti Yojana for SC/ST Girls (Bihar)

## Summary
Additional cash scholarship of ₹15,000 for 1st division and ₹10,000 for 2nd division for SC and ST girl students passing Intermediate (12th) examinations.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹15,000 (1st Div) or ₹10,000 (2nd Div) credited into the student’s bank account to encourage higher collegiate education.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an SC or ST female student who passed Intermediate (12th) from Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB).

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 16–25
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **BSEB Intermediate Marksheet and Admit Card** (BSEB): Proves pass division.
- **Caste Certificate (SC/ST)** (Circle Officer): Mandatory for SC/ST verification.
- **Student Bank Account Details in Bihar** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### MedhaSoft Portal (Medhavruti Section) (online)
Apply online at medhasoft.bih.nic.in.
Link: https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in

### Intermediate College Office (panchayat)
Verify college registration roster.

## Caveats
Can be availed in addition to the standard Kanya Utthan ₹25,000 incentive.

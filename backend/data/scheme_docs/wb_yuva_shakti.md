# Yuva Shakti / Yuva Shakti Bharosa Card Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly financial support of ₹3,000 per month for unemployed graduate youth (or ₹2,000 per month for other unemployed youth) aged 21 to 45 alongside state-backed credit card career assistance.

## Benefit
Direct monthly transfer of ₹3,000/mo (graduates) or ₹2,000/mo (non-graduates) to support youth undergoing skill development or preparing for public sector examinations.

## Eligibility
You are a resident of West Bengal aged 21 to 45 with annual family income below ₹1 Lakh not receiving other major DBT pensions.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 21–45
- Income ceiling: < 1.0 Lakh
- Education level: 10th / 12th / Graduate
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Educational Qualification Certificate (Degree / Marksheet)** (University / Board of Education): Determines ₹3,000 vs ₹2,000 assistance tier.
- **Income Certificate (< ₹1 Lakh/year)** (BDO / SDO / Municipality): Proof of household income ceiling.
- **Aadhaar Card with West Bengal Address** (UIDAI): Proof of identity and state residence.

## How to Apply
### Duare Sarkar Camp / Block Development Office (BDO) (blockOffice)
Submit Yuva Shakti enrollment form during state camps.

### West Bengal State Portal (online)
Track application status at wb.gov.in.
Link: https://wb.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must have family income under ₹1,00,000 and not hold permanent public/private formal employment.

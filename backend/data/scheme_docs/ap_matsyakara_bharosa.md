# YSR Matsyakara Bharosa (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Annual financial relief of ₹10,000 during the annual 61-day marine fishing ban period (April 15 to June 14) + subsidized diesel at ₹9 per litre for traditional fishers.

## Benefit
₹10,000 direct bank transfer during breeding ban period + diesel subsidy of ₹9/litre for mechanized and motorized boats + ₹10 Lakh accidental ex-gratia.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a traditional marine fisher operating a registered motorized or non-motorized craft in coastal Andhra Pradesh.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Boat Registration Certificate (MFRA)** (Fisheries Dept AP): Proves authorized fishing vessel.
- **Fisherman Biometric Card / Aadhaar Card** (Coastal Security / UIDAI): Validates marine fisherman status.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Coastal Village Secretariat / Fisheries Assistant (panchayat)
Submit boat inspection slip to Village Fisheries Assistant.
Link: https://fisheries.ap.gov.in

### AP Fisheries Portal (online)
Verify beneficiary enrollment status.
Link: https://fisheries.ap.gov.in

## Caveats
Only active sea-going fishers operating registered boats with MFRA licenses are eligible.

# Nagaland State Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme

## Summary
Full compulsory tuition fee reimbursement, maintenance allowances, and study tour grants for indigenous Naga students enrolled in post-matric, degree, and technical colleges.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of full course tuition fees + monthly maintenance allowance up to ₹1,200/month directly into student’s personal bank account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous Scheduled Tribe student of Nagaland pursuing post-matric studies with family income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Nagaland
- Age: 16–28
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Indigenous Inhabitant / ST Certificate of Nagaland** (Deputy Commissioner): Mandatory indigenous tribal proof.
- **College Admission Fee Receipt and Marksheet** (College Principal): Proves active enrollment and fee details.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Account in Nagaland** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Common Scholarship Portal of Nagaland (online)
Apply online at scholarship.nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://scholarship.nagaland.gov.in

### College Nodal Scholarship Officer Desk (panchayat)
Submit verification copy to college principal.

## Caveats
Student must maintain minimum 75% attendance in college and pass annual semester exams.

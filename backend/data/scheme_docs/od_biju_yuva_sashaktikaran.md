# Biju Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana (Free Laptop Scheme Odisha)

## Summary
Free modern high-speed laptop (or ₹30,000 direct bank transfer) awarded to meritorious Class 12th (CHSE) students across Science, Commerce, Arts, and Vocational streams.

## Benefit
Free branded laptop with latest Windows OS, processor, and 3-year warranty (or direct ₹30,000 transfer into student’s bank account to purchase a device of choice).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student in Odisha who secured high merit ranking in CHSE Class 12th board examinations.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 16–22
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **CHSE Odisha Class 12th Board Marksheet and Pass Certificate** (Council of Higher Secondary Education (CHSE) Odisha): Mandatory merit rank proof.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Higher Education / College ID Card** (Admitting College Principal): Proves continuation of higher education.

## How to Apply
### DHE Odisha Higher Education Portal (online)
Check merit selection list at dhe.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://dhe.odisha.gov.in

### District Nodal College (e.g. BJB Autonomous College, Ravenshaw, etc.) (blockOffice)
Collect laptop with allotment token and original marksheet.

## Caveats
Laptops are distributed across streams (Science, Commerce, Arts) strictly on merit rank cutoffs.

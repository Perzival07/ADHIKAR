# Mission Vatsalya (Child Protection Services)

## Summary
Non-institutional care monthly sponsorship of ₹4,000 per month per child for orphans, children of widows, or children in extreme distress to ensure education within family.

## Benefit
Direct monthly sponsorship assistance of ₹4,000 per child credited to the joint bank account of child and guardian until the child attains 18 years of age.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an orphan, child living with extended family/widowed mother in extreme financial vulnerability.

- Region: All India
- Age: 0–18
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Child Welfare Committee (CWC) Recommendation Order** (District CWC): Mandatory statutory authorization for sponsorship.
- **Death Certificate of Parents (for orphans)** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Required to verify orphan/single-parent status.
- **School Bonafide Certificate** (Headmaster): Confirms child is continuously attending formal school.

## How to Apply
### District Child Protection Unit (DCPU) / Child Welfare Committee (blockOffice)
Submit sponsorship application form with guardian documents.
Link: https://wcd.gov.in

### Ministry of WCD Portal (online)
Track guidelines and district helpline contacts.
Link: https://wcd.gov.in

## Caveats
Family income must not exceed ₹72,000/yr (rural) or ₹96,000/yr (urban).

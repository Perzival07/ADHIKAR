# Mizoram Widow Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly social welfare pension of ₹500 per month directly credited to the bank accounts of destitute widowed women residing in Mizoram.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹500 directly credited into the widow’s bank account to support basic household maintenance.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a widowed woman residing in Mizoram with annual family income under ₹24,000.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Husband's Death Certificate / Church Record** (Registrar of Births & Deaths / Church Pastor): Mandatory proof of widowhood.
- **Village Council Income Certificate** (Village Council President): Confirms destitute status.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Mizoram** (Bank Branch in Mizoram): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) Mizoram (blockOffice)
Submit application with husband death certificate.

### Social Welfare Mizoram Portal (online)
Check beneficiary sanction list at socialwelfare.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must not have remarried and must not be in receipt of any other regular government pension.

# Annapurna Bhandar Scheme (West Bengal)

## Summary
Flagship enhanced women welfare initiative providing ₹3,000 per month via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into verified bank accounts, plus 100% free bus travel in state government buses for female residents.

## Benefit
Direct monthly transfer of ₹3,000 into the female beneficiary’s bank account + unlimited free bus travel on all state government-operated buses.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a permanent female resident of West Bengal from low and middle-income families.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 21–60
- Income ceiling: Low / Middle Income
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Swasthya Sathi Card / Family Card** (Health & Family Welfare Dept WB): Proof of household identity.
- **Aadhaar Card of Female Beneficiary** (UIDAI): Identity verification and biometric KYC.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Single Bank Account Passbook** (Scheduled Commercial Bank): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Duare Sarkar Camp / BDO Office / Borough Executive Office (blockOffice)
Submit physical application form during Duare Sarkar outreach camp.

### West Bengal State Portal (online)
Track registration and payment status at wb.gov.in.
Link: https://wb.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must not be in regular central or state government employment with monthly pension.

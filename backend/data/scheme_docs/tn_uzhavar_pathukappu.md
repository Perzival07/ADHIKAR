# Chief Minister’s Uzhavar Pathukappu Thittam (Farmers Social Security)

## Summary
Comprehensive social security umbrella providing monthly pensions of ₹1,000 for destitute elderly farmers, marriage grants (up to ₹10,000), funeral relief, and scholarship aid for farmers children.

## Benefit
₹1,000/month old age pension + ₹10,000 daughter marriage grant + ₹20,000 natural death relief + ₹1,00,000 accident death assistance.

## Eligibility
You are an agricultural laborer or small/marginal farmer registered with the Farmers Social Security Scheme.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 18–65
- Income ceiling: < 1.2 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Uzhavar Pathukappu Membership Card** (Special Tahsildar (SSS)): Proof of scheme enrollment.
- **Smart Ration Card** (Civil Supplies Dept): Family unit verification.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Special Tahsildar (Social Security Schemes) / e-Sevai Center (csc)
Submit claim forms for marriage, funeral, or educational assistance.

### Tamil Nadu Revenue Portal (online)
Verify scheme provisions at tn.gov.in.
Link: https://www.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Member must hold an Uzhavar Pathukappu Membership Identity Card.

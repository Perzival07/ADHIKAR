# Chief Minister's Corpus Fund for Unemployed Youth (Nagaland)

## Summary
Seed capital soft loans and grants up to ₹5 Lakh at nominal 4% interest to unemployed educated Naga youth to launch private enterprises and service ventures.

## Benefit
Concessional seed capital up to ₹5 Lakh with 20% margin money grant and repayment tenure up to 5 years through Nagaland Industrial Development Corporation (NIDC).

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed educated indigenous youth of Nagaland aged 18 to 35 registered with the employment exchange.

- Region: Nagaland
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate (IIC)** (Deputy Commissioner): Mandatory indigenous proof.
- **Live Employment Registration Card** (Department of Employment & Craftsmen Training): Proves unemployed status.
- **Project Proposal & Machinery Quotations** (Self): Technical and commercial details.

## How to Apply
### Planning & Coordination Department, Nagaland Civil Secretariat, Kohima (blockOffice)
Submit project dossier to Member Secretary, CM Corpus Fund.

### Nagaland State Portal (online)
Track guidelines at nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://nagaland.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must not have defaulted on earlier government or bank loan schemes.

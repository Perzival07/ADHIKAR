# One Family One Livelihood Scheme (Manipur)

## Summary
Financial assistance of up to ₹10 Lakh (combining 30% government grant + bank loan) to families displaced or severely affected to establish sustainable livelihoods.

## Benefit
Upfront 30% government capital grant up to ₹3 Lakh on project bank loans up to ₹10 Lakh for agriculture, handloom, livestock, and retail ventures.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident family of Manipur without regular employment income seeking dedicated livelihood rehabilitation.

- Region: Manipur
- Age: 18–55
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Resident / Domicile Certificate of Manipur** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): Proves state residency.
- **Ration Card of Manipur** (CAF&PD Dept): Family verification.
- **Livelihood Project Plan (Fishery, Poultry, Weaving, Retail)** (Self): Details planned economic activity.

## How to Apply
### Deputy Commissioner Office / SDO Office Manipur (blockOffice)
Submit application to District Livelihood Task Force.

### Manipur State Portal (online)
Track guidelines and announcements at manipur.gov.in.
Link: https://manipur.gov.in

## Caveats
Family must not have any member in regular government service or organized PSU employment.

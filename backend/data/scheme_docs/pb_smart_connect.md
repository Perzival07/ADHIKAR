# Punjab Smart Connect Scheme (Free Smartphone for Students)

## Summary
Free touchscreen 4G smartphones distributed to government senior secondary school students in Class 12 to support digital education, e-learning, and competitive exams.

## Benefit
Free branded 4G smartphone pre-loaded with government school e-curriculum (e-Punjab School app) and academic question banks to facilitate digital learning.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an enrolled student in Class 12 in a government senior secondary school across Punjab.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 16–19
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **e-Punjab School Student Unique ID** (Government School Headmaster): Enrolment proof in Class 12.
- **Student's Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Government Senior Secondary School Principal Desk (panchayat)
Smartphones handed over directly in school distribution drives.

### e-Punjab School Portal (online)
Track academic learning modules at epunjabschool.gov.in.
Link: https://punjab.gov.in

## Caveats
Universal entitlement for all regular students studying in government Class 12 classes with zero charges.

# Chief Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme (Meghalaya)

## Summary
Annual merit-cum-means scholarship of ₹5,000 to ₹20,000 per year for indigenous tribal students pursuing post-matric, degree, and engineering/medical courses.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹5,000/yr (HS/Diploma), ₹10,000/yr (Degree), and ₹20,000/yr (Professional Medical/Engineering) credited directly to student account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous Scheduled Tribe student of Meghalaya enrolled in a recognized college/university with family income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 16–28
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Meghalaya Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): Mandatory indigenous tribal proof.
- **College Admission Fee Receipt and Marksheet** (College Principal): Proves active course enrollment.
- **Student Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Meghalaya** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### National Scholarship Portal (NSP) / Meghalaya Education Portal (online)
Apply online under Meghalaya state section at scholarships.gov.in.
Link: https://megeducation.gov.in

### College Nodal Officer Desk (panchayat)
Submit verification copy to college principal.

## Caveats
Student must maintain minimum 50% marks in previous examinations and 75% attendance.

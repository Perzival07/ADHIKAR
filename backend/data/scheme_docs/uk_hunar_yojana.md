# Mukhyamantri Hunar Yojana (Craftsman Skilling & Toolkit)

## Summary
Free residential skill training, free advanced toolkits, and seed capital assistance up to ₹25,000 for traditional artisans, woodcarvers, ringal weavers, and minority craftsmen in Uttarakhand.

## Benefit
Free advanced skill development training + certified toolkit worth ₹10,000 + seed grant to establish independent artisanal enterprise.

## Eligibility
You are a traditional artisan or minority craftsman in Uttarakhand seeking modern skill enhancement.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: < 2.0 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mool Niwas / Domicile Certificate** (Tahsildar / SDM): Residency proof.
- **Traditional Artisan Proof / Gram Pradhan Certificate** (Gram Pradhan / DIC): Certifies craft trade.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct seed grant deposit.

## How to Apply
### District Minority Welfare Officer (DMWO) / DIC Office (blockOffice)
Register for upcoming skill training batches.

### Uttarakhand Minority Welfare Portal (online)
View training notifications at minoritywelfare.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://minoritywelfare.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must be engaged in traditional handicrafts, weaving, ringal, or metal craft.

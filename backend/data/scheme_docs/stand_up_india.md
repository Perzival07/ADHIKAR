# Stand-Up India Scheme

## Summary
Bank loans between ₹10 Lakh and ₹1 Crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch for setting up a greenfield enterprise.

## Benefit
Composite bank loan (term loan + working capital) covering up to 85% of project cost between ₹10 Lakh and ₹1 Crore with lowest applicable interest rates and margin money support.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an SC/ST or woman entrepreneur setting up a new venture in manufacturing, services, or agri-allied sector.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **PAN Card and Aadhaar Card** (IT Dept / UIDAI): Mandatory identity and credit check proof.
- **Caste Certificate (for SC/ST applicants)** (Tehsil): Required for SC/ST category verification.
- **Greenfield Project Proposal and Quotations** (Chartered Accountant / Self): Contains machinery cost estimates, site details, and financial projections.

## How to Apply
### Stand-Up Mitra Portal (online)
Apply online and choose preferred bank branches at standupmitra.in.
Link: https://www.standupmitra.in

### Scheduled Commercial Bank Branch (bankBranch)
Meet Branch Manager with project dossier for loan sanction.

## Caveats
Enterprise must be a greenfield project. If non-individual enterprise, at least 51% shareholding must be held by SC/ST or woman entrepreneur.

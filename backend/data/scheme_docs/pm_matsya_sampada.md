# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)

## Summary
Flagship scheme for sustainable fisheries development offering 40% (general) to 60% (SC/ST/women) financial subsidy for fish ponds, biofloc, RAS, and insulated transport vehicles.

## Benefit
Direct subsidy of 40% of unit cost for general category and 60% for SC, ST, and women beneficiaries on aquaculture inputs, motorboats, and processing equipment.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a fish farmer, traditional fisher, or youth entrepreneur establishing aquaculture infrastructure.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land / Waterbody Ownership or 7-Year Registered Lease** (Revenue Dept / Sub-Registrar): Confirms legal right to develop fishery project.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Mandatory identity document.
- **Detailed Aquaculture Project Report** (Fisheries Officer / Consultant): Specifies pond dimension, species, and equipment costs.

## How to Apply
### PMMSY National Portal (online)
Submit project online at pmmsy.dof.gov.in and select district.
Link: https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in

### District Fisheries Office (DFO) (blockOffice)
Submit hard copies to Assistant Director of Fisheries for inspection.

## Caveats
Applicant must have registered ownership or minimum 7-year registered lease deed of land/waterbody.

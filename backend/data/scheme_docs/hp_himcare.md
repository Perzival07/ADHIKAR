# HIMCARE (Himachal Health Care Scheme)

## Summary
Cashless hospitalization treatment up to ₹5 Lakh per year for families residing in Himachal Pradesh not covered under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.

## Benefit
100% cashless hospitalization, ICU, surgery, and diagnostics up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year across 260+ impanelled government and private hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a permanent resident family of Himachal Pradesh holding a state ration card.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Digital Ration Card of Himachal Pradesh** (Food & Civil Supplies Dept HP): Mandatory for family matching.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **BPL Card / Disability Certificate / Ekal Nari proof (for fee waiver)** (Competent Authority): Required for zero fee registration.

## How to Apply
### HPSBYS HIMCARE Online Portal (online)
Register or renew HIMCARE Card at hpsbys.in.
Link: https://www.hpsbys.in

### LokMitra Kendra (LMK) / Hospital Arogya Mitra Kiosk (csc)
Apply via LokMitra Kendra with ration card.

## Caveats
Card covers up to 5 family members and is renewable annually.

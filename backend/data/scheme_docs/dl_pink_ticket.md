# Delhi Free Bus Travel for Women (Pink Ticket Scheme)

## Summary
100% free bus travel across all Delhi Transport Corporation (DTC) and Delhi Integrated Multi-Modal Transit System (DIMTS) Cluster buses for all female commuters.

## Benefit
Zero-cost single-journey Pink Pass issued on-board across the entire fleet of red, green, and blue AC/non-AC electric and CNG public buses in Delhi.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female commuter boarding any DTC or Cluster public transit bus in Delhi.

- Region: Delhi (NCT)
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **No prior documentation required** (On-board Conductor): Collect single-journey Pink Ticket upon boarding.

## How to Apply
### DTC Official Portal (online)
View bus routes and operational details on dtc.delhi.gov.in.
Link: https://dtc.delhi.gov.in

### Any DTC / Cluster Bus in Service (panchayat)
Request Pink Ticket directly from conductor upon entering bus.

## Caveats
No ID card or pre-registration required; ticket issued directly on boarding.

# Namo Saraswati Vigyan Sadhana Yojana (Gujarat)

## Summary
Direct scholarship of ₹25,000 for students who choose the Science stream in Classes 11 and 12 (₹10,000 in 11th and ₹15,000 in 12th) to promote STEM careers.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹1,000/month during academic term + ₹5,000 upon passing 12th Science board examination, totaling ₹25,000.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student enrolled in Higher Secondary Science stream (Class 11 & 12) in Gujarat.

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 15–19
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Class 11/12 Science Stream Admission Slip** (School Principal): Proves enrollment in Group A / B / AB science.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹6 Lakh)** (Mamlatdar): Economic criteria proof.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Account** (Bank Branch): Direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Science Higher Secondary School Principal Desk (panchayat)
Direct processing through school portal roster.

### Digital Gujarat Scholarships Portal (online)
Verify scheme inclusion and DBT release.
Link: https://gujaratindia.gov.in

## Caveats
Family income must be under ₹6 Lakh. Both male and female science students are eligible.

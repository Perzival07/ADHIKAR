# Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak Scheme

## Summary
One-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116 disbursed directly to the bride’s mother’s bank account to support marriage expenses of girls from SC, ST, BC, EBC, and Minority families.

## Benefit
₹1,00,116 one-time direct bank transfer to the bride’s mother to ensure dignified marriage and eliminate girl child marriage debt.

## Eligibility
You are a bride aged 18+ from an SC, ST, BC, EBC, or Minority family in Telangana with annual income under ₹2 Lakh.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 2.0 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Before or within 1 month after marriage)

## Documents Required
- **Wedding Invitation Card (Lagna Patrika / Shadi Card)** (Bride / Groom Family): Details of marriage venue and date.
- **Birth Certificate / SSC Certificate of Bride** (School Board / Municipality): Confirms bride is 18 years or older.
- **Integrated Caste and Income Certificate** (Tahsildar via Meeseva): Proves category and income eligibility.
- **Bride’s Mother’s Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): Mandatory account for fund transfer.

## How to Apply
### Meeseva Citizen Service Centre (csc)
Upload application along with wedding invitation and age proofs.

### Telangana ePASS Portal (online)
Apply online at telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in.
Link: https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in

## Caveats
Bride must have completed 18 years and groom 21 years at the time of marriage.

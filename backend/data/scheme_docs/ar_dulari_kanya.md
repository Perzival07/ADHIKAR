# Dulari Kanya Scheme (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Fixed deposit of ₹20,000 opened in the name of female infant delivered in government/recognized hospitals, maturing with interest when girl child turns 18.

## Benefit
₹20,000 fixed deposit credited by state government into child’s account at birth, maturing into a significant savings corpus at age 18 upon passing Class 10.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an APST mother delivering a female child in a government or accredited hospital in Arunachal Pradesh.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 0–1
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Institutional Delivery Discharge Certificate** (Medical Superintendent / MO): Proves birth in accredited hospital.
- **Girl Child's Birth Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Confirms birth details.
- **Mother's APST Certificate and Aadhaar** (DC Office / UIDAI): Validates eligibility.

## How to Apply
### Medical Officer in-charge / CDPO Office (blockOffice)
Submit application form at hospital desk at time of discharge.

### Arunachal Health Portal (online)
Download scheme application form.
Link: https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in

## Caveats
Delivery must take place in an institutional healthcare facility (government hospital or empanelled CHC/PHC).

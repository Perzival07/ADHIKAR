# Swami Vivekananda Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana (Free Smartphone & Tablet)

## Summary
Free distribution of 2 crore high-configuration smartphones and tablets to youth enrolled in undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, skill development, and paramedical courses across UP.

## Benefit
Free digital tablet/smartphone preloaded with study materials, government e-portals, job search applications, and carrier updates.

## Eligibility
You are a student enrolled in a higher education degree or diploma course in Uttar Pradesh.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 17–30
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: College / University / ITI / Polytechnic
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **College Student Identity Card & Roll Number** (College / University): Used by college nodal officer for DigiShakti mapping.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Device biometric authentication.

## How to Apply
### College Nodal Desk / DigiShakti Distribution Campus Ceremony (blockOffice)
Collect device during college distribution function.

### DigiShakti Web Portal (online)
Check registration and dispatch status at digishakti.up.gov.in.
Link: https://digishakti.up.gov.in

## Caveats
Students are automatically mapped and verified by their colleges on the DigiShakti portal.

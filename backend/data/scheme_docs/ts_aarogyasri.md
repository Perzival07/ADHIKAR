# Rajiv Aarogyasri Health Scheme (Enhanced ₹10 Lakh Cover)

## Summary
Enhanced universal catastrophic health insurance providing 100% cashless hospitalization coverage up to ₹10,00,000 per family per year across 1,300+ government and private network hospitals.

## Benefit
Cashless treatment up to ₹10 Lakh per family per year covering 1,672 critical medical therapies, cancer treatments, and major surgeries.

## Eligibility
You are a resident family of Telangana enrolled under a White Food Security Card (White Ration Card).

- Region: Telangana
- Income ceiling: BPL (White Card)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **White Food Security Card (Ration Card)** (Civil Supplies Dept): Primary identity for insurance cashless cover.
- **Aadhaar Card of Patient** (UIDAI): Identity verification at hospital.

## How to Apply
### Aarogya Mithra Help Desk at any Network Hospital (blockOffice)
Meet Aarogya Mithra at hospital reception for pre-authorization and admission.

### Aarogyasri Official Web Portal (online)
Locate network hospitals and package lists at aarogyasri.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://aarogyasri.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Facilitated on-site at hospitals by Aarogya Mithra desk.

# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U)

## Summary
Central subsidy and financial assistance up to ₹2.50 Lakh for EWS, LIG, and Middle Income Families to construct, purchase, or expand pucca urban homes.

## Benefit
Interest subsidy up to ₹2.50 Lakh under Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) / Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC) direct grant.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an urban family belonging to EWS/LIG category not owning a pucca house anywhere in India.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Mandatory for deduplication.
- **Income Certificate / Salary Slip** (Tehsildar / Employer): Must establish EWS or LIG income limits.
- **Affidavit of No Pucca House** (Notary): Confirms no pucca house is owned anywhere in India.

## How to Apply
### PMAY-Urban Official Portal (online)
Apply online with Aadhaar and income details at pmay-urban.gov.in.
Link: https://pmay-urban.gov.in

### Urban Local Body (ULB) / Municipal Corporation Helpdesk (csc)
Submit application and documents at municipal housing cell.

## Caveats
Female ownership or co-ownership of the house is mandatory for all new house allotments under PMAY-U.

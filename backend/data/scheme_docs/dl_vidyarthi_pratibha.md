# Chief Minister Vidyarthi Pratibha Puraskar (Delhi)

## Summary
Annual financial merit award of ₹5,000 for Class 9-10 and ₹10,000 for Class 11-12 students of SC/ST/OBC/Minority categories securing 50%+ marks.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 per academic year for meritorious students in Delhi government and recognized schools.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student in Delhi studying in classes 9 to 12 belonging to SC/ST/OBC/Minority with 50%+ marks.

- Region: Delhi (NCT)
- Age: 13–19
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Previous Year Marksheet (50%+ marks)** (School Authority): Verifies academic achievement.
- **Caste / Community Certificate issued by GNCTD** (Revenue Dept GNCTD): Must be issued in Delhi.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Direct credit of scholarship.

## How to Apply
### e-District Delhi Portal (online)
Apply online via edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in with student credentials.
Link: https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in

### Delhi Government School Principal Office (panchayat)
Submit through school scholarship nodal incharge.

## Caveats
Family income ceiling is ₹3 Lakh per annum for OBC/Minority categories (no income limit for SC/ST).

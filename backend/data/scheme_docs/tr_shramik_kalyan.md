# Mukhyamantri Cha Sramik Kalyan Prakalpa

## Summary
Dedicated comprehensive welfare program providing housing land rights, pucca housing assistance, piped drinking water, electricity, ration cards, and social insurance to 7,000+ tea garden workers.

## Benefit
Free pucca housing allotment, land lease rights, clean piped drinking water, and prioritized coverage under PDS ration cards.

## Eligibility
You are an enrolled tea garden worker employed across tea estates in Tripura.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Tea Garden Worker Identity Card** (Tea Estate Management / Labour Dept): Proof of garden employment.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Permanent Resident Certificate / Plantation Roster** (Labour Officer): Residency confirmation.

## How to Apply
### Labour Officer (Tea Garden Division) / Plantation Manager Desk (blockOffice)
Verification and inclusion in garden welfare roster.

### Tripura State Portal (online)
Read scheme notifications at tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Worker must be enrolled in the register of recognized tea garden estates.

# Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
Direct social pension of ₹1,500 per month (₹2,000 for family with 2+ beneficiaries) for destitute elderly persons (65+), widows, divorced women, and persons with disabilities.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,500 credited directly into the destitute citizen’s bank account on the 1st week of every month.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a destitute elderly person, widow, or disabled person residing in Maharashtra with annual family income under ₹50,000.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **15-Year Maharashtra Residence Certificate** (Tahsildar / Setu Kendra): Mandatory 15-year continuous stay proof.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹50,000)** (Tahsildar Office): Proves destitute status.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Maharashtra** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### MahaDBT Pension Portal (online)
Apply online via mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.
Link: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

### Tahsildar Office / Setu Suvidha Kendra (panchayat)
Submit application for Tehsil Niradhar Committee sanction.

## Caveats
Applicant must have resided continuously in Maharashtra for at least 15 years.

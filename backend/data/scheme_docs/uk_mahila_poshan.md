# Mukhyamantri Mahila Poshan Yojana (Uttarakhand)

## Summary
Nutritional assistance providing weekly boiled eggs, fruits, dry rations, and roasted grams to pregnant and lactating mothers along with nutrition awareness to eliminate maternal wasting in hill regions.

## Benefit
Free weekly nutritional food rations, eggs/fruits, and nutritional counselling throughout pregnancy and lactation up to 6 months post-delivery.

## Eligibility
You are an expectant or nursing mother registered at an Anganwadi Center in Uttarakhand.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Throughout pregnancy & lactation)

## Documents Required
- **Mother & Child Protection (MCP) Card** (Anganwadi Worker / ANM): Proof of ANC registration.
- **Aadhaar Card of Mother** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Village Anganwadi Centre (panchayat)
Collect weekly nutritional supplies directly during Take Home Ration days.

### Uttarakhand WECD Portal (online)
Access program notifications at wecd.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://wecd.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must be registered on the Poshan Tracker portal.

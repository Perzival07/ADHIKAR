# Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Bunkar Yojana (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
7% interest subvention on working capital loans up to ₹2 Lakh for women handloom weavers to purchase yarns, modern looms, and traditional weaving accessories.

## Benefit
7% interest rebate on bank loans up to ₹2 Lakh, enabling women weavers to produce traditional Gale, Shawls, and tribal apparel without high interest burdens.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing woman handloom weaver or member of a weavers SHG in Arunachal Pradesh.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Pehchan Weaver ID / Artisan Card** (Office of Development Commissioner Handlooms): Proves genuine handloom weaver status.
- **APST Certificate and Aadhaar Card** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identification proof.

## How to Apply
### Assistant Director of Textile & Handicrafts (blockOffice)
Submit application with loom verification report.

### State Bank of India / Apex Bank Branch (bankBranch)
Bank branch disburses subsidized working capital loan.

## Caveats
Applicant must operate an active loin loom or frame loom and hold an artisan weaver card.

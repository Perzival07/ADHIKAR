# Lakshadweep Sea Livelihood & Tuna Modernization Support

## Summary
Subsidized distribution of modern tuna long-lining equipment, insulated fish transport boxes, solar copra dryers, and subsidized diesel for islanders.

## Benefit
60% capital subsidy on fishing gear, life-saving beacons, fish finders, and modern copra processing units to enhance island export earnings.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a native resident of Lakshadweep engaged in fishing or coconut processing.

- Region: Lakshadweep
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Lakshadweep Native Identity Card / ST Certificate** (Sub-Divisional Officer Kavaratti): Proves indigenous islander status.
- **Fisherman Registration Passbook** (Fisheries Officer): Confirms active fishing activity.
- **Bank Passbook with Syndicate / Canara Bank Kavaratti** (Bank Branch): Required for subsidy disbursement.

## How to Apply
### Fisheries Officer Office on Respective Island (blockOffice)
Submit subsidy application at your island administrative unit.
Link: https://lakshadweep.gov.in

### Lakshadweep UT Portal (online)
View island development circulars and scheme announcements.
Link: https://lakshadweep.gov.in

## Caveats
Restricted strictly to indigenous islander families residing on inhabited islands.

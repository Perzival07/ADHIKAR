# Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
100% fully funded professional coaching at top national institutes + annual residential accommodation stipend of ₹40,000 for competitive exams (UPSC, RPSC, JEE, NEET, CA, CLAT).

## Benefit
Full coaching course fees paid directly by state government to Allen, Resonance, Drishti, Chanakya, etc. + ₹40,000/year living stipend for students studying outside their hometown.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an SC, ST, OBC, EWS, or MBC student of Rajasthan with family income under ₹8 Lakh selected on Class 10/12 merit.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Age: 15–30
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jan Aadhaar Card** (Jan Aadhaar Authority): Mandatory for student profile creation.
- **Class 10th and 12th Board Marksheets** (RBSE / CBSE): Determines merit selection cutoff.
- **Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) and Income Certificate** (Tehsildar): Validates reservation and income limit.

## How to Apply
### SJED Anuprati Coaching Portal / SSO Rajasthan (online)
Apply online via sso.rajasthan.gov.in under SJED applications.
Link: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in

### Any e-Mitra Kiosk in Rajasthan (csc)
Upload documents and submit preference of coaching institute.

## Caveats
Selection is strictly based on marks obtained in Class 10th and 12th board examinations.

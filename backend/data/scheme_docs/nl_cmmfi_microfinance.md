# Chief Minister's Micro Finance Initiative (CMMFI Nagaland)

## Summary
Subsidized bank loans up to ₹15 Lakh with 3% interest subvention and 30% capital grant for farmers, SHGs, and youth setting up agri-allied, food processing, or MSME ventures.

## Benefit
30% capital subsidy from state government + 3% interest subvention on commercial bank loans up to ₹15 Lakh, with minimal margin money requirement.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous resident of Nagaland, farmer group, or entrepreneur setting up an enterprise.

- Region: Nagaland
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate (IIC) / ST Certificate** (Deputy Commissioner): Mandatory domicile.
- **Detailed Project Proposal and Cost Quotations** (Self / Consultant): Outlines capital investment and viability.
- **In-principle Bank Sanction Letter from Commercial Bank** (SBI / Nagaland Rural Bank / Apex Bank): Confirms loan financing readiness.

## How to Apply
### Deputy Commissioner Office / District Level Implementation Committee (DLIC) (blockOffice)
Submit project dossier to District Agriculture / Industries Officer.

### CMMFI Nagaland Finance Portal (online)
Track guidelines and approved ventures at finance.nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://finance.nagaland.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must be an indigenous Naga resident and maintain clean banking credit history.

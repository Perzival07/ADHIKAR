# Thalliki Vandanam Scheme (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Annual financial assistance of ₹15,000 directly transferred to the mother’s bank account for every school-going child from Class 1 to Intermediate (12th).

## Benefit
₹15,000 per academic year per child directly deposited into the mother’s bank account via DBT.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a mother residing in Andhra Pradesh sending your child to school with minimum 75% attendance.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mother's Aadhaar Card linked to active bank account** (UIDAI): Direct beneficiary for DBT.
- **Child Student ID (Child Info Code) & Aadhaar** (School Headmaster): Enrolment and attendance records.
- **Rice Card / White Ration Card** (Civil Supplies Dept): Income validation.

## How to Apply
### Government / Recognized School / Village Secretariat (panchayat)
School HM enters student attendance; Sachivalayam verifies mother KYC.
Link: https://cse.ap.gov.in

### AP CSE Student Info Portal (online)
Check eligibility status via child Aadhaar number.
Link: https://cse.ap.gov.in

## Caveats
Student must maintain minimum 75% school attendance during the academic term.

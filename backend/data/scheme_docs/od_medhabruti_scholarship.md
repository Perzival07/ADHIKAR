# Mukhyamantri Medhabruti Puraskar (Odisha)

## Summary
One-time direct cash award of ₹5,000 for top meritorious Class 10th (HSC) students in every Gram Panchayat and urban ward across Odisha.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹5,000 credited directly into the student’s personal savings account to celebrate academic achievement and encourage higher schooling.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student who ranked among the top 100 toppers in BSE Class 10th board exams in your Gram Panchayat.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 14–19
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **BSE Odisha 10th Board (HSC) Marksheet and Admit Card** (Board of Secondary Education (BSE) Odisha): Mandatory merit proof.
- **Certificate confirming residency in Gram Panchayat** (Gram Panchayat Executive Officer): GP allocation verification.
- **Student Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account in Odisha** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Odisha State Scholarship Portal (online)
Verify roll number and submit bank details at scholarship.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://scholarship.odisha.gov.in

### Headmaster Office of High School (panchayat)
School verifies GP topper list on state portal.

## Caveats
Up to 4 top students (2 boys and 2 girls) in each of the 6,798 Gram Panchayats receive the award.

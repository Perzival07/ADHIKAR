# Sikkim Krishi Aatmanirbhar Yojana (Organic Price Incentive)

## Summary
Direct cash price incentives and production subsidies for 100% organic certified produce including large cardamom, ginger, turmeric, buckwheat, and orange to assure farmers of fair prices.

## Benefit
Direct cash incentive per kilogram of produced and marketed organic crops credited directly to farmers bank accounts.

## Eligibility
You are a certified organic cultivator in Sikkim registered under the Organic Farming Mission.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Harvest season cycles)

## Documents Required
- **Organic Farmer Certificate / SOCCA Card** (Sikkim Organic Certification Agency): Certification of organic grower.
- **Land Record (Parcha)** (Land Revenue Dept): Confirms cultivated acreage.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct subsidy credit.

## How to Apply
### Assistant Director of Agriculture (AD Agriculture) / Block Farm Extension Office (blockOffice)
Submit harvest declaration and crop sale slip.

### Sikkim Agriculture Portal (online)
Check incentive price list at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Farms must be within Sikkim 100% Organic certified cluster zones.

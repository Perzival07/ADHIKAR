# Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹1,250 per month (₹15,000 per year) directly credited into the bank accounts of married, widowed, and abandoned women aged 21 to 60.

## Benefit
₹1,250 per month DBT credited on the 10th of every month directly to the woman’s personal Aadhaar-linked DBT account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a married, widowed, or divorced woman aged 21 to 60 residing in Madhya Pradesh with annual family income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 21–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Samagra Family ID and Individual Samagra ID** (Samagra Social Security Mission MP): Must have e-KYC completed.
- **Aadhaar Card with active mobile link** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit of ₹1,250.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat / Ward Office Camp Helpdesk (panchayat)
Camp operators fill online form and capture live photograph.

### CM Ladli Behna Portal (online)
Track application and monthly payment status at cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in.
Link: https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in

## Caveats
Family members must not be income tax payees or regular government employees, or own 5+ acres of land or a 4-wheeler (tractor exempt).

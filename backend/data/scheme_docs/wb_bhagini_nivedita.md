# Bhagini Nivedita Scheme (Higher Education Support for Women)

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹50,000 spread over 3 continuous years (₹17,000 in Year 1, ₹17,000 in Year 2, and ₹16,000 in Year 3) for unmarried women pursuing undergraduate higher education.

## Benefit
Total grant of ₹50,000 disbursed in 3 yearly tranches to eliminate financial roadblocks in completing college graduation.

## Eligibility
You are an unmarried female student pursuing undergraduate higher education in West Bengal.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 18–25
- Income ceiling: Low / Middle Income
- Gender: Female
- Education level: Undergraduate (BA / BSc / BCom / Professional)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Start of College Academic Year)

## Documents Required
- **College Bonafide Student Certificate & Admission Slip** (College Principal / Registrar): Confirms active undergraduate enrollment.
- **Self-Declaration / Certificate of Unmarried Status** (Self / Notary): Mandatory condition for scheme entitlement.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### College Nodal Desk / Principal Office (blockOffice)
Colleges verify and forward student dossiers to Higher Education Directorate.

### West Bengal State Higher Education Portal (online)
Review eligibility and application notifications at wbsche.wb.gov.in.
Link: https://wbsche.wb.gov.in

## Caveats
Student must maintain continuous regular attendance verified by the college principal.

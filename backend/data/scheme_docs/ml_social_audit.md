# Meghalaya Community Participation and Public Services Social Audit

## Summary
Pioneering statutory grassroots social audit framework guaranteeing citizen entitlements, public service timelines, and 100% transparency across all 21 government departments.

## Benefit
Guaranteed time-bound redressal of delayed pensions, subsidies, ration delivery, and school supplies backed by the Meghalaya Social Audit Act 2017.

## Eligibility
You qualify as any resident citizen of Meghalaya accessing welfare schemes and public services in your village.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **EPIC Voter ID / Aadhaar Card** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Confirms village residence.

## How to Apply
### Village Social Audit Public Hearing (Gram Sabha / Dorbar Shnong) (panchayat)
Voice entitlement issues directly before Social Audit Resource Persons.

### MSSAT Official Portal (online)
Inspect village social audit report cards at mssat.nic.in.
Link: https://mssat.nic.in

## Caveats
Any citizen can raise grievances or report missing scheme payouts directly during Gram Sabha social audits.

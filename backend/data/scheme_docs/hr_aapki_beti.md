# Aapki Beti Hamari Beti Scheme (Haryana)

## Summary
One-time investment of ₹21,000 deposited in LIC in the name of the first girl child of SC/BPL families, and second/third girl child of all families in Haryana.

## Benefit
₹21,000 fixed deposit certificate maturing into ₹1 Lakh+ when the girl child turns 18 years of age and remains unmarried.

## Eligibility
You qualify as the parent of a girl child born in Haryana under the priority eligibility category.

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 0–18
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Girl Child's Official Birth Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths Haryana): Mandatory birth proof in Haryana.
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Mandatory for child registration.
- **Child Immunization / Mamata Card** (Health Sub-Centre): Proves institutional immunization.

## How to Apply
### Antyodaya Saral Portal Haryana (online)
Apply online under WCD services at saralharyana.gov.in.
Link: https://saralharyana.gov.in

### Anganwadi Worker / Primary Health Centre (PHC) (panchayat)
Submit physical application within 1 year of delivery.

## Caveats
Application must be submitted within 1 year of girl child’s birth through Anganwadi or Saral portal.

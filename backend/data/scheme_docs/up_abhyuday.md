# Mukhyamantri Abhyuday Yojana (Free Competitive Coaching)

## Summary
Free physical classroom coaching, digital study modules, and tablet distribution for economically weaker students preparing for UPSC, UPPSC, JEE, NEET, NDA, and CDS examinations.

## Benefit
Free expert classroom lectures, library access, mentorship by civil servants, and free digital study tablets for meritorious aspirants.

## Eligibility
You are a student preparing for competitive examinations residing in Uttar Pradesh.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 17–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 12th Pass / Graduate
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Annual Selection Cycle)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card with UP Address** (UIDAI): Identity and residency verification.
- **Educational Certificates (12th / Degree)** (Board / University): Academic eligibility for target exam.

## How to Apply
### Divisional Abhyuday Center / District Social Welfare Officer (blockOffice)
Attend physical lectures after qualifying screening exam.

### Abhyuday Official Portal (online)
Register for entrance test at abhyuday.up.gov.in.
Link: https://abhyuday.up.gov.in

## Caveats
Selection for physical classroom centers based on online screening entrance test.

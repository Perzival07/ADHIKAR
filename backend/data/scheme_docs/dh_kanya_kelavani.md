# Saraswati Vidya Mandir & Kanya Kelavani Grant (DNH & DD)

## Summary
100% free school textbooks, stationery, uniforms, bicycles, and full tuition fee waiver for tribal and underprivileged girl students through college.

## Benefit
Free bicycles for girls entering Class 8 + complete fee reimbursement for undergraduate and professional polytechnic courses.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female student enrolled in a government or aided school/college in DNH & DD.

- Region: Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu
- Age: 12–22
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School / College Bonafide Certificate** (Head of Institution): Proves active enrollment in DNH & DD.
- **Domicile / Resident Certificate** (Mamlatdar Office): Confirms UT residence.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Government School / College Principal Office (panchayat)
Entitlements distributed directly through educational institution.
Link: https://daman.nic.in

### UT Administration Daman & Diu Official Portal (online)
View welfare scheme orders and guidelines.
Link: https://daman.nic.in

## Caveats
Student must maintain minimum 75% school attendance.

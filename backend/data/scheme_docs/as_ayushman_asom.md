# Ayushman Asom (Mukhya Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana)

## Summary
Cashless medical treatment cover of ₹5 Lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across all ration-card holding families in Assam.

## Benefit
Cashless hospitalisation up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year covering diagnostic tests, surgical care, medicines, and ICU support across empanelled hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Assam possessing an active NFSA Ration Card.

- Region: Assam
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **NFSA Ration Card** (Food, Civil Supplies Dept): Primary identity for family coverage.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for hospital authorization.

## How to Apply
### Ayushman Asom Beneficiary Portal (online)
Download digital Ayushman Asom Card with Ration Card number.
Link: https://aaas-mmy.assam.gov.in

### Hospital Pradhan Mantri Arogya Mitra Kiosk (csc)
Direct biometric verification at network hospital.

## Caveats
Ration card must be Aadhaar-seeded for all family members.

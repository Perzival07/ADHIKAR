# Mizoram Handloom Weavers Financial Support Scheme

## Summary
Subsidized yarns, free modern fly-shuttle frame looms, and working capital grants for women weaving traditional Mizo Puan (Puanchei, Ngotekherh) garments.

## Benefit
Free frame loom set (worth ₹25,000) + 50% subsidized cotton/silk yarn distribution + direct participation in state handloom expos (Puan Expo).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a woman handloom weaver in Mizoram weaving traditional Mizo Puan apparel.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Weaver Identity Card / Pehchan Card** (Office of DC Handlooms / Industries Dept): Proves genuine artisan status.
- **ST Certificate / Aadhaar Card** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch in Mizoram): Required for DBT of yarn grant.

## How to Apply
### District Commerce & Industries Centre (DCIC) (blockOffice)
Submit application with weaver card.

### Mizoram Industries Portal (online)
Track handloom schemes at industries.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://industries.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must operate an active loom and possess an artisan/weaver card.

# Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹1.20 Lakh in plains and ₹1.30 Lakh in hilly/difficult areas for constructing durable pucca houses for homeless and kutcha-house rural families.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹1,20,000 (plains) or ₹1,30,000 (NE/hilly states) in 3 construction tranches + 90 days of MGNREGA wages (~₹25,000) + ₹12,000 Swachh Bharat toilet assistance.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a rural houseless family or living in zero/one/two-room kutcha house verified under Awaas+ list.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card of household head and spouse** (UIDAI): Mandatory for Aadhaar-seeded DBT.
- **MGNREGA Job Card** (Gram Panchayat): Required to claim unskilled labour component of 90-95 days.
- **Bank Passbook / DBT Account** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct transfer of construction tranches.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat & Gram Sabha Resolution (panchayat)
Verify inclusion in Awaas+ permanent waitlist via Panchayat Secretary.

### AwaasSoft Portal (online)
Monitor registration status, geotagging photos, and tranche release.
Link: https://pmayg.nic.in

## Caveats
Family must not own a motorized 4-wheeler, mechanized agri-equipment, or have regular government employment.

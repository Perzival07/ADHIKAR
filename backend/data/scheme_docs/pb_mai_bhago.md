# Mai Bhago I-Stree Shakti Scheme (Punjab)

## Summary
Micro-credit loans up to ₹50,000 at nominal interest rates through Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS) to strengthen rural women’s self-help groups.

## Benefit
Concessional credit up to ₹50,000 per member without mortgage security to establish tailoring, dairy, pickle making, and handicraft micro-enterprises.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an active member of a rural Women Self Help Group in Punjab linked to a cooperative society.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Women SHG Passbook and Member Record** (PACS / Cooperative Society): Proves active group membership.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Individual Savings Account Passbook** (Central Cooperative Bank in Punjab): Required for loan disbursement.

## How to Apply
### Primary Agricultural Cooperative Society (PACS) / Cooperative Bank (panchayat)
Apply through PACS secretary with group resolution.

### Punjab State Portal (online)
Track cooperative development circulars at punjab.gov.in.
Link: https://punjab.gov.in

## Caveats
Group must hold regular monthly meetings and maintain transparent savings ledger.

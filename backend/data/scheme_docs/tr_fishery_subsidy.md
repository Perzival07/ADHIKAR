# Mukhyamantri Matsya Vikas Yojana (Tripura Pisciculture Support)

## Summary
Subsidized distribution of quality fish fingerlings, supplementary fish feed, and 50% capital subsidy on new pond excavation to expand inland freshwater fish production.

## Benefit
50% financial subsidy for new aquaculture pond construction + high-quality fingerling seed kits at subsidized rates.

## Eligibility
You are a fish farmer or waterbody owner in Tripura engaged in commercial freshwater pisciculture.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Pre-monsoon stocking period)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRTC)** (SDM Office): Proof of state residency.
- **Waterbody Land Record (Khatian / Parcha) or Lease Deed** (Revenue Dept): Proof of pond ownership or minimum 5-year lease.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct subsidy credit.

## How to Apply
### Fishery Officer, Block Level Office / Superintendent of Fisheries (blockOffice)
Submit pond dimension survey and fingerling indent.

### Tripura Fisheries Department Portal (online)
Check seed availability at fisheries.tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://fisheries.tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Waterbody must be minimum 0.2 acres and free from legal encumbrance.

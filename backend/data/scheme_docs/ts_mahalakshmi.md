# Mahalakshmi Scheme (Telangana)

## Summary
Comprehensive women welfare scheme delivering ₹2,500 monthly DBT financial assistance, subsidized LPG cylinders at ₹500, and 100% free bus travel for women in TSRTC express and city ordinary buses.

## Benefit
₹2,500 per month DBT + ₹500 subsidized cooking gas cylinder + unlimited free travel across Telangana in state RTC buses with zero bus pass fee.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a permanent female resident of Telangana with Praja Palana registration.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: BPL / Underprivileged
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card with Telangana Address** (UIDAI): Proof of identity and state residence.
- **Praja Palana Application Acknowledgment** (Mandal Revenue Office): Registration under Six Guarantees.
- **Active Domestic LPG Consumer Number** (Oil Marketing Company): For ₹500 gas cylinder subsidy DBT.

## How to Apply
### Any TSRTC City Ordinary / Palle Velugu / Express Bus (blockOffice)
Show Aadhaar card to conductor to obtain zero-fare pink ticket.

### Praja Palana Portal (online)
Track Six Guarantees status at prajapalana.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://prajapalana.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Free bus travel requires showing Telangana domicile Aadhaar / voter card to bus conductor for zero-fare ticket.

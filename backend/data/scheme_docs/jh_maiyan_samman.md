# Jharkhand Mukhyamantri Maiyan Samman Yojana (JMMSY)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹1,000 per month (₹12,000 per year) directly credited into the bank accounts of women aged 18 to 50 across Jharkhand.

## Benefit
₹1,000 per month DBT credited on the 15th of every month directly to the beneficiary woman’s personal bank account with zero fee.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female permanent resident of Jharkhand aged 18 to 50 holding an active Aadhaar and ration card.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jharkhand Ration Card (Yellow / Pink / Green Card)** (Food Dept Jharkhand): Mandatory family matching document.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch / Pragya Kendra): Direct monthly credit of ₹1,000.

## How to Apply
### Special Camp at Panchayat Bhawan / Pragya Kendra (CSC) (csc)
Submit form with Aadhaar and bank details at camp.

### JMMSY Official Portal (online)
Track registration and monthly payment status at mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in.
Link: https://mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in

## Caveats
Family members must not be regular government employees or income tax payees. Age relaxed to 18-50.

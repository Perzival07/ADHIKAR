# Punjab Free Domestic Electricity (300 Units Scheme)

## Summary
Zero electricity bills for domestic residential consumers consuming up to 300 units of electricity per month (600 units for bi-monthly billing cycles) across Punjab.

## Benefit
100% waiver of electricity charges, fuel surcharge, and government duty, resulting in ₹0 bill for households consuming within 600 units per bi-monthly bill.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a domestic residential electricity consumer in Punjab with a connected load of up to 7 kW.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **PSPCL Electricity Account Number (Contract Account)** (PSPCL): Found on monthly electricity bill.
- **Aadhaar Card of Consumer** (UIDAI): Identity linkage.

## How to Apply
### PSPCL Consumer Online Services Portal (online)
Verify bill and consumption history at pspcl.in.
Link: https://pspcl.in

### PSPCL Sub-Division Office / Suvidha Kendra (blockOffice)
Check meter reading and zero-bill status.

## Caveats
Applies strictly to domestic residential meters. If consumption exceeds 600 units in a 2-month cycle, full bill is charged (relaxed for SC/BC/BPL families).

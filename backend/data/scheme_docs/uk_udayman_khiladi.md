# Mukhyamantri Udayman Khiladi Unnayan Yojana (Sports Scholarship)

## Summary
Monthly sports scholarship of ₹1,500 disbursed to 3,900 budding young athletes (equal boys and girls) aged 8 to 14 years across all 13 districts to support diet, sports gear, and coaching.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,500 per month (total ₹18,000 per year) credited directly to the young athlete’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You are a school sports athlete aged 8 to 14 years residing in Uttarakhand who qualified the physical fitness battery tests.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 8–14
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School (Age 8-14)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Annual Selection Trials)

## Documents Required
- **Birth Certificate / School Age Certificate (Age 8 to 14)** (School Board / Municipality): Proof of qualifying age bracket.
- **Domicile Certificate (Mool Niwas)** (Tahsildar / SDM): Proof of Uttarakhand domicile.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct monthly scholarship transfer.

## How to Apply
### District Sports Stadium / District Sports Officer (DSO) (blockOffice)
Participate in open district physical trial battery tests.

### Uttarakhand Sports Department Portal (online)
Check trial schedules and results at sports.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://sports.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Athlete must clear mandatory battery of physical fitness tests (speed, agility, endurance).

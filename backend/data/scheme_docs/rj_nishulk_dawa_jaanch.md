# Mukhyamantri Nishulk Dawa & Jaanch Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
100% free essential medicines (1,800+ drugs) and 100% free diagnostic laboratory tests (up to 133 tests) in all government medical college hospitals, CHCs, and PHCs.

## Benefit
Zero cost for all outpatient and inpatient medicines, surgical sutures, implants, MRI, CT scan, sonography, X-rays, and pathology tests in government healthcare institutions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as any patient receiving medical treatment at any government healthcare institution across Rajasthan.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Government Hospital OPD Slip and Doctor Prescription** (Government Hospital MO): Prescription for medicines and tests.
- **Jan Aadhaar Card (Optional for e-Aushadhi record)** (DOIT&C): Used for digital prescription tracking.

## How to Apply
### Government Hospital Pharmacy (Nishulk Dawa Vitran Kendra) (blockOffice)
Present OPD slip at counter to collect medicines free of cost.

### e-Aushadhi Rajasthan Portal (online)
Check medicine availability in district hospitals at health.rajasthan.gov.in.
Link: https://health.rajasthan.gov.in

## Caveats
Medicines and tests must be prescribed by a government medical officer on the institutional OPD slip.

# T-PRIDE (Telangana Program for Rapid Incubation of Dalit Entrepreneurs)

## Summary
Special industrial incentive package providing 35% capital investment subsidy (up to ₹75 Lakhs), 100% stamp duty reimbursement, and 9% interest subsidy for SC/ST and Differently-Abled entrepreneurs.

## Benefit
35% capital subsidy on plant, machinery and transport vehicles + ₹1.50 per unit power tariff rebate for 5 years.

## Eligibility
You are an SC, ST, or Differently-Abled entrepreneur setting up a micro/small industry or commercial vehicle unit in Telangana.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Integrated Community Certificate (SC / ST / PwD Certificate)** (Revenue Dept via Meeseva): Proof of affirmative category.
- **Udyam Registration Certificate** (Ministry of MSME): Formal MSME recognition.
- **Bank Term Loan Sanction Letter & Quotations** (Scheduled Commercial Bank): Proves investment financing.

## How to Apply
### General Manager, District Industries Centre (DIC) (blockOffice)
Submit DPR for scrutiny and subsidy sanction.

### TS-iPASS Single Window System (online)
Apply for incentives online at ipass.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://ipass.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant enterprise must hold Udyam Registration and TS-iPASS single window clearance.

# Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarojgar Yojana (MMYSY UP)

## Summary
Bank loans up to ₹25 Lakhs for industrial manufacturing units and up to ₹10 Lakhs for service sector enterprises with a 25% margin money capital subsidy for educated unemployed youth.

## Benefit
25% margin money subsidy (up to ₹6.25 Lakh for industry, ₹2.5 Lakh for service) which converts into a grant after 2 years of successful business operation.

## Eligibility
You are an educated unemployed resident of UP aged 18 to 40 with minimum High School (Class 10) pass.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th Pass or Above
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **High School (10th) Marksheet & Certificate** (UP Board / CBSE / ICSE): Proof of minimum education qualification.
- **UP Domicile Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra)** (Tahsildar via eDistrict): Proof of state residence.
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** (Self / Consultant): Business financials and machinery estimates.

## How to Apply
### District Industries Centre (DIC) / General Manager Office (blockOffice)
Appear before DLC interview after online application.

### UP MSME Portal (online)
Submit project proposal at diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in.
Link: https://diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must have passed at least Class 10 (High School) and not defaulted with any bank.

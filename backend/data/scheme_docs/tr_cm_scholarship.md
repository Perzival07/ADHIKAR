# Chief Minister Special Scholarship for Meritorious Students

## Summary
Annual scholarship of ₹1,00,000 for top-ranking students of Tripura Board (TBSE) and CBSE schools in Class 10 and 12 who secure admission to IITs, AIIMS, IIMs, and premier national institutions.

## Benefit
Direct financial stipend of ₹1,00,000 per academic year throughout the duration of the undergraduate professional degree course.

## Eligibility
You are a permanent resident student of Tripura who secured admission into a top-tier national institution.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 17–24
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Undergraduate (IIT / AIIMS / NIT / Central Univ)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Start of College Academic Session)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate of Tripura (PRTC)** (Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)): Proof of Tripura domicile.
- **Seat Allotment Letter & Fee Receipt (IIT/AIIMS/NIT)** (JOSAA / Central Counselling Board): Proof of admission into recognized premier institute.
- **Student Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Directorate of Higher Education, Shiksha Bhavan, Agartala (blockOffice)
Submit application with admission letter and PRTC.

### Tripura Higher Education Portal (online)
Apply online at highereducation.tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://highereducation.tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Student must possess Permanent Resident Certificate of Tripura (PRTC).

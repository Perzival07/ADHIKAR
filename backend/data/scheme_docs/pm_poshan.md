# PM POSHAN (Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman)

## Summary
Nutritious hot cooked meals provided free of cost every school day to over 11.80 crore children studying in Classes 1 to 8 across 11.20 lakh government schools.

## Benefit
Free hot cooked midday meal providing minimum 450 calories and 12g protein (Primary) and 700 calories and 20g protein (Upper Primary) + fortified rice and iron-folic acid tablets.

## Eligibility
Your child qualifies automatically as an enrolled student in Class 1 to 8 in any government or local body school.

- Region: All India
- Age: 6–14
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School Admission Record / Student ID** (Government School Headmaster): Enrolment automatically activates daily midday meal.
- **Child's Aadhaar Card (Optional for APAAR ID)** (UIDAI): Used to create APAAR student registry ID.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary / Upper Primary School (panchayat)
Automatic entitlement upon school admission.
Link: https://pmposhan.education.gov.in

### PM POSHAN Monitoring Portal (online)
Inspect school nutrition menus and social audit reports.
Link: https://pmposhan.education.gov.in

## Caveats
Coverage is universal for all children enrolled in eligible schools without any income ceiling.

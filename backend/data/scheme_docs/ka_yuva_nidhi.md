# Yuva Nidhi Scheme (Karnataka)

## Summary
Monthly unemployment stipend of ₹3,000 for degree holders and ₹1,500 for diploma holders for up to 2 years to support youth seeking employment.

## Benefit
Monthly allowance of ₹3,000 (Graduates) or ₹1,500 (Diploma holders) credited directly via DBT for up to 24 months or until securing employment/higher studies.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a graduate or diploma holder from Karnataka who remained unemployed 6 months after passing.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 20–30
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Degree / Diploma Passing Certificate** (Recognized University / DTE Karnataka): Proves qualifying graduation.
- **Karnataka Domicile / 6-Year Study Certificate** (School / College Principal / BEO): Proves 6 years of education in Karnataka.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Seva Sindhu Yuva Nidhi Portal (online)
Submit application and self-declaration at sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in.
Link: https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in

### Gram One / Karnataka One Center (csc)
Biometric registration and document submission.

## Caveats
Candidate must be domiciled in Karnataka for at least 6 years. Monthly self-declaration of unemployment required.

# Mo Ghara Scheme (Credit-Linked Housing Scheme Odisha)

## Summary
Credit-linked housing loan subsidy of up to ₹60,000 on housing loans up to ₹3 Lakh for lower and lower-middle class rural households to build or expand pucca homes.

## Benefit
Capital subsidy of ₹30,000 (loan ₹1 Lakh), ₹45,000 (loan ₹1.5 Lakh), ₹60,000 (loan ₹2-3 Lakh) + 1-year loan repayment moratorium.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a rural household in Odisha living in a kutcha house with monthly family income below ₹25,000.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Record of Rights (RoR / Patta) for Homestead Land** (Tahasildar / Bhulekh Odisha): Proves clear title to homestead plot.
- **Monthly Income Certificate under ₹25,000** (Revenue Inspector / Tahasildar): Validates economic eligibility.
- **Aadhaar Card of Applicant and Family Head** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Mo Ghara Odisha Portal (online)
Submit loan and subsidy application at rhodisha.gov.in/moghara.
Link: https://rhodisha.gov.in

### Commercial / Regional Rural / Cooperative Bank Branch (bankBranch)
Bank sanctions subsidized loan after technical inspection.

## Caveats
Family must not have previously availed full assistance under PMAY-G or Biju Pucca Ghar.

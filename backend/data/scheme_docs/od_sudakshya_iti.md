# Sudakshya Scheme for Girl Students in ITIs (Odisha)

## Summary
100% free ITI technical education, free hostel accommodation, uniforms, shoes, learning materials, and ₹1,500 per month maintenance stipend for girl students in government ITIs.

## Benefit
Zero admission and tuition fees + free hostel lodging and mess allowance + ₹1,500/month stipend credited via DBT to encourage girls in technical and engineering trades.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female student enrolled in any engineering or non-engineering trade in a Government ITI in Odisha.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 14–30
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: Diploma / ITI
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Government ITI Admission Allotment Slip** (Government ITI Principal): Proves active enrollment in trade.
- **Class 10th (Matriculation) Pass Marksheet** (BSE Odisha / Recognized Board): Validates educational qualification.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Account in Odisha** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly ₹1,500 stipend DBT.

## How to Apply
### Government ITI Principal Office / Training Officer Desk (panchayat)
Entitlements granted automatically on admission.

### DTET Odisha Skill Portal (online)
Apply for ITI admission at skill.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://skill.odisha.gov.in

## Caveats
Girl student must maintain minimum 75% attendance in theory and practical ITI workshops.

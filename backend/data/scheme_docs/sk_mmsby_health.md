# Mukhya Mantri Swasthya Bima Yojana (MMSBY Sikkim)

## Summary
Universal state health insurance providing cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalization coverage up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year across empanelled hospitals.

## Benefit
Cashless hospitalization coverage of ₹5 Lakh per annum per family covering serious ailments, diagnostics, and surgeries.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a permanent resident family of Sikkim holding a Sikkim Subject / COI.

- Region: Sikkim
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Certificate of Identification (COI)** (District Collectorate): Proof of state residency.
- **State Ration Card / Family Card** (Food & Civil Supplies Dept): Family unit verification.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Individual identity authentication.

## How to Apply
### MMSBY Kiosk at District Hospitals / STNM Gangtok (csc)
Biometric registration and health golden card generation.

### Sikkim State Health Portal (online)
Access hospital network details at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Family must be enrolled and issued an MMSBY Health Card.

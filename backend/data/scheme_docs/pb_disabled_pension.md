# Punjab Financial Assistance to Disabled Persons

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹1,500 per month and free assistive mobility aids for persons with benchmark physical or intellectual disabilities (50%+).

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,500 credited directly into the disabled citizen’s bank account + free motorized tricycles, hearing aids, and artificial limbs.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a person residing in Punjab holding a valid disability certificate showing 50%+ disability with income under ₹60,000.

- Region: Punjab
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Disability Certificate (50%+) / UDID Card** (Civil Surgeon / Medical Board): Mandatory medical certification.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹60,000)** (Tehsildar / Sewa Kendra): Income criteria validation.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch in Punjab): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Sewa Kendra Punjab / District Social Security Officer (DSSO) (csc)
Submit application with medical board certificate.

### e-Sewa Punjab Portal (online)
Track pension disbursement at connect.punjab.gov.in.
Link: https://socialsecurity.punjab.gov.in

## Caveats
UDID (Unique Disability ID) card is accepted for fast-track processing.

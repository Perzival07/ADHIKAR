# Mukhyamantri Kanya Aatmanirbhar Yojana (Tripura Free Bicycles)

## Summary
Free bicycles distributed to all female students who successfully pass Class 8 and enroll in Class 9 in government and government-aided schools to eliminate mobility barriers and curb girl dropouts.

## Benefit
Free durable bicycle provided directly to each eligible school girl student at the beginning of Class 9.

## Eligibility
You are a female student enrolled in Class 9 in a Tripura government or government-aided school.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 13–17
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School (Class 9)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Start of Academic Session)

## Documents Required
- **Class 8 Pass Certificate & Marksheet** (School Headmaster): Proves progression to secondary schooling.
- **Class 9 Government School Enrollment Card** (School Headmaster): Confirms active student status.
- **Aadhaar Card of Student** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Government High / Higher Secondary School Headmaster Desk (blockOffice)
Bicycle delivered directly through the school distribution roster.

### Tripura School Education Portal (online)
Access program notifications at tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Student must maintain regular attendance verified by the school headmaster.

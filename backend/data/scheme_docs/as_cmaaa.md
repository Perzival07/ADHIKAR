# Chief Minister’s Atmanirbhar Asom Abhijan (CMAAA)

## Summary
Seed capital assistance of ₹2 Lakh (for graduates/ITIs) and ₹5 Lakh (for professional degree holders) with 50% government grant and 50% interest-free loan for youth startups.

## Benefit
₹2 Lakh assistance (₹1 Lakh grant + ₹1 Lakh interest-free loan) or ₹5 Lakh assistance (₹2.5 Lakh grant + ₹2.5 Lakh interest-free loan) + 1-month enterprise training.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed educated resident of Assam aged 28 to 40 holding required educational qualification.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 28–40
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Employment Exchange Registration Card** (District Employment Exchange Assam): Mandatory registration proof.
- **Degree / Diploma / ITI Pass Certificate** (Recognized University / Board): Determines eligible funding category.
- **Bank Passbook and PAN Card** (Bank Branch / IT Dept): Required for disbursement.

## How to Apply
### CMAAA Official Portal (online)
Register online and upload business concept paper at cmaaa.assam.gov.in.
Link: https://cmaaa.assam.gov.in

### District Industries & Commerce Centre (DICC) (blockOffice)
Attend DLTFC interview and mandatory incubation.

## Caveats
Applicant must be registered with the Employment Exchange in Assam before the cut-off date.

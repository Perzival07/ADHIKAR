# Rythu Bima Scheme (Telangana Farmers Group Life Insurance)

## Summary
Comprehensive group life insurance providing ₹5,00,000 financial compensation directly to the nominated family member within 10 days of the demise of an enrolled farmer aged 18 to 59 years.

## Benefit
100% state-paid premium life insurance providing ₹5,00,000 direct death claim payout to nominee bank accounts without any delay.

## Eligibility
You are a landholder farmer aged 18 to 59 years possessing a valid Pattadar Passbook in Telangana.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 18–59
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Annual August Enrollment Cycle)

## Documents Required
- **Pattadar Passbook (Dharani Portal Record)** (Revenue Dept): Valid agricultural title document.
- **Nominee Aadhaar Card & Bank Passbook** (Bank / UIDAI): Required for instant claim settlement.
- **Death Certificate of Farmer (for claim)** (Panchayat Secretary / Registrar): Required only at claim submission.

## How to Apply
### Village Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO) / Rythu Vedika (blockOffice)
Submit nominee declaration form during village camp.

### Rythu Bima Portal (online)
Check insurance enrollment and policy receipt at rythubima.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://rythubima.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Farmer must be enrolled with nominee declaration during the annual August renewal window.

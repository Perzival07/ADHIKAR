# Mukhyamantri Amrutam (MA / MAA Vatsalya Scheme Gujarat)

## Summary
Universal cashless hospitalisation coverage of up to ₹10 Lakhs per family per year across 650+ empanelled government and private hospitals for serious illnesses.

## Benefit
100% cashless medical care up to ₹10 Lakh per family per year covering cardiology, oncology, neurosurgery, burns, poly-trauma, kidney transplant, and ICU.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Gujarat holding a BPL card or with annual family income up to ₹4 Lakh.

- Region: Gujarat
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **MA / MAA Vatsalya Card / Ayushman PM-JAY Gujarat Card** (Health Dept Gujarat): Primary identification for admission.
- **Income Certificate under ₹4 Lakh** (Mamlatdar): Economic criteria proof.
- **Aadhaar Card of patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for hospital authorization.

## How to Apply
### Civic Centre / Taluka Kiosk / Empanelled Hospital Helpdesk (csc)
Visit MA kiosk with income certificate for instant card issuance.

### MA Gujarat Official Portal (online)
Check hospital empanelment and procedure packages at magujarat.com.
Link: https://magujarat.com

## Caveats
Card covers all declared family members including senior citizen parents and newborn infants.

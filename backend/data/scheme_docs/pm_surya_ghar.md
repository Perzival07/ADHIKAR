# PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

## Summary
Direct capital subsidy of up to ₹78,000 for installing rooftop solar panels, providing up to 300 units of free electricity every month to 1 crore households.

## Benefit
Capital subsidy of ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above rooftop solar systems + collateral-free loans at ~7%.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a residential electricity consumer with a suitable roof for solar panel installation.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Latest Electricity Bill** (Power Distribution Company (DISCOM)): Consumer Account Number must be clear.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Must match name on electricity account.
- **House Ownership Proof / Tax Receipt** (Municipal / Gram Panchayat): Proves ownership of rooftop space.

## How to Apply
### National Portal for PM Surya Ghar (online)
Apply online, choose registered vendor, and submit bank details at pmsuryaghar.gov.in.
Link: https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in

### Local Electricity DISCOM Office / CSC (csc)
Visit DISCOM sub-division for net meter application and technical feasibility.

## Caveats
Electricity connection must be in applicant name and domestic residential tariff.

# Maitri & Manasvini Schemes (Karnataka)

## Summary
Direct monthly pension of ₹1,200 per month for transgender citizens (under Maitri) and unmarried destitute or deserted women (under Manasvini) across Karnataka.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,200 directly credited to the beneficiary’s bank account to support dignified self-reliant living.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a transgender person or unmarried/deserted woman aged 40+ residing in Karnataka with family income under ₹32,000.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Transgender Identity Certificate / Deserted Woman Certificate** (District Screening Committee / Tahsildar): Mandatory statutory proof.
- **Income Certificate** (Nadakacheri): Validates economic criteria.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account Passbook** (Bank Branch in Karnataka): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Nadakacheri / Seva Sindhu Portal (online)
Apply online via nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in.
Link: https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in

### Atalji Janasnehi Kendra (AJK) / Tahsildar Office (panchayat)
Submit application for social security order.

## Caveats
Transgender applicants must produce certificate issued by the District Transgender Identity Board / CMO.

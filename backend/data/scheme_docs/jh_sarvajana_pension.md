# Jharkhand Mukhyamantri Sarvajana Pension Yojana

## Summary
Universal monthly social security pension of ₹1,000 per month for all citizens aged 50+ (for SC/ST) and 60+ (for others), destitute widows, and disabled persons without BPL cap.

## Benefit
Direct benefit transfer of ₹1,000 per month directly into beneficiary’s bank account on the 5th of every month with zero quota restrictions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Jharkhand aged 50+ (SC/ST) or 60+ (others) not receiving other government pensions.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 50+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Non-taxpayer)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID showing 50+ for SC/ST or 60+)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Validates relaxed age threshold.
- **Caste Certificate (for SC/ST 50-year eligibility)** (Circle Officer): Required to claim age relaxation from 50.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Jharkhand** (Bank Branch / Pragya Kendra): Direct monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat Secretary / Block Development Office (BDO) (panchayat)
Submit application form to Panchayat Secretary.

### SSPIS Jharkhand Social Security Portal (online)
Apply online via sspis.jharkhand.gov.in.
Link: https://sspis.jharkhand.gov.in

## Caveats
Age threshold reduced to 50 years specifically for SC and ST residents and women.

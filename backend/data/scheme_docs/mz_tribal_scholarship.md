# Mizoram Special Scholarship Scheme for Tribal Students

## Summary
Full tuition fee waiver, maintenance allowances, and book grants for indigenous Mizo students pursuing post-matric, degree, and higher professional education.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer covering 100% compulsory non-refundable college course fees + annual maintenance stipend of up to ₹15,000 for hostellers.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous Scheduled Tribe student of Mizoram enrolled in an accredited post-matric/degree course.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 16–28
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mizoram Scheduled Tribe Certificate** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): Mandatory ST verification.
- **College Admission Fee Receipt and Bonafide Certificate** (College Principal): Proves active enrollment and fee structure.
- **Student Aadhaar-linked Bank Account in Mizoram** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### National Scholarship Portal (NSP) (online)
Apply online under Mizoram ST Post-Matric scheme at scholarships.gov.in.
Link: https://scholarships.gov.in

### College Nodal Scholarship Officer Desk (panchayat)
Submit verification copy to college principal.

## Caveats
Family annual income must not exceed ₹2.5 Lakh per annum.

# Chief Minister's Krishi Rinn Yojana (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Additional 4% interest subvention on Kisan Credit Card (KCC) crop loans up to ₹3 Lakh, making farm credit 100% zero-interest for farmers repaying promptly.

## Benefit
Zero percent effective interest on agricultural crop loans up to ₹3 Lakh (State provides 4% subsidy in addition to Central 3% prompt repayment incentive).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing farmer or self-help group in Arunachal Pradesh availing crop loans via KCC.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Possession Certificate (LPC) / Cultivation Certificate** (Circle Officer / ADC): Confirms cultivable land in Arunachal.
- **Kisan Credit Card Account Passbook** (SBI / Apex Bank / Arunachal Rural Bank): Required for interest subsidy crediting.
- **APST Certificate and Aadhaar Card** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identification.

## How to Apply
### Arunachal Pradesh Rural Bank / Apex Bank / SBI (bankBranch)
Apply for KCC crop loan with Circle Officer Land Possession Certificate.

### Arunachal Agriculture Portal (online)
View guidelines and district targets.
Link: https://agri.arunachal.gov.in

## Caveats
Loan must be repaid within the stipulated 12-month repayment period.

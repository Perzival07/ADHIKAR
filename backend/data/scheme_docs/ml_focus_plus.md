# FOCUS / FOCUS+ (Farmers Collectivization for Upscaling Production, Meghalaya)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹5,000 per farming household (FOCUS+) and ₹5,000 per producer group member (FOCUS) for seed procurement, farm inputs, and value addition.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹5,000 credited directly into the farmer’s bank account + community investment fund grants up to ₹5 Lakh for registered Producer Groups (PGs).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing farmer or member of an active Producer Group in Meghalaya.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Producer Group (PG) Passbook / FOCUS ID** (MBMA / Enterprise Facilitation Centre): Proves membership in registered group.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Meghalaya** (Bank Branch / Meghalaya Rural Bank): Required for ₹5,000 direct credit.

## How to Apply
### Enterprise Facilitation Centre (EFC) at Block Office / Village Headman (panchayat)
Register through Village Community Facilitator (VCF).

### FOCUS Meghalaya Official Portal (online)
Track beneficiary payout status at focus.meghalaya.gov.in.
Link: https://focus.meghalaya.gov.in

## Caveats
Farmer must be enrolled in an active Producer Group facilitated by the local Enterprise Facilitation Centre (EFC).

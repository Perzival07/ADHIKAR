# Chief Minister’s Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS)

## Summary
Universal secondary and tertiary cashless hospitalization insurance covering up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year across 1,700+ empanelled government and private hospitals.

## Benefit
Cashless treatment up to ₹5,00,000 per family per annum covering 1,513 medical procedures, surgeries, and diagnostics.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident family of Tamil Nadu with annual income under ₹1.2 Lakh (or Smart Ration Card holder).

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Income ceiling: < 1.2 Lakh (or Smart Card)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Smart Ration Card** (Civil Supplies Dept): Primary eligibility document.
- **Income Certificate (< ₹1.2 Lakh/year)** (Tahsildar / Revenue Dept): Verifies annual household income.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### District Collectorate CMCHIS Enrolment Centre (blockOffice)
Biometric enrolment and immediate card generation.

### CMCHIS Official Portal (online)
Search empanelled hospitals and package rates at cmchistn.com.
Link: https://www.cmchistn.com

## Caveats
Patient must present CMCHIS Smart Card / Smart Ration Card at hospital insurance desk.

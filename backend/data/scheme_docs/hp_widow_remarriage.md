# Himachal Pradesh Widow Remarriage Scheme

## Summary
Direct rehabilitation grant of ₹65,000 provided to a widow upon her dignified legal remarriage, transferred directly to her savings account.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹65,000 paid to the remarried woman to support social dignity, household establishment, and livelihood stability.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a widowed woman residing in Himachal Pradesh who has legally remarried.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Former Husband's Death Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Proves prior widowhood.
- **Remarriage Registration Certificate** (Marriage Registrar / Tehsildar): Mandatory legal registration proof.
- **Himachal Bonafide Certificate** (Tehsildar): Permanent residence proof.

## How to Apply
### Tehsil Welfare Officer (TWO) / CDPO Office (blockOffice)
Submit application with marriage registration certificate.

### e-District Himachal Portal (online)
Apply online via edistrict.hp.gov.in.
Link: https://edistrict.hp.gov.in

## Caveats
Application must be submitted within 2 years of the date of solemnization of remarriage.

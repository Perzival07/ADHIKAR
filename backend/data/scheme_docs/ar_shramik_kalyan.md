# Chief Minister's Shramik Kalyan Yojana (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Comprehensive social welfare benefits including ₹1,000 to ₹10,000 maternity assistance, educational stipends for children, and accidental insurance for registered workers.

## Benefit
Maternity assistance up to ₹10,000, children education scholarships up to ₹20,000/yr, marriage assistance of ₹50,000, and accidental death coverage of ₹4 Lakh.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a registered building or construction worker holding an active APBOCWWB passbook.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **APBOCWWB Registered Worker Passbook** (Registering Officer / Labour Officer): Proves active membership and contribution.
- **90 Days Construction Work Certificate** (Registered Contractor / Engineer): Mandatory statutory proof.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT benefit payouts.

## How to Apply
### District Labour Officer / APBOCWWB Branch (blockOffice)
Submit claim form with original passbook for stamping.

### APBOCWWB Online Portal (online)
Check registration and annual renewal status.
Link: https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in

## Caveats
Worker must have completed minimum 90 days of construction work in the preceding 12 months.

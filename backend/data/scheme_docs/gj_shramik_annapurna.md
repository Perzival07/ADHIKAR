# Shramik Annapurna Yojana (Gujarat)

## Summary
Nutritious, hot cooked wholesome full meals (roti, sabzi, dal, rice) provided at a nominal price of only ₹5 per meal for registered construction workers at Kadia Nakas.

## Benefit
Hygienic, high-protein hot cooked lunch meal at ₹5 per plate distributed from specialized food delivery booths located at major labour gathering nakas.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a registered building and other construction worker holding an active e-Nirman card in Gujarat.

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **e-Nirman Card (Gujarat BOCW Worker ID)** (GBOCWWB / Labour Dept): Scanned at counter to receive ₹5 meal token.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Worker identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Any Shramik Annapurna Booth at Kadia Naka (blockOffice)
Scan e-Nirman card at counter and collect lunch.
Link: https://bocw.gujarat.gov.in

### e-Nirman Gujarat Labour Portal (online)
Register for e-Nirman smart card online.
Link: https://bocw.gujarat.gov.in

## Caveats
Worker must display digital or physical e-Nirman Card or e-Shram Card at the food counter.

# Ladli Laxmi Yojana 2.0 (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Cumulative financial assurance of ₹1.43 Lakh: school scholarships from Class 6 to 12 + ₹25,000 for college admission + final lump sum of ₹1 Lakh upon turning 21 unmarried.

## Benefit
Staged education scholarships (₹2,000 in 6th, ₹4,000 in 9th, ₹6,000 in 11th & 12th) + ₹25,000 in two installments for college degree admission + ₹1 Lakh at age 21.

## Eligibility
You qualify as the parent of a girl child born in Madhya Pradesh registered under the Ladli Laxmi Act.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 0–21
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Non-taxpayer)
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Ladli Laxmi Registration Certificate** (WCD Dept MP): Mandatory tracking certificate.
- **Samagra Member ID** (Samagra Mission MP): Matches state citizen database.
- **College Admission Allotment (for ₹25,000 grant)** (Admitting University / College): Required for higher education grant.

## How to Apply
### Ladli Laxmi 2.0 Official Portal (online)
Download digital certificate and claim scholarship at ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in.
Link: https://ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in

### Anganwadi Centre / Project Office (panchayat)
Submit documents to Anganwadi worker.

## Caveats
Girl must be registered within 1 year of birth at Anganwadi center; family must not be income tax payees.

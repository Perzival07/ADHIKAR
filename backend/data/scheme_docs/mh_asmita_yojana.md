# Asmita Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
Subsidized sanitary napkins at only ₹5 per pack (8 pads) for rural adolescent schoolgirls (Classes 6-12), and ₹24 for rural women distributed through Self-Help Groups.

## Benefit
Distribution of high-quality hygienic biodegradable sanitary napkins via Asmita App to adolescent girls studying in Zilla Parishad schools at nominal ₹5/pack.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an adolescent girl student enrolled in Class 6 to 12 in a rural government/ZP school in Maharashtra.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 11–19
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Asmita Card / Student ID** (ZP School Headmaster): Issued through school portal.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Zilla Parishad School / Village UMED SHG Kendra (panchayat)
Collect sanitary pack directly from teacher using Asmita Card.

### UMED Asmita Portal (online)
Track school quotas and distribution at umed.in/asmita.
Link: https://umed.in

## Caveats
Students receive digital Asmita Cards generated by school headmaster on Asmita portal.

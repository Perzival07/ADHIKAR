# Delhi Ladli Scheme

## Summary
Staged financial assistance of ₹11,000 to ₹35,000 deposited in the name of girl children from birth through Class 12 to promote girl education and arrest dropouts.

## Benefit
Financial milestone deposits (₹11,000 at institutional birth, ₹5,000 on school admission, and ₹5,000 each in classes 6, 9, 10, 12) maturing with interest when girl turns 18.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a girl child born or studying in Delhi with family annual income under ₹1 Lakh.

- Region: Delhi (NCT)
- Age: 0–18
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **3-Year Delhi Residence Proof** (Election Commission / Food Dept): Proves continuous 3-year stay in NCT of Delhi.
- **Girl Child's Birth Certificate** (MCD / NDMC): Proves birth in Delhi.
- **Income Certificate under ₹1 Lakh** (Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)): Must be currently valid.

## How to Apply
### e-District Delhi Ladli Service (online)
Register through eDistrict Delhi or through school coordinator.
Link: https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in

### WCD District Office / School Principal (blockOffice)
Submit form to school vice-principal or district WCD desk.

## Caveats
Family must have resided in Delhi for at least 3 years prior to application.

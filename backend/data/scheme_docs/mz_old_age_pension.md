# Mizoram Old Age Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹500 (aged 60-79) and ₹1,000 (aged 80+) per month for destitute senior citizens residing in Mizoram.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹500 to ₹1,000 directly credited into the elderly citizen’s savings bank account with zero fee.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 60 or older residing in Mizoram with family income under ₹24,000.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Certificate (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Church Certificate)** (Election Commission / Church Authority): Must confirm age 60 or older.
- **Village Council Poverty Certificate** (Village Council President): Confirms low income status.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Mizoram** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) / Village Council Office (panchayat)
Submit application form through Village Council President.

### Social Welfare Mizoram Portal (online)
Track pension lists at socialwelfare.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must not be a retired government employee or draw any statutory family pension.

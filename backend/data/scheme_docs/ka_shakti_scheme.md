# Shakti Scheme (Free Bus Travel for Women in Karnataka)

## Summary
100% free bus travel for all women, girl students, and transgender persons domiciled in Karnataka across all state transport corporation buses.

## Benefit
Zero-cost bus travel across ordinary and express intra-state buses operated by KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, and KKRTC with no distance limits within Karnataka.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female or transgender resident of Karnataka presenting a valid government photo identity card.

- Region: Karnataka
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Government Photo ID showing Karnataka address (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Driving License)** (Govt of India / Karnataka): Shown to conductor to get zero-fare ticket.

## How to Apply
### Any KSRTC / BMTC / NWKRTC / KKRTC Regular or Express Bus (panchayat)
Show photo ID to conductor on boarding to receive Shakti zero-fare ticket.

### Seva Sindhu Shakti Smart Card Portal (online)
Apply for Shakti Smart Card.
Link: https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in

## Caveats
Valid for intra-state travel within Karnataka; luxury/AC buses (Airavat, Flybus, Rajahamsa) are excluded.

# Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal (Bihar)

## Summary
Free treated piped tap water supply connection to every household across rural gram panchayats and urban wards in Bihar.

## Benefit
Free installation of pipeline, domestic water meter, tap fitting, and daily supply of chlorinated safe potable drinking water at doorstep.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident household in Bihar residing in an uncovered or habitated ward.

- Region: Bihar
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Ration Card / Aadhaar Card / Electricity Consumer No.** (Civil Supplies / DISCOM): Validates residence in ward.

## How to Apply
### Ward Implementation & Management Committee (WIMC) (panchayat)
Contact Ward Member / Mukhiya to report pipeline connection.
Link: https://prdbihar.gov.in

### Bihar Panchayati Raj e-Grievance Portal (online)
Register tap water complaints or connection requests.
Link: https://prdbihar.gov.in

## Caveats
Community connection is provided 100% free with zero installation charge.

# Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
Direct benefit transfer of ₹1,500 per month (₹18,000 per year) directly credited into the bank accounts of women aged 21 to 65 years with family income below ₹2.5 Lakh.

## Benefit
₹1,500 per month DBT credited directly into the woman’s personal Aadhaar-seeded bank account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female resident of Maharashtra aged 21 to 65 with annual family income below ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 21–65
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Maharashtra Domicile Certificate / Ration Card (Yellow/Orange) / 15-Year Voter ID** (Revenue Dept Maharashtra / Election Commission): Confirms state domicile.
- **Aadhaar Card with linked mobile number** (UIDAI): Identity verification and e-KYC.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit of ₹1,500.

## How to Apply
### Majhi Ladki Bahin Official Portal & Nari Shakti Doot App (online)
Apply online at ladkibahin.maharashtra.gov.in.
Link: https://ladkibahin.maharashtra.gov.in

### Anganwadi Sevika / Gram Panchayat / Setu Kendra Helpdesk (panchayat)
Submit form to local Anganwadi worker.

## Caveats
Yellow or Orange Ration card holders are exempt from submitting a separate income certificate. 4-wheeler owners excluded (tractors exempt).

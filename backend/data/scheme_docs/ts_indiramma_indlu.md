# Indiramma Indlu Housing Scheme

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹5,00,000 provided in phased construction tranches to homeless families possessing a residential plot to construct a quality pucca house.

## Benefit
₹5 Lakh direct grant released in stages linked to foundation, lintel, roof, and finishing milestones.

## Eligibility
You are a homeless family in Telangana owning a plot of land and registered in Praja Palana.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 21+
- Income ceiling: BPL / Underprivileged
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Residential Plot Patta / Registered Title Deed** (Revenue / Registration Dept): Proof of plot ownership.
- **Praja Palana Application Number** (Mandal Office): Confirms scheme enumeration.
- **Aadhaar Card of Female Head** (UIDAI): House sanctioned preferably in female head name.

## How to Apply
### Gram Panchayat / Ward Secretariat / MPDO Office (panchayat)
Field verification by Housing Assistant and geotagging of plot.

### Telangana Housing Portal (online)
Track stage-wise fund release at housing.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://housing.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
House construction must strictly adhere to state engineering specifications with geotagged inspection.

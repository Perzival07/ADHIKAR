# LIFE Mission (Livelihood Inclusion and Financial Empowerment Kerala)

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹4 Lakh for homeless families who own land to construct a durable pucca house, and free apartment housing units for landless-houseless families.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹4 Lakhs in 4 construction tranches for individual house construction, or allotment of fully constructed 2-BHK apartment unit in LIFE housing complex.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a homeless family residing in Kerala verified on the LIFE Mission priority beneficiary list.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Kerala Ration Card (Non-Priority / Priority)** (Civil Supplies Dept): Validates family composition.
- **Land Title Deed / Possession Certificate (for ₹4L grant)** (Village Officer / Sub-Registrar): Proves ownership of house plot.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch in Kerala): Direct transfer of housing tranches.

## How to Apply
### LIFE Mission Portal Kerala (online)
Verify inclusion in published ward priority list at lifemission.kerala.gov.in.
Link: https://lifemission.kerala.gov.in

### Grama Panchayat / Municipality Helpdesk (panchayat)
Submit geo-tagged house plan and documents.

## Caveats
Family members must not own a pucca house anywhere in India.

# Phulo Jhano Ashirwad Abhiyan (Jharkhand)

## Summary
Direct interest-free livelihood seed fund of ₹10,000 and counseling to support women traditionally engaged in country liquor trade to transition into dignified micro-enterprises.

## Benefit
₹10,000 interest-free initial capital grant + integration into Sakhi Mandal (SHG) micro-finance loans up to ₹50,000 for grocery, poultry, or dairy businesses.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a woman residing in Jharkhand formerly or currently engaged in traditional country liquor brewing seeking dignified alternative livelihood.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Sakhi Mandal (SHG) Membership Record** (JSLPS Community Cadre): Validates SHG linkage.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Account** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Village Organisation (VO) / Sakhi Mandal Cadre (panchayat)
Contact local Community Resource Person (CRP) or JSLPS field coordinator.

### JSLPS Official Portal (online)
View livelihood success stories and contacts.
Link: https://jharkhand.gov.in

## Caveats
Candidate is provided complete social dignity and livelihood support with zero legal harassment.

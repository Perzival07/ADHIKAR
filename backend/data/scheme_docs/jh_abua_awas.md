# Jharkhand Abua Awas Yojana

## Summary
3-room pucca house financial assistance of ₹2 Lakhs in 4 installments (plus 90-95 days of MGNREGA wages and Swachh toilet) for homeless and kucha house rural families.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹2 Lakhs in 4 construction tranches (Foundation ₹30k, Lintel ₹50k, Roof ₹1 Lakh, Completion ₹20k) + ₹25,000 MGNREGA wages + ₹12,000 toilet grant.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a rural houseless family or living in a kutcha house in Jharkhand not covered under PM-Awas.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jharkhand Priority Ration Card** (Food Dept): Confirms economic vulnerability.
- **MGNREGA Job Card** (Gram Panchayat): Required for claiming unskilled wage component.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch / Pragya Kendra): Direct transfer of housing tranches.

## How to Apply
### Aapki Yojana Aapki Sarkar Aapke Dwar Camp / Gram Panchayat (panchayat)
Submit application to Panchayat Secretary.

### Abua Awas Monitoring Portal (online)
Track geotagged construction progress and tranche releases.
Link: https://aay.jharkhand.gov.in

## Caveats
Priority given to Primitive Tribal Groups (PVTG), families with disabled members, and disaster-affected households.

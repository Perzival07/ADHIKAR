# Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy Maternity Benefit Scheme

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹18,000 disbursed in 5 cash installments plus 2 Amma Nutrition Kits worth ₹4,000 for pregnant mothers to ensure institutional deliveries and optimal child nutrition.

## Benefit
₹18,000 total DBT cash assistance + 2 Amma Nutrition Kits containing iron syrup, protein powder, nutrition mix, and ghee.

## Eligibility
You are an expectant mother registered under PICME in Tamil Nadu delivering in government health facilities.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 19+
- Income ceiling: BPL / Underprivileged
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (First 12 weeks of pregnancy)

## Documents Required
- **PICME Registration Number / RCH ID** (Village Health Nurse (VHN) / PHC): Mandatory maternal tracking ID.
- **Aadhaar Card of Mother** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account Passbook** (Bank): For direct cash transfer.

## How to Apply
### Primary Health Centre (PHC) / Urban PHC / Village Health Nurse (blockOffice)
Register pregnancy with VHN before 12 weeks to initiate PICME schedule.

### PICME Web Portal (online)
Track instalment release at picme.tn.gov.in.
Link: https://picme.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Registration on PICME portal with Village Health Nurse (VHN) is mandatory.

# Assam Divyangjan Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly disability pension of ₹1,000 per month for persons with benchmark physical or intellectual disabilities (40%+) residing in Assam.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,000 credited directly into the disabled citizen’s bank account + free mobility appliances.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a person residing in Assam holding a valid disability certificate showing 40%+ disability.

- Region: Assam
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Disability Certificate (40%+) / UDID Card** (District Medical Board): Mandatory medical certification.
- **Domicile / PRC Certificate of Assam** (Circle Officer): Proves Assam residency.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Office (DSWO) Assam (blockOffice)
Submit application with medical board certificate.

### Social Welfare Portal Assam (online)
Track pension disbursement and apply online.
Link: https://socialwelfare.assam.gov.in

## Caveats
UDID card registration is accepted for instant direct processing.

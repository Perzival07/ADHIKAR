# YSR Pension Kanuka (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Enhanced monthly social security pension of ₹3,000 to ₹10,000 per month delivered at the citizen’s doorstep on the 1st of every month for seniors, widows, and disabled.

## Benefit
Monthly cash pension of ₹3,000 (Old Age, Widow, Weavers, Toddy Tappers, Single Women) and up to ₹6,000-₹10,000 (Disabled and Chronic Kidney Disease patients) delivered on 1st of month.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen (60+), widow, disabled person (40%+ disability), or chronic patient residing in AP.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Age Certificate (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Date of Birth)** (UIDAI / Election Commission): Must prove age 60+ for OAP.
- **SADAREM Disability Certificate (for Divyang pension)** (Medical Board AP): Must show 40%+ permanent disability.
- **Rice Card / White Ration Card** (Civil Supplies Dept): Economic eligibility verification.

## How to Apply
### Grama / Ward Sachivalayam (Village / Ward Secretariat) (panchayat)
Submit application to Welfare and Education Assistant.
Link: https://sspensions.ap.gov.in

### SSPensions AP Portal (online)
Track application and monthly biometric payout records.
Link: https://sspensions.ap.gov.in

## Caveats
Family landholding must not exceed 3 acres wetland / 10 acres dry land; family must not own a 4-wheeler vehicle.

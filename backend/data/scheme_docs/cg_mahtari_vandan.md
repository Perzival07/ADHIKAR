# Mahtari Vandan Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
Direct bank transfer of ₹1,000 per month (₹12,000 per year) directly credited to married, widowed, and abandoned women aged 21 and above across Chhattisgarh.

## Benefit
₹1,000 per month credited directly to the Aadhaar-linked bank account of every eligible woman on the first week of every month.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a married, widowed, or abandoned woman aged 21+ residing permanently in Chhattisgarh with annual family income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 21+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card of Applicant and Spouse** (UIDAI): Mandatory for family linkage and DBT.
- **Marriage Certificate / Ration Card with Spouse Name** (Gram Panchayat / Food Dept): Proves married / marital status.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Direct credit of ₹1,000/mo.

## How to Apply
### Mahtari Vandan Portal & Mobile App (online)
Check registration and monthly payment status.
Link: https://mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in

### Anganwadi Centre / Gram Panchayat Secretary Desk (panchayat)
Submit physical application through Anganwadi worker.

## Caveats
Applicant or family members must not be income tax payees or regular government employees.

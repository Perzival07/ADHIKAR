# Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah / Nikah Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹55,000 for mass marriage (Samuhik Vivah) of daughters belonging to poor, destitute, and widowed families across MP.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹49,000 directly into the bride’s bank account to establish household assets + ₹6,000 for event and ceremony arrangements.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an adult bride residing in Madhya Pradesh belonging to an economically weaker family solemnizing marriage in mass ceremony.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Samagra Member ID of Bride and Groom** (Samagra Mission MP): Matches state citizen database.
- **Age Proof of Bride (18+) and Groom (21+)** (10th Marksheet / Birth Cert / Voter ID): Verifies legal marriageable age.
- **Bride’s Individual Bank Account with IFSC in MP** (Bank Branch in MP): Required for ₹49,000 direct credit.

## How to Apply
### Janpad Panchayat / Municipal Corporation Office (panchayat)
Register application at least 15 days before mass wedding date.

### Kanya Vivah MP Portal (online)
Check approved mass marriage calendar at kanyavivah.mp.gov.in.
Link: https://kanyavivah.mp.gov.in

## Caveats
Bride must have completed 18 years and groom must have completed 21 years of age.

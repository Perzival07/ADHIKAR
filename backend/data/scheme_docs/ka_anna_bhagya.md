# Anna Bhagya Scheme (DBT Cash-in-Lieu of Rice)

## Summary
Direct Benefit Transfer of ₹170 per person per month (₹34/kg for 5 kg) transferred directly into bank accounts in lieu of additional 5 kg rice for Antyodaya and BPL cardholders.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹170 per registered beneficiary per month (e.g. ₹680/month for a 4-member family) + 5 kg free Central NFSA food grains.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a member of an active Antyodaya (AAY) or Priority Household (BPL) ration card in Karnataka with seeded bank account.

- Region: Karnataka
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Active BPL / Antyodaya Ration Card** (Food & Civil Supplies Dept): Mandatory eligibility card.
- **Aadhaar Card of Family Head** (UIDAI): NPCI bank mapping required.

## How to Apply
### Ahara Karnataka Food Portal (online)
Check monthly DBT cash transfer status using Ration Card number.
Link: https://ahara.kar.nic.in

### Fair Price Shop (PDS Dealer) / Gram One Center (panchayat)
Verify Aadhaar biometric seeding on ration database.

## Caveats
Head of household bank account must be actively seeded with Aadhaar on NPCI mapping.

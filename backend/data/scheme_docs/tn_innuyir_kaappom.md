# Innuyir Kaappom - Nammai Kaakkum 48 (Emergency Accident Care)

## Summary
Free emergency medical treatment up to ₹1,00,000 for any road accident victim across Tamil Nadu within the critical first 48 hours in 680+ empanelled government and private hospitals, regardless of state of origin.

## Benefit
100% cashless emergency trauma stabilization, surgery, ICU care, and diagnostics up to ₹1 Lakh per person for the first 48 hours.

## Eligibility
Any victim of a road traffic accident occurring within the geographic territory of Tamil Nadu.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Immediate Trauma Emergency)

## Documents Required
- **Accident Incident Record / 108 Ambulance Handover Sheet** (EMRI 108 / Hospital Emergency Desk): No documentation required for initial emergency stabilization.

## How to Apply
### Nearest Empanelled Trauma Centre / Hospital Emergency Ward (blockOffice)
Direct emergency admission via 108 ambulance dispatch.

### Innuyir Kaappom Portal (online)
View 680+ empanelled hospital network at innuyirkaappom.tn.gov.in.
Link: https://innuyirkaappom.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Universal emergency coverage for 81 designated life-saving trauma procedures.

# Tamil Pudhalvan Scheme (Higher Education Assistance for Boys)

## Summary
Monthly financial stipend of ₹1,000 provided to male students who studied in government schools from Classes 6 to 12 and enroll in undergraduate degree, engineering, polytechnic, or ITI programs.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,000 per month to buy textbooks, uniforms, and meet educational expenses throughout the course duration.

## Eligibility
You are a male student who studied in government schools from Class 6 to 12 and currently enrolled in higher education in Tamil Nadu.

- Region: Tamil Nadu
- Age: 17–25
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Male
- Education level: Undergraduate / Diploma / ITI
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Start of College Academic Year)

## Documents Required
- **EMIS Certificate of Govt Schooling (Class 6 to 12)** (Headmaster / School Education Dept): Verifies government school background.
- **College ID Card & Bonafide Letter** (College Principal): Confirms active college attendance.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account Passbook** (Bank): For direct monthly transfer.

## How to Apply
### College Scholarship Section / Nodal Officer (blockOffice)
Register during admission cycle through college nodal verification.

### Tamil Pudhalvan Portal (online)
Monitor registration status at tamilpudhalvan.tn.gov.in.
Link: https://tamilpudhalvan.tn.gov.in

## Caveats
Student must maintain active semester enrollment verified by the college registrar.

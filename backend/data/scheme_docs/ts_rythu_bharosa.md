# Rythu Bharosa (Telangana)

## Summary
Enhanced investment support of ₹15,000 per acre per year for landholding farmers and tenant farmers, alongside ₹12,000 per year for landless agricultural laborers.

## Benefit
₹15,000 per acre annually (₹7,500 for Kharif and ₹7,500 for Rabi) credited directly to farmers bank accounts through DBT.

## Eligibility
You are a farmer, tenant cultivator, or agricultural laborer in Telangana verified through land records.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Seasonal Sowing Cycles)

## Documents Required
- **Pattadar Passbook / Dharani Land Record** (Registration & Revenue Dept): Proof of cultivable agricultural land.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification and biometric seeding.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct investment credit.

## How to Apply
### Mandal Agriculture Officer (MAO) / Rythu Vedika (blockOffice)
Verify land ownership and crop survey details with Village Agriculture Extension Officer.

### Rythu Bharosa Portal (online)
Check disbursement status at rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Coverage includes tenant farmers registered through village revenue records.

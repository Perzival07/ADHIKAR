# Atal Ayushman Uttarakhand Yojana (AAUY Universal Health)

## Summary
Universal state health insurance providing 100% cashless hospitalization coverage up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year to all 27+ lakh resident families across government and empanelled private hospitals.

## Benefit
Cashless hospitalization coverage up to ₹5,00,000 per family per annum covering 1,750+ medical packages and procedures.

## Eligibility
You are a resident family of Uttarakhand possessing an AAUY Golden Card or State Ration Card.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Universal)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Uttarakhand Food & Civil Supplies Ration Card** (Food & Civil Supplies Dept): Family unit verification document.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### CSC Center / Ayushman Mitra Desk at Empanelled Hospital (csc)
Biometric e-KYC and immediate Golden Card printing.

### AAUY Official Portal (online)
Search empanelled hospitals and card status at ayushmanuttarakhand.org.
Link: https://ayushmanuttarakhand.org

## Caveats
Beneficiaries must produce their AAUY Golden Card / Aadhaar card at the hospital desk.

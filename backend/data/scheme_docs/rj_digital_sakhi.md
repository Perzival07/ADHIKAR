# Indira Gandhi Smartphone / Digital Sakhi Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
Free modern 4G/5G smartphone with 3 years of free voice calling and 20 GB monthly high-speed internet data for female heads of Chiranjeevi/MAA families.

## Benefit
Free smartphone (up to ₹6,800 purchase subsidy via e-wallet DBT) + free SIM card with 9 months upfront unlimited calling and 20 GB monthly data, extendable to 3 years.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female head of family enrolled in Chiranjeevi/MAA or student in government higher education.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jan Aadhaar Card of Female Head** (Jan Aadhaar Authority): Primary document for camp allotment.
- **Aadhaar Card with linked mobile** (UIDAI): Identity verification and e-KYC.
- **PAN Card of Female Head (or Form 60)** (Income Tax Dept): Required for wallet activation.

## How to Apply
### Special Digital Sakhi Camp at Block Headquarters / IT Kendra (csc)
Attend camp with Jan Aadhaar to collect smartphone and active SIM.

### IGSY Rajasthan Portal (online)
Check eligibility status using Jan Aadhaar number at igsy.rajasthan.gov.in.
Link: https://igsy.rajasthan.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must have Jan Aadhaar e-KYC linked with PAN card and mobile number.

# UP Nirashrit Mahila (Destitute Widow) Pension Yojana

## Summary
Monthly financial assistance of ₹1,000 (quarterly ₹3,000) credited directly to destitute widows aged 18 years and above residing in Uttar Pradesh.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,000 per month directly into the verified bank account of the destitute woman.

## Eligibility
You are a widowed woman residing in UP whose annual family income is under ₹2,00,000.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 2.0 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Husband’s Death Certificate** (Registrar of Births and Deaths): Mandatory widowhood proof.
- **Income Certificate** (Tahsildar via eDistrict): Income verification.
- **Aadhaar Card of Applicant** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct pension remittance.

## How to Apply
### CSC Jan Seva Kendra / District Probation Officer (csc)
Submit online form with death certificate and bank passbook.

### SSPY Pension Portal (online)
Apply online at sspy-up.gov.in.
Link: https://sspy-up.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must not be receiving any other government widow pension or re-married.

# YSR Sunna Vaddi (Zero Percent Interest Loans for SHGs)

## Summary
Complete reimbursement of bank interest on bank linkage loans up to ₹3 Lakh for Women SHGs repaying loans on time, making credit 100% interest-free.

## Benefit
Full bank loan interest amount reimbursed directly into SHG bank accounts via DBT, resulting in zero effective interest on micro-enterprise loans.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a member of a Women SHG repaying bank credit regularly without default.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **SHG Bank Loan Repayment Ledger** (Bank Branch): Proves timely repayment of monthly loan EMI.
- **SHG Registration & VO Code** (SERP / MEPMA): Validates group standing.

## How to Apply
### Village Secretariat Welfare Assistant (panchayat)
Automated through bank data sharing with SERP portal.
Link: https://navasakam2.ap.gov.in

### Lending Bank Branch (bankBranch)
Ensure timely EMI payment to trigger state interest reimbursement.

## Caveats
Loan must be standard asset with no NPA default.

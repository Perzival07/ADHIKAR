# Telangana Nutrition Kit Scheme (KCR / Telangana Poshan Kit)

## Summary
Nutritional intervention kits containing protein powder, iron tonics, dried dates, ghee, and maternal hygiene items distributed in 2 tranches to pregnant mothers to eliminate maternal anaemia.

## Benefit
2 high-nutrition kits valued at ₹2,000 each distributed during 2nd and 3rd antenatal checkups (ANC) at government health facilities.

## Eligibility
You are an expectant mother registered for antenatal care (ANC) in any Telangana government hospital.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (During ANC Checkups)

## Documents Required
- **MCH Mother and Child Health Card** (PHC / Area Hospital): Tracks mandatory ANC checkups.
- **Aadhaar Card of Mother** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Primary Health Centre (PHC) / Community Health Centre (CHC) (blockOffice)
Collect kit from medical officer during designated ANC checkup days.

### Commissioner of Health & Family Welfare Portal (online)
View scheme data at chfw.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://chfw.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Mother must undergo mandatory ANC blood and hemoglobin screening.

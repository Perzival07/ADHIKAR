# Mukhyamantri Samuhik Vivah Yojana (UP Collective Marriage Scheme)

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹51,000 per couple in grand collective marriages, including ₹35,000 direct bank transfer to the bride, ₹10,000 for wedding gifts, and ₹6,000 for event organization.

## Benefit
₹35,000 direct bank transfer to the bride + ₹10,000 worth of silver ornaments, bride clothing, utensils, and trunk + dignified grand wedding ceremony.

## Eligibility
You are a resident family in UP with annual income up to ₹2,00,000 marrying your daughter in a collective marriage.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 2.0 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Before scheduled ceremony date)

## Documents Required
- **Age Proof of Bride (18+) and Groom (21+)** (School Board / Aadhaar): Mandatory legal age verification.
- **Family Income Certificate (< ₹2 Lakh)** (Tahsildar via eDistrict): Income verification.
- **Bride’s Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Block Development Officer (BDO) / Urban Local Body Commissioner (blockOffice)
Submit application form for collective ceremony enrollment.

### CMSVY Official Portal (online)
Register online at cmsvy.upsdc.gov.in.
Link: https://cmsvy.upsdc.gov.in

## Caveats
Bride must be 18+ and groom must be 21+ at the date of wedding.

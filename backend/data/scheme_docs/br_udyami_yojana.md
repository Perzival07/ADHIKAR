# Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana (Bihar)

## Summary
Financial assistance of up to ₹10 Lakh (50% grant of ₹5 Lakh + 50% interest-free loan of ₹5 Lakh) for SC, ST, EBC, Mahila, and Yuva entrepreneurs to set up industries.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹10 Lakh in 3 tranches (₹5 Lakh grant + ₹5 Lakh loan repaid in 84 monthly installments with 0% interest for women/SC/ST/EBC and 1% for general youth).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Bihar aged 18 to 50 holding 10+2/ITI/Diploma or Degree setting up a manufacturing or service enterprise.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (Nivas Praman Patra)** (Circle Officer (CO) Bihar): Mandatory Bihar domicile.
- **Intermediate (10+2) / ITI / Polytechnic Diploma / Degree Certificate** (Recognized Board / University): Required minimum educational proof.
- **Current Bank Account Statement & Cancelled Cheque** (Bank Branch): Must be in the name of the enterprise.

## How to Apply
### Udyami Bihar Official Portal (online)
Submit project selection and registration at udyami.bihar.gov.in.
Link: https://udyami.bihar.gov.in

### District Industries Centre (DIC) Bihar (blockOffice)
Attend DPR appraisal and 2-week entrepreneurship training.

## Caveats
Applicant must have a Current Account opened in the name of the firm or sole proprietorship.

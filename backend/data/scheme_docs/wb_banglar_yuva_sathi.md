# Banglar Yuva Sathi (Unemployment Assistance Scheme)

## Summary
Direct monthly financial assistance of ₹1,500 per month via DBT for up to 5 continuous years (or until employment is secured) for unemployed youth aged 21 to 40 who have passed Class 10.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,500 per month directly into the verified bank account of the unemployed youth to support skill development and job search.

## Eligibility
You are a permanent resident of West Bengal aged 21 to 40, minimum Class 10 pass, and registered as unemployed.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 21–40
- Income ceiling: Unemployed / Underprivileged
- Education level: 10th Pass (Madhyamik) or Above
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Madhyamik (Class 10) Pass Admit Card & Marksheet** (WBBSE / Recognized Board): Proof of minimum education and age.
- **Employment Bank Enrolment Slip (EB ID)** (Labour Department WB): Mandatory registration proof on employmentbankwb.gov.in.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account Passbook** (Bank): For direct monthly remittance.

## How to Apply
### Duare Sarkar Camp / Sub-Divisional Employment Exchange (blockOffice)
Submit Annexure forms with Employment Bank enrolment slip.

### West Bengal Employment Bank Portal (online)
Register profile online at employmentbankwb.gov.in.
Link: https://wb.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must be enrolled in the West Bengal Employment Bank portal.

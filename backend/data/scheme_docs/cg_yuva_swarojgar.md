# Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarojgar Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
Bank loans up to ₹25 Lakh for manufacturing, ₹10 Lakh for service, and ₹2 Lakh for business with 25% margin money subsidy (up to ₹3.5 Lakh) for local youth.

## Benefit
25% margin money government subsidy (up to ₹3.5 Lakh for SC/ST/Women/Minority) credited to bank loan account after 2 years of successful business operation.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Chhattisgarh aged 18 to 35 (up to 40 for SC/ST/Women) with minimum 8th class pass qualification.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Educational Marksheet (Minimum 8th pass)** (School Authority): Verifies educational qualification.
- **Chhattisgarh Domicile Certificate** (Tehsildar): Mandatory residence proof.
- **Project Profile and Quotations** (Self / Consultant): Outlines machinery and working funds.

## How to Apply
### District Industries Centre (DIC) Chhattisgarh (blockOffice)
Submit project application to General Manager DIC.

### Industries CG Portal (online)
Track task force clearance and bank sanction.
Link: https://industries.cg.gov.in

## Caveats
Family annual income must not exceed ₹3 Lakh.

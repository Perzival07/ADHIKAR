# Chandigarh Social Security Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹1,000 per month for elderly citizens (60+), destitute widows, and persons with disabilities residing in UT of Chandigarh.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,000 directly credited to Aadhaar-seeded bank account with no intermediaries.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Chandigarh aged 60+ or widow/divorced woman with household income under ₹1.5 Lakh.

- Region: Chandigarh
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **3-Year Chandigarh Residence Proof** (Estate Office / Election Card): Proves continuous residence.
- **Income Certificate** (Sub Divisional Magistrate (SDM)): Income must be within limits.
- **Bank Passbook with IFSC** (Bank Branch in Chandigarh): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### e-Services Chandigarh Administration (online)
Apply online via services.chandigarh.gov.in.
Link: https://services.chandigarh.gov.in

### Sampark Centre Chandigarh (csc)
Submit physical documents at any neighborhood Sampark Centre.

## Caveats
Applicant must have resided continuously in Chandigarh for at least 3 years.

# Chief Minister-gi Sotharabasingi Tengbang (CMST Manipur)

## Summary
Direct caregiver financial allowance of ₹1,500 per month for persons with benchmark disabilities (40%+), plus annual education scholarships up to ₹8,000.

## Benefit
Monthly direct allowance of ₹1,500 credited via DBT + free motorized tricycles, hearing aids, and interest-free micro-enterprise soft loans up to ₹50,000.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a person with benchmark disability (40%+) residing in Manipur holding a valid disability card.

- Region: Manipur
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Disability Certificate (40%+) / UDID Card** (District Medical Board JNIMS / RIMS): Mandatory medical proof.
- **Resident / Domicile Certificate of Manipur** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): State domicile proof.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Manipur** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) Manipur (blockOffice)
Submit application with medical board certificate.

### Social Welfare Manipur Portal (online)
Track application status at socialwelfare.mn.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.mn.gov.in

## Caveats
Persons with profound disability (80%+) receive highest priority for caregiver allowance.

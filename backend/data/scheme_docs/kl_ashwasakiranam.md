# Ashwasakiranam Scheme (Caregiver Allowance Kerala)

## Summary
Monthly financial allowance of ₹600 per month for dedicated caregivers who look after bedridden, autistic, or mentally/physically challenged patients.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹600 per month credited directly to the caregiver’s bank account to support home palliative care assistance.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a full-time caregiver residing in Kerala tending to a completely bedridden or severely disabled patient.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Medical Certificate confirming Patient is Bedridden / 100% Disabled** (Medical Officer / Assistant Surgeon): Mandatory medical certification.
- **Income Certificate under ₹1 Lakh** (Village Officer): Income criteria proof.
- **Caregiver's Aadhaar-linked Bank Account** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Social Justice Department Kerala SUNEETHI Portal (online)
Apply online at suneethi.sjd.kerala.gov.in.
Link: https://socialjustice.kerala.gov.in

### District Social Justice Officer (DSJO) / Anganwadi Desk (blockOffice)
Submit physical copy with medical certificate.

## Caveats
Family annual income must not exceed ₹1 Lakh per annum.

# Vidya Lakshmi Bond Scheme (Gujarat)

## Summary
₹2,000 government savings bond deposited in the name of girl children enrolled in Class 1 in villages with low female literacy or urban BPL families, maturing after Class 8.

## Benefit
₹2,000 savings bond maturing with accrued interest upon the girl child passing Class 8 to incentivize continuous elementary schooling.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a girl child admitted to Class 1 in a village with female literacy below 35% or from an urban BPL family in Gujarat.

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 5–14
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Class 1 School Admission Record** (Primary School Principal): Issued during Shala Praveshotsav.
- **BPL Card / Village Domicile Proof** (Talati-cum-Mantri): Validates eligibility criteria.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary School Principal Desk (panchayat)
Bond awarded directly during Shala Praveshotsav.

### GSEB / Gujarat Education Portal (online)
Check scheme guidelines.
Link: https://gseb.org

## Caveats
Girl must complete Class 8 without dropping out to encash maturity value.

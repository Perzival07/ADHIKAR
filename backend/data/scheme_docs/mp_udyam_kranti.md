# Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Bank loans from ₹1 Lakh up to ₹50 Lakh for manufacturing and up to ₹25 Lakh for service/retail with 3% annual interest subsidy and 100% state credit guarantee (CGTMSE).

## Benefit
3% interest subsidy per annum for 7 years on bank loans up to ₹50 Lakh + 100% state bank guarantee fee reimbursement, eliminating requirement for personal mortgage collateral.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Madhya Pradesh aged 18 to 45 holding minimum 8th class pass qualification starting a new enterprise.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Non-taxpayer)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Samagra ID and Aadhaar Card** (Samagra Mission / UIDAI): Identity verification in MP.
- **Educational Marksheet (Minimum 8th standard pass)** (Recognized School): Educational threshold.
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR) and Machine Quotation** (Self / Chartered Accountant): Outlines capital and revenue projections.

## How to Apply
### Samast MP Online Portal (Udyam Kranti) (online)
Submit digital loan application at samast.mponline.gov.in.
Link: https://samast.mponline.gov.in

### Designated Lending Commercial Bank Branch (bankBranch)
Branch manager sanctions loan under state guarantee scheme.

## Caveats
Family members must not be income tax payees. Applies strictly to new greenfield business setups.

# Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
Financial assistance of ₹50,000 provided for the marriage of daughters from BPL and destitute families, including direct cash, household gifts, and ceremony arrangements.

## Benefit
Total grant of ₹50,000 (₹35,000 direct bank transfer to the bride + ₹10,000 worth of household utility items and clothes + ₹5,000 for mass wedding event arrangements).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a bride aged 18+ resident in Chhattisgarh belonging to an identified BPL/Antyodaya family.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **BPL / Antyodaya Ration Card** (Food Dept CG): Confirms economic poverty criteria.
- **Age Proof of Bride (18+) and Groom (21+)** (School Certificate / Aadhaar / Voter ID): Verifies legal marriage age.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook of Bride** (Bank Branch): Required for ₹35,000 cash DBT.

## How to Apply
### Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) / Anganwadi Centre (blockOffice)
Submit application at local Anganwadi center.

### WCD Chhattisgarh Portal (online)
Download registration form and event dates.
Link: https://wcd.cg.gov.in

## Caveats
Bride must be at least 18 years and groom at least 21 years of age on wedding date.

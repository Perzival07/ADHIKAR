# Haryana Solar Water Pumping Scheme (PM-KUSUM Component-B)

## Summary
75% capital subsidy on standalone solar water pumping systems (3 HP to 10 HP) for farmers to replace diesel pump sets and ensure clean irrigation.

## Benefit
75% subsidy (Central 30% + Haryana State 45%), requiring the farmer to pay only 25% of the total system cost for high-efficiency solar water pumps.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a farmer residing in Haryana holding agricultural land with micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler) setup.

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Mandatory for farmer verification.
- **Jamabandi / Fard Land Ownership Record** (Revenue Dept Haryana): Proves agricultural land possession.
- **Micro-Irrigation (Drip/Sprinkler) Verification Certificate** (MICADA Haryana): Mandatory prerequisite.

## How to Apply
### Antyodaya Saral Portal (HAREDA Solar Services) (online)
Apply online at saralharyana.gov.in when registration portal opens.
Link: https://saralharyana.gov.in

### Project Officer / Additional Deputy Commissioner Office (ADC) (blockOffice)
Deposit farmer share and complete site verification.

## Caveats
Installation of micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler) or underground pipeline is mandatory to qualify for 75% subsidy.

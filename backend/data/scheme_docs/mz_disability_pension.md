# Mizoram Disability Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹500 per month and free assistive mobility devices for persons with benchmark physical or intellectual disabilities (40%+).

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹500 credited directly into the disabled citizen’s bank account + free wheelchairs, crutches, and hearing aids.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a person residing in Mizoram holding a valid disability certificate showing 40%+ disability.

- Region: Mizoram
- Income ceiling: < 1.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Disability Certificate (40%+) / UDID Card** (District Medical Board Civil Hospital Aizawl): Mandatory medical certification.
- **Resident Certificate of Mizoram** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): Proves state residency.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch in Mizoram): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Office (DSWO) Mizoram (blockOffice)
Submit application with medical board certificate.

### Social Welfare Portal Mizoram (online)
Track pension disbursement at socialwelfare.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
UDID (Unique Disability ID) card is accepted for fast-track processing.

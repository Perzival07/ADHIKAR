# Swami Atmanand English Medium School Scheme (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
100% free high-quality English medium education with modern robotic labs, language laboratories, free textbooks, and digital libraries for underprivileged students.

## Benefit
Zero tuition fee, free bilingual textbooks, smart uniform grant, world-class sports facilities, and STEM lab education from Nursery to Class 12.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student residing in Chhattisgarh seeking admission to government English medium excellence schools.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Age: 3–18
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Child Birth Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Age verification for grade eligibility.
- **Parent Aadhaar / Ration Card / Electricity Bill** (UIDAI / Food Dept): Confirms residency in school locality.

## How to Apply
### Swami Atmanand Online Admission Portal (online)
Apply online at cgschool.in during open registration window.
Link: https://cgschool.in

### School Principal Desk (panchayat)
Check lottery results and submit original documents.

## Caveats
50% seats are reserved for girl students and priority is given to economically weaker sections.

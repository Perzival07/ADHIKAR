# Sabooj Sathi Scheme (Free Bicycles for Students)

## Summary
Free branded eco-friendly bicycles distributed to all boys and girls studying in Classes 9, 10, 11, and 12 across government, government-aided schools, and Madrasahs to empower student mobility.

## Benefit
Free durable bicycle fitted with reflective safety reflectors delivered directly to each student at school campus.

## Eligibility
You are a student studying in Class 9, 10, 11, or 12 in a West Bengal state-run or aided school.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 13–19
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School (Class 9-12)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Start of Academic Year)

## Documents Required
- **Banglar Shiksha Student ID Card** (School Headmaster): Unique student tracking ID on banglarshiksha.gov.in.
- **Class 9-12 School Enrollment Record** (School Headmaster): Confirms current schooling.

## How to Apply
### School Campus Distribution Ceremony (blockOffice)
Bicycles assembled and handed over directly inside school premises.

### Sabooj Sathi Portal (online)
Track bicycle consignment and student receipt at wbsaboojsathi.gov.in.
Link: https://wbsaboojsathi.gov.in

## Caveats
Universal distribution to all enrolled students without caste or income distinctions.

# Raita Vidya Nidhi Scholarship (Karnataka)

## Summary
Annual educational scholarship of ₹2,000 to ₹11,000 directly credited to bank accounts of children of farmers, agricultural labourers, weavers, and fishermen.

## Benefit
Direct bank transfer of ₹2,000 to ₹11,000 per academic year directly into student’s personal account through State Scholarship Portal (SSP).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student enrolled in post-matric/degree/professional college whose parent is a registered farmer/labourer in Karnataka.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 16–28
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parent FRUITS ID / Farmer Registration Number** (Agriculture Dept Karnataka): Validates farmer / landholding status.
- **College Admission Fee Receipt & Student ID** (College Principal): Enrolment proof in post-matric/degree course.
- **Student Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP) (online)
Apply online at ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in with FRUITS ID.
Link: https://ksp.karnataka.gov.in

### Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) (panchayat)
Generate parent FRUITS ID with land records.

## Caveats
Parent must be registered on the FRUITS (Farmer Registration and Unified Beneficiary Information System) portal.

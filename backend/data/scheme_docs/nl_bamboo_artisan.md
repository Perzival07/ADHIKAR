# Nagaland Bamboo Development Agency (NBDA) Artisan Support

## Summary
Free advanced toolkits worth ₹25,000, specialized master training in bamboo furniture, basketry, and bamboo charcoal, and participation in national craft expos.

## Benefit
Free specialized bamboo hand and power toolkits + ₹500/day training stipend during 2-week skill upgrading at Nagaland Bamboo Resource Centre (NBRC) Dimapur.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing bamboo/cane artisan or rural craftsman residing in Nagaland.

- Region: Nagaland
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Pehchan Artisan Card / Village Council Certificate** (Development Commissioner Handicrafts / VCC): Proves artisan status.
- **Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate and Aadhaar Card** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook in Nagaland** (Bank Branch): Required for stipend DBT.

## How to Apply
### Nagaland Bamboo Resource Centre (NBRC), 6th Mile, Sovima, Dimapur (blockOffice)
Apply for residential artisan training and toolkit.

### NBDA Nagaland Portal (online)
Download training forms at nbda.nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://nbda.nagaland.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must display existing manual bamboo crafting skills in baseline assessment.

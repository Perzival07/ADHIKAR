# Tripura Livestock Livelihood Mission (Piglet & Duckery Support)

## Summary
Free distribution of 3 exotic piglets or 20 ducklings along with feed, vaccination kits, and housing subsidies to rural tribal and BPL households to ensure supplementary livestock income.

## Benefit
Free livestock units (3 piglets or 20 ducklings) + initial balanced feed + veterinary medical care to build sustained household animal husbandry assets.

## Eligibility
You are a rural or tribal resident household of Tripura in need of supplementary livestock livelihood.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 1.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Seasonal Camp Cycles)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRTC)** (SDM Office): Proof of state residency.
- **Ration Card (BPL / Antyodaya)** (Food Dept): Confirms economic vulnerability.
- **Bank Account Passbook** (Bank): For any direct cash support.

## How to Apply
### Veterinary Officer / ARDD Block Dispensary (blockOffice)
Submit application form for inclusion in the village livestock distribution roster.

### Tripura ARDD Portal (online)
Access scheme schedules at ardd.tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://ardd.tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must have adequate pen or backyard shelter facility for livestock.

# Sona Sobhran Dhoti Saree Vitran Yojana (Jharkhand)

## Summary
Subsidized distribution of one quality saree and one dhoti/lungi at a nominal price of only ₹10 each twice every year to all BPL and Antyodaya families.

## Benefit
Distribution of 2 sarees and 2 dhotis/lungis per year at ₹10 per garment from Fair Price Shops (PDS ration dealers).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a priority household or Antyodaya cardholder in Jharkhand.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Active Jharkhand Ration Card (AAY / PHH)** (Food Dept): Biometric e-POS matching.
- **Aadhaar Card of Family Member** (UIDAI): e-POS fingerprint authentication.

## How to Apply
### Local Fair Price Shop (PDS Ration Dealer) (panchayat)
Visit dealer with ration card to collect garments at ₹10 each.

### Aahar Jharkhand Food Portal (online)
Verify dealer distribution quota.
Link: https://aahar.jharkhand.gov.in

## Caveats
Collected using biometric e-POS authentication at registered PDS dealer.

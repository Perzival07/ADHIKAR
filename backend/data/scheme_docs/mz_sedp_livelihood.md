# Mizoram SEDP (Socio-Economic Development Policy) Family Assistance

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹50,000 to ₹3 Lakh for trade, agriculture, piggery, and handloom enterprises to ensure self-reliant household economic development.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of financial grants and subsidized credit to selected families to establish commercially viable trades and micro-enterprises.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a permanent resident family of Mizoram verified under the SEDP family livelihood enumeration.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mizoram Scheduled Tribe Certificate / Domicile Certificate** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): Mandatory indigenous tribal proof.
- **Village Council / Local Council Recommendation Letter** (Village Council President (VCP)): Confirms resident status and trade suitability.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Mizoram** (Bank Branch in Mizoram): Required for DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Village Council (VC) Office / Local Council Office (panchayat)
Submit trade choice and application to VC President.

### SEDP Mizoram Portal (online)
Track beneficiary selection at sedp.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://sedp.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
Family must be verified by the local Village Council / Local Council (VC/LC).

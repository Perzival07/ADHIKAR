# Namo Lakshmi Yojana (Gujarat)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹50,000 across Classes 9 to 12 (₹10,000/yr in 9th & 10th, and ₹15,000/yr in 11th & 12th) to ensure secondary education for adolescent girls.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹50,000 distributed in monthly installments and milestone grants directly into the girl student or mother’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female student enrolled in Class 9, 10, 11, or 12 in a government or grant-in-aid school in Gujarat.

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 13–19
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Student Aadhar U-DISE ID and Bonafide** (School Headmaster): Enrolment record in Gujarat school.
- **Family Income Certificate under ₹6 Lakh** (Mamlatdar / TDO Gujarat): Economic validation.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook of Student or Mother** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### School Principal / Class Teacher Desk (panchayat)
School enters student details on Child Tracking System (CTS) portal.
Link: https://namolakshmi.gujarat.gov.in

### Namo Lakshmi Gujarat Portal (online)
Verify student enrollment and DBT transaction status.
Link: https://namolakshmi.gujarat.gov.in

## Caveats
Family annual income must not exceed ₹6 Lakh per annum. Girl must maintain 75% attendance.

# Sikkim Vatsalya Scheme

## Summary
Financial grant of up to ₹3,00,000 provided to childless resident couples of Sikkim undergoing In-Vitro Fertilization (IVF) treatment.

## Benefit
Financial reimbursement and grant up to ₹3,00,000 in two installments for fertility treatments at empanelled healthcare centers.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a married resident couple of Sikkim seeking medical assistance for IVF treatment.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 21–50
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Certificate of Identification (COI)** (Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)): Proof of Sikkim origin.
- **Registered Marriage Certificate** (Registrar of Marriages): Proof of marital relationship.
- **Medical Assessment & Treatment Recommendation** (STNM Hospital / Empanelled Doctor): Mandatory clinical recommendation.

## How to Apply
### Office of Chief Medical Officer (CMO) / Health Department Gangtok (blockOffice)
Submit clinical case papers and COI for screening committee approval.

### Sikkim Health Department Portal (online)
Check details at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Couple must possess valid COI and clinical certification from authorized gynecologists.

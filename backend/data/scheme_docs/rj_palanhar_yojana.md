# Palanhar Yojana (Rajasthan)

## Summary
Direct monthly financial assistance of ₹1,500/mo (age 0-6) and ₹2,500/mo (age 6-18) + annual clothing allowance of ₹2,000 for caregivers of orphan and destitute children.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹1,500/month (children up to 6 years) and ₹2,500/month (school-going children aged 6-18) + ₹2,000 annual lump sum for clothes and shoes.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a foster parent/guardian residing in Rajasthan nurturing an orphan child or child of a widow/life-convict parent.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Age: 0–18
- Income ceiling: < 1.5 Lakh
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Death Certificate of Parents / Widow Pension Certificate of Mother** (Registrar of Births & Deaths / SJED): Proves eligibility category.
- **School Attendance / Anganwadi Enrolment Certificate** (School Headmaster / Anganwadi Worker): Confirms child is attending school.
- **Palanhar Guardian's Jan Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch in Rajasthan): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Palanhar Portal Rajasthan / e-Mitra Kiosk (online)
Apply online via sje.rajasthan.gov.in or any e-Mitra kiosk.
Link: https://ssp.rajasthan.gov.in

### Assistant Director, Social Justice & Empowerment Department (blockOffice)
Submit verification copy.

## Caveats
Child must be enrolled in an Anganwadi center (age 0-6) or attending school regularly (age 6-18).

# Krishak Bandhu (New) Scheme

## Summary
Annual financial assistance of up to ₹10,000 per year (minimum ₹4,000/year for even fractional land) disbursed in two seasonal installments, plus ₹2,00,000 death insurance compensation for farmer families.

## Benefit
Direct cash assistance of up to ₹10,000/year (Kharif + Rabi) + ₹2,00,000 insurance payout to legal heirs in the event of death of farmer aged 18-60.

## Eligibility
You are a farmer, recorded cultivator, or Bhagchasi (sharecropper) in West Bengal.

- Region: West Bengal
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Kharif / Rabi Sowing Cycles)

## Documents Required
- **Agricultural Land Record of Rights (Parcha / RoR)** (Land & Land Reforms Dept (Banglarbhumi)): Proof of cultivable land holding.
- **Aadhaar Card of Farmer** (UIDAI): Identity verification and biometric KYC.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct seasonal installment deposit.

## How to Apply
### Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) / Duare Sarkar Camp (blockOffice)
Submit enrollment form with Parcha copy.

### Krishak Bandhu Official Portal (online)
Check entitlement and DBT payment status at krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in.
Link: https://krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in

## Caveats
Coverage extends to recorded Bhagchasis (sharecroppers) and Patta holders.

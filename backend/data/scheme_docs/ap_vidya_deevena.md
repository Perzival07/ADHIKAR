# Jagananna Vidya Deevena (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Full tuition fee reimbursement for college students pursuing Polytechnic, ITI, Degree, Engineering, and Post-Graduate courses, credited to mother’s account.

## Benefit
100% college tuition fee reimbursement released quarterly directly to the student’s mother’s bank account to pay colleges.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a post-intermediate student enrolled in an eligible degree/professional college in AP with family income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 17–30
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **College Allotment Order & Fee Receipt** (AP EAPCET / Degree Convener): Proves merit quota admission.
- **Mother's Aadhaar and Bank Passbook** (UIDAI / Bank Branch): Required for fee DBT.
- **Integrated Income Certificate / Rice Card** (MeeSeva): Validates economic criteria.

## How to Apply
### JnanaBhumi Higher Education Portal (online)
Apply online through college verification desk.
Link: https://jaganannavidyadeevena.ap.gov.in

### College Principal Office / Village Secretariat (panchayat)
Principal biometric authentication and secretariat validation.

## Caveats
Students must maintain 75% college attendance. Covers regular convener quota admissions.

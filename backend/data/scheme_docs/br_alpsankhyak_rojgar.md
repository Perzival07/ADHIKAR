# Mukhyamantri Alpsankhyak Rojgar Rin Yojana (Bihar)

## Summary
Concessional business loan of up to ₹5 Lakh at nominal 5% simple interest (with 0.5% prompt repayment rebate) for minority youth to set up independent businesses.

## Benefit
Concessional working capital and term loans up to ₹5 Lakh repayable in 20 equal quarterly installments with simple interest.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Bihar belonging to notified minority communities (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi) aged 18 to 50.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 18–50
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Minority Community Certificate / Self-Declaration** (Competent Authority / Notary): Confirms notified minority status.
- **Family Income Certificate under ₹4 Lakh** (Circle Officer): Proves income criteria.
- **Project Quotation / Business Plan** (Self): Details machinery and working capital.

## How to Apply
### BSMFC Online Portal (online)
Submit digital application at bsmfc.bihar.gov.in.
Link: https://bsmfc.bihar.gov.in

### District Minority Welfare Office (DMWO) (blockOffice)
Submit physical documents and attend interview.

## Caveats
Annual family income must not exceed ₹4 Lakh per annum.

# YSR Aasara Scheme (Andhra Pradesh)

## Summary
Complete reimbursement of outstanding bank loans of Women Self Help Groups (SHGs) across Andhra Pradesh in four annual installments.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of full outstanding loan balance directly into savings bank accounts of women SHG members in 4 annual installments.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an active member of an eligible Women SHG (DWCRA) in Andhra Pradesh.

- Region: Andhra Pradesh
- Age: 18–65
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **SHG Group Code & Individual Member Passbook** (SERP / MEPMA): Validates group membership.
- **Individual Savings Account Passbook** (Commercial / Grameen Bank): Direct DBT receipt.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Village Secretariat / Grama Sachivalayam Welfare Assistant (panchayat)
Verification conducted at Village Organisation (VO) meetings.
Link: https://navasakam.ap.gov.in

### Navasakam Aasara Portal (online)
Check loan waiver credit status.
Link: https://navasakam.ap.gov.in

## Caveats
Member must be part of an active SHG recognized by SERP (rural) or MEPMA (urban).

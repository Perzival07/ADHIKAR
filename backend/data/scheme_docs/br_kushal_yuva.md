# Kushal Yuva Program (KYP Bihar)

## Summary
240 hours of free computer literacy, English and Hindi communication skills, and workplace soft skills training with certificate and ₹1,000 security refund.

## Benefit
Free digital literacy (MS Office, internet, digital payments), soft skills, communication proficiency, and joint certification by BSDM and MKCL.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a youth aged 15 to 28 residing in Bihar with minimum 10th class qualification.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 15–28
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **10th (Matriculation) Pass Marksheet & Certificate** (BSEB / Recognized Board): Validates educational qualification and age.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Residence Certificate of Bihar** (Circle Officer): Proves state residency.

## How to Apply
### BSDM Skill Portal (online)
Register online at skillmissionbihar.org.
Link: https://www.7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in

### District Registration and Counseling Centre (DRCC) / Local Skill Center (blockOffice)
Get batch allotment at your nearest block skill center.

## Caveats
Refundable ₹1,000 security deposit is returned immediately to the student account upon course completion.

# Mizoram Bamboo Mission Subsidy Scheme

## Summary
Capital subsidies up to 50% for commercial bamboo plantation (Melocanna baccifera / Mautak), harvesting equipment, and bamboo artisanal processing units.

## Benefit
Subsidy up to ₹25,000 per hectare for commercial bamboo plantation + 50% capital subsidy on bamboo chippers, charcoal kilns, and agarbatti stick making machines.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landholding farmer or bamboo artisan group in Mizoram establishing commercial bamboo cultivation.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Settlement Certificate / Village Council Land Pass** (Land Revenue & Settlement Dept / VC): Proves land ownership for bamboo planting.
- **ST Certificate / Aadhaar Card** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch in Mizoram): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Divisional Forest Officer (DFO) / Range Officer (blockOffice)
Submit plantation proposal to local forest range office.

### Mizoram Forest Portal (online)
Track bamboo mission guidelines at forest.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://forest.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
Plantation must maintain minimum 80% survival rate for multi-year maintenance installments.

# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY 2.0)

## Summary
Deposit-free LPG cylinder connection, free first cylinder refill, free hotplate stove, and targeted subsidy of ₹300 per cylinder for adult women from poor households.

## Benefit
Free LPG connection with ₹1,600 security deposit waiver + free first refill cylinder + free stove + ongoing targeted subsidy of ₹300 per cylinder for up to 12 refills/year.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an adult female member of an eligible BPL/SC/ST/Antyodaya family without an active LPG connection.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card of applicant and adult family members** (UIDAI): Aadhaar authentication is mandatory.
- **Ration Card / Family Composition Certificate** (Food Dept): Confirms household composition.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for receiving cylinder subsidy DBT.

## How to Apply
### PMUY Online Application Portal (online)
Choose IOCL (Indane), BPCL (Bharatgas), or HPCL and apply online.
Link: https://www.pmuy.gov.in

### Authorized LPG Distributorship (Indane / Bharatgas / HP Gas) (blockOffice)
Submit standard KYC form with distributor in your locality.

## Caveats
Connection is issued strictly in the name of an adult female member (18+) of the household.

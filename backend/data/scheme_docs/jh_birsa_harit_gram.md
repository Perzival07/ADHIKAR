# Birsa Harit Gram Yojana (Jharkhand)

## Summary
Integrated fruit orchard development on 1 acre of unused rural land, providing 100% free saplings (Mango, Guava), fencing, pit-digging wages, and generating ₹50,000/yr.

## Benefit
100% free high-yield fruit saplings, 3 years of free fertilizer/pesticides, drip kits, and over 150 days of guaranteed MGNREGA wages for pit digging and plant maintenance.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a rural family holding at least 0.5 to 1 acre of land in Jharkhand with an active MGNREGA Job Card.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **MGNREGA Job Card** (Gram Panchayat): Mandatory for wage disbursal.
- **Land Revenue Record / Possession Slip for 0.5-1 acre** (Circle Officer / Gram Pradhan): Proves land ownership.
- **Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch): Required for wage credit.

## How to Apply
### Gram Rozgar Sevak / Gram Panchayat Mukhiya (panchayat)
Apply during Gram Sabha planning meeting for inclusion in annual work plan.

### Jharkhand Rural Development Portal (online)
Check scheme guidelines and orchard targets.
Link: https://jharkhand.gov.in

## Caveats
Land must be undisputed and have access to water for sapling establishment.

# Tripura Solar Micro-Grid & Standalone Pump Scheme

## Summary
Free installation of standalone solar microgrids, household home solar lighting systems, and solar irrigation pumps across remote, off-grid tribal hamlets (Paras) in hilly districts.

## Benefit
Free household solar lighting units (LED bulbs, mobile charging ports) and uninterrupted solar mini-grid electricity for remote tribal communities.

## Eligibility
You are a resident family living in an off-grid or remote tribal settlement (Para) in Tripura.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRTC) / ST Certificate** (SDM Office): Proof of state residency in tribal block.
- **Village Council / ADC Chairperson Habitational Certificate** (TTAADC Village Council): Certifies off-grid hamlet residence.

## How to Apply
### TTAADC Village Council / Block Development Office (BDO) (panchayat)
Enroll household during village electrification survey.

### TREDA Official Portal (online)
Monitor project guidelines at treda.tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://treda.tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Targeted for habitations certified as off-grid or transmission-deficit by power corporation.

# Mizoram Universal Health Care Scheme / State Health Care Scheme

## Summary
Reimbursement of medical and hospitalisation expenses of up to ₹5 Lakh per year per family across empanelled hospitals within Mizoram and premier national hospitals.

## Benefit
Reimbursement of inpatient medical bills up to ₹5 Lakh covering surgical procedures, critical cancer treatment, cardiac care, and ICU across network hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a permanent resident family of Mizoram holding a state health registration card.

- Region: Mizoram
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mizoram Health Care Card / Ration Card** (Health Care Society / Food Dept): Primary card for claim processing.
- **Hospital Discharge Summary, Original Bills, and Medicine Cash Memos** (Empanelled Hospital): Required for medical reimbursement.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch in Mizoram): Required for DBT of claim amount.

## How to Apply
### Mizoram State Health Care Society Office, Dinthar, Aizawl (blockOffice)
Submit claim forms with original hospital bills.

### Mizoram Health Department Portal (online)
Download claim forms and search network hospitals at health.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://health.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
Treatment taken outside the state requires prior referral certification from the State Medical Board.

# PM Vishwakarma Scheme

## Summary
End-to-end holistic support for traditional artisans and craftspeople including recognition, skill upgrading, ₹15,000 toolkit incentive, and collateral-free loans up to ₹3 Lakh at 5% interest.

## Benefit
Free PM Vishwakarma Certificate & ID, ₹15,000 e-voucher toolkit grant, ₹500/day training stipend, and enterprise credit up to ₹3 Lakh (₹1L tranche 1 + ₹2L tranche 2) at 5% concessional interest.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools in one of the 18 eligible traditional family trades.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card with biometric verification** (UIDAI): Mobile number must be linked for OTP authentication.
- **Active Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT of toolkit voucher and loan disbursement.
- **Ration Card / Family Proof** (Food Dept): Only one member per family is eligible.

## How to Apply
### Common Service Centre (CSC) Biometric Registration (csc)
Visit CSC with Aadhaar and bank details for biometric trade verification.
Link: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in

### PM Vishwakarma Official Portal (online)
Track application status and download digital certificate.
Link: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must be engaged in one of 18 trades (Carpenter, Blacksmith, Potter, Tailor, Barber, Cobbler, Mason, Sculptor, Basket maker, etc.) and not have availed PMKVY/PMEGP in last 5 years.

# Mukhya Mantri Seekho Kamao Yojana (MMSKY Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
Paid industry skill internship stipend of ₹8,000 (12th pass), ₹8,500 (ITI), ₹9,000 (Diploma), and ₹10,000 (Degree/PG) per month for youth aged 18 to 29 across 700+ courses.

## Benefit
Monthly stipend of ₹8,000 to ₹10,000 directly credited via DBT (75% paid by state government, 25% by industry) + SCVT/NCVT certified practical skill training.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed resident of Madhya Pradesh aged 18 to 29 holding 12th/ITI/Diploma/Degree qualification.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 18–29
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Samagra ID with biometric e-KYC** (Samagra Mission): Mandatory for student profile creation.
- **12th / ITI / Diploma / Degree Passing Certificate** (Recognized Board / University): Determines eligible monthly stipend tier.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account with DBT enabled** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly government stipend.

## How to Apply
### MMSKY Candidate Portal (online)
Register, browse corporate vacancies, and apply at mmsky.mp.gov.in.
Link: https://mmsky.mp.gov.in

### Government ITI / District Employment Office (blockOffice)
Attend district apprenticeship melas and interviews.

## Caveats
Applicant must register and link Samagra e-KYC on the MMSKY portal and sign an establishment contract.

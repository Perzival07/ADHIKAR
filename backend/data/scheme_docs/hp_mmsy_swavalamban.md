# Mukhya Mantri Swavalamban Yojana (MMSY Himachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Front-ended capital investment subsidy of 25% (males), 30% (females/SC/ST), and 35% (widows/transgender) on project costs up to ₹1 Crore for youth startups.

## Benefit
Capital subsidy up to ₹35 Lakh on project cost + 5% interest subvention for 3 years on bank loans up to ₹60 Lakh.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh aged 18 to 45 starting a new manufacturing or service venture.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Himachal Bonafide Certificate** (Tehsildar): Mandatory domicile in HP.
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR) and Machinery Quotations** (Chartered Accountant / Self): Outlines capital investment.
- **In-principle Bank Loan Sanction Letter** (Commercial / Cooperative Bank in HP): Confirms loan readiness.

## How to Apply
### MMSY Himachal Official Portal (online)
Apply online and track DLC status at mmsy.hp.gov.in.
Link: https://mmsy.hp.gov.in

### District Industries Centre (DIC) / General Manager DIC (blockOffice)
Attend District Level Committee interview.

## Caveats
Enterprise must be established within the geographical territory of Himachal Pradesh.

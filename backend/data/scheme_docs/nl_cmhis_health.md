# Chief Minister's Health Insurance Scheme (CMHIS Nagaland)

## Summary
Universal cashless hospitalisation coverage of up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year across 2,000+ impanelled hospitals for all indigenous residents and government staff.

## Benefit
100% cashless hospital care up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year (Category CMHIS General) and up to ₹20 Lakh (Category CMHIS Employees) covering secondary & tertiary care.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an indigenous resident of Nagaland possessing an Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate (IIC) or state ration card.

- Region: Nagaland
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate (IIC) of Nagaland** (Deputy Commissioner Office): Mandatory indigenous resident proof.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Identity verification and e-KYC.
- **Ration Card of Nagaland** (Food & Civil Supplies Dept): Helps match family roster.

## How to Apply
### CMHIS Nagaland Official Portal (online)
Register online and generate digital CMHIS Card at cmhis.nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://cmhis.nagaland.gov.in

### District Hospital / Empanelled Hospital Helpdesk Kiosk (csc)
Show card to CMHIS Arogyamitra for instant admission pre-authorization.

## Caveats
All indigenous citizens of Nagaland not covered under Ayushman Bharat are automatically covered under CMHIS General.

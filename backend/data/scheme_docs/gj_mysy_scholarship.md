# Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana (MYSY Gujarat)

## Summary
50% tuition fee assistance (up to ₹2 Lakh/year for Medical, ₹50,000/year for Engineering, ₹25,000/year for Diploma) + ₹1,200/mo hostel allowance for higher education.

## Benefit
50% tuition fee reimbursement directly credited to student bank account every year + ₹10,000 equipment/book grant + ₹1,200/month hostel lodging assistance.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student securing 80%+ percentile in 10th or 12th board exams admitted to degree/diploma with family income under ₹6 Lakh.

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 16–26
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **10th / 12th Board Marksheet with 80+ Percentile** (GSEB / CBSE): Mandatory merit threshold.
- **College Admission Allotment Order and Fee Receipt** (ACPC / Medical Council): Proves tuition fee paid.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹6 Lakh)** (Mamlatdar Office): Validates family income.

## How to Apply
### MYSY Gujarat Portal (online)
Submit application and verify documents at mysy.guj.nic.in.
Link: https://mysy.guj.nic.in

### Help Center at Government Polytechnic / Engineering College (blockOffice)
Verify original documents at designated government help centers.

## Caveats
Student must secure at least 50% marks in university exams and maintain 75% attendance for renewal.

# Goa Shetkari Adhar Nidhi Scheme

## Summary
Comprehensive price deficit support and direct compensation for paddy, cashew, coconut, arecanut, and vegetable farmers against crop damage and market volatility.

## Benefit
Direct price support payments ensuring minimum guaranteed returns on coconut, cashew, and paddy + 100% crop damage compensation for unseasonal rains.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a registered farmer holding Krishi Card in Goa cultivating horticultural or agricultural crops.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Digital Krishi Card of Goa** (Zonal Agriculture Office (ZAO)): Mandatory identity card for Goan farmers.
- **Sale Receipts from Registered Marketing Cooperative (e.g. Goa Sahakar Bhandar)** (Registered Agri Cooperative): Proves quantity sold for price support.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch in Goa): Direct credit of support.

## How to Apply
### Zonal Agricultural Office (ZAO) / Krishi Bhavan (blockOffice)
Submit sale bills and claim forms to local ZAO.

### Goa Agriculture Portal (online)
Track Krishi Card status and claims at agri.goa.gov.in.
Link: https://agri.goa.gov.in

## Caveats
Farmer must hold a digital Krishi Card issued by the Directorate of Agriculture.

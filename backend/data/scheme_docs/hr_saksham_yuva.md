# Saksham Yuva Scheme (Haryana)

## Summary
Monthly unemployment allowance up to ₹3,000 + honorarium of ₹6,000 for 100 hours of government/departmental work (total up to ₹9,000/month) for educated youth.

## Benefit
Monthly allowance of ₹900 (12th pass), ₹1,500 (Graduates), ₹3,000 (Postgraduates) + ₹6,000 for 100 hours of work, credited directly via DBT.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed graduate/postgraduate/12th pass resident of Haryana aged 18 to 35 registered on the employment exchange.

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Employment Exchange Live Registration ID** (Employment Dept Haryana): Must be active on hrex.gov.in.
- **12th / Degree / PG Marksheets and Degrees** (Recognized University / Board in Haryana): Academic proof.
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Mandatory for income check.

## How to Apply
### Saksham Yuva Portal (online)
Apply online and log attendance hours at hrex.gov.in.
Link: https://hrex.gov.in

### Divisional / District Employment Exchange (blockOffice)
Submit physical documents for employment verification.

## Caveats
Family annual income must not exceed ₹3 Lakh. Maximum participation duration is 3 years or age 35.

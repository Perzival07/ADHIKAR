# Mizoram Fishery Development Assistance Scheme

## Summary
50% capital subsidy for construction of new community and individual fish ponds, fish seed supply, and subsidized fish feed for fish farmers in hill valleys.

## Benefit
50% financial subsidy up to ₹1.5 Lakh per hectare for pond construction, water harvesting check dams, fingerlings, and feed pellet equipment.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a fish farmer or village cooperative in Mizoram holding suitable valley land with perennial stream water.

- Region: Mizoram
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Settlement Pass / Village Council Land Certificate** (Land Revenue & Settlement / VC): Proves undisputed land title for pond excavation.
- **ST Certificate and Aadhaar Card** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook in Mizoram** (Bank Branch): Required for subsidy DBT.

## How to Apply
### District Fisheries Development Officer (DFDO) (blockOffice)
Submit application for soil-water technical feasibility check.

### Mizoram Fisheries Portal (online)
Check scheme guidelines at fisheries.mizoram.gov.in.
Link: https://fisheries.mizoram.gov.in

## Caveats
Minimum 0.2 hectare suitable water holding terrain required with perennial water supply.

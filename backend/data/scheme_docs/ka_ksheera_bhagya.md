# Ksheera Bhagya Scheme (Karnataka)

## Summary
Free nutritious milk (150 ml per child) served 5 days a week to school children in government and aided schools, and 3 days a week to Anganwadi children.

## Benefit
Daily distribution of 150 ml fortified milk prepared from whole milk powder produced by Karnataka Milk Federation (Nandini), eliminating child malnutrition.

## Eligibility
Your child qualifies automatically as an enrolled student in an Anganwadi centre or government/aided school in Classes 1 to 10.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 3–16
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School / Anganwadi Enrolment Record** (School Headmaster): Automatic daily benefit upon enrollment.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary / High School / Anganwadi Centre (panchayat)
Entitlement served fresh daily during morning assembly.
Link: https://schooleducation.kar.nic.in

### Karnataka School Education Portal (online)
View nutrition guidelines and monitoring dashboard.
Link: https://schooleducation.kar.nic.in

## Caveats
Universal entitlement for all enrolled children with zero documentation or fees.

# Mukhya Mantri Nijut Moina Asoni (Assam)

## Summary
Monthly financial stipend of ₹1,000 for HS students, ₹1,250 for Degree students, and ₹2,500 for Post-Graduate girl students to eliminate early marriage and promote higher education.

## Benefit
Monthly stipend directly into girl’s bank account: ₹1,000/mo (HS), ₹1,250/mo (Degree), ₹2,500/mo (PG) for 10 months each academic year.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unmarried female student enrolled in Class 11, 12, Degree, or PG course in Assam.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 16–28
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Self-Declaration of Unmarried Status** (Self / College Head): Mandatory condition.
- **College Enrollment Certificate and Roll Number** (College Principal): Proves active enrollment.
- **Student Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Samarth Assam Student Portal (online)
Apply online with institutional registration.
Link: https://assam.gov.in

### College Nodal Officer Desk (panchayat)
Submit verification form to college principal.

## Caveats
Girl students who marry during their studies are disqualified from subsequent payouts.

# Bhavantar Bharpayee Yojana (BBY Haryana)

## Summary
Direct price deficit compensation paid to farmers when market prices fall below government benchmark prices for vegetables, fruits, bajra, and mustard.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of price difference per quintal into farmer’s bank account, shielding farmers from post-harvest distress sale losses.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a farmer in Haryana who registered crop cultivation area on Meri Fasal Mera Byora (MFMB) portal.

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Tax Exempt)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Meri Fasal Mera Byora (MFMB) Registration Slip** (Agriculture Dept Haryana): Validates crop and acreage.
- **J-Form Mandi Sale Slip** (Market Committee Mandi): Proves sale at market rate below benchmark.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Meri Fasal Mera Byora Portal (online)
Register land and crop details at fasal.haryana.gov.in.
Link: https://fasal.haryana.gov.in

### Grain Market / Anaj Mandi Market Committee Office (panchayat)
Submit J-form and weighment slip for Bhavantar sanction.

## Caveats
Farmer must complete seasonal crop self-declaration on MFMB portal before designated deadlines.

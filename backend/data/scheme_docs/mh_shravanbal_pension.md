# Shravanbal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
Direct social pension of ₹1,500 per month credited directly to the bank accounts of senior citizens aged 65 and above living below the poverty line.

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,500 credited directly into the elderly citizen’s savings bank account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 65 or above residing in Maharashtra holding a BPL card or with income under ₹21,000/yr.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 65+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **15-Year Maharashtra Residence Certificate** (Tahsildar): Mandatory residence proof.
- **BPL Survey 2002 inclusion proof / Yellow Ration Card** (Gram Panchayat / Food Dept): Proves poverty criteria.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### MahaDBT Pension Portal (online)
Apply online under Shravanbal section at mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.
Link: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

### Tahsildar Office / Setu Suvidha Kendra (panchayat)
Submit verification copy for committee approval.

## Caveats
Applicant must have 15 years continuous domicile in Maharashtra.

# Gruha Jyothi Scheme (200 Units Free Electricity)

## Summary
Free domestic electricity up to 200 units every month for all eligible residential households in Telangana holding white ration cards.

## Benefit
Zero electricity bill for households whose monthly electricity consumption is within 200 units.

## Eligibility
You are a domestic consumer in Telangana with a white food security card and monthly usage under 200 units.

- Region: Telangana
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: BPL (White Ration Card)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Food Security Card (FSC / White Ration Card)** (Civil Supplies Dept): Confirms BPL entitlement.
- **Latest Domestic Electricity Bill with USCNO** (TSSPDCL / TSNPDCL): Links meter connection to beneficiary.
- **Aadhaar Card of Consumer** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### DISCOM Electricity Revenue Office / Meeseva Centre (blockOffice)
Link electricity service number with FSC card.

### TSSPDCL / TSNPDCL Web Portal (online)
Verify subsidy status online at energy.telangana.gov.in.
Link: https://energy.telangana.gov.in

## Caveats
Consumers must link their Unique Service Number (USCNO) with their Food Security Card and Aadhaar.

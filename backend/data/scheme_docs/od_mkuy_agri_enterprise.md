# Mukhyamantri Krushi Udyog Yojana (MKUY Odisha)

## Summary
Capital Investment Subsidy of 40% (general) to 50% (SC, ST, Women, and Agriculture Graduates) up to ₹50 Lakh for setting up commercial agri-enterprises and food processing units.

## Benefit
Direct capital investment subsidy of up to ₹50 Lakh credited to the entrepreneur’s bank loan account through Agricultural Promotion and Investment Corporation of Odisha (APICOL).

## Eligibility
You qualify as an individual, group of farmers, or agriculture graduate in Odisha setting up a commercial agro-enterprise.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land RoR / Lease Deed for minimum 15 years** (Sub-Registrar / Tahasildar): Proves site ownership for agro-unit.
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR) and Machinery Quotations** (Chartered Engineer / APICOL Consultant): Outlines capital investment and viability.
- **Bank Term Loan Sanction Letter** (Commercial Bank in Odisha): Confirms loan financing readiness.

## How to Apply
### GO-SUGAM Agri Portal Odisha (MKUY Section) (online)
Submit project dossier online at gosugam.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://agrisnetodisha.ori.nic.in

### APICOL Office / Chief District Agriculture Officer (CDAO) (blockOffice)
Attend District Level Committee interview.

## Caveats
Applicant must contribute at least 10% to 20% promoter equity and obtain commercial bank term loan.

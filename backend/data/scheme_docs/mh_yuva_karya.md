# Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana (Maharashtra)

## Summary
6 months of on-the-job industrial internship training with monthly stipend of ₹6,000 (12th pass), ₹8,000 (ITI/Diploma), and ₹10,000 (Degree/PG) for youth aged 18 to 35.

## Benefit
Monthly stipend of ₹6,000 to ₹10,000 credited directly into the youth’s bank account via DBT during the 6-month internship in private or government establishments.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed resident of Maharashtra aged 18 to 35 with minimum 12th/ITI/Diploma/Degree qualification.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: 10th / 12th
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mahaswayam Portal Registration ID** (Skill Development Dept Maharashtra): Must be active on rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in.
- **12th / ITI / Diploma / Degree Passing Certificate** (Recognized University / Board in Maharashtra): Determines monthly stipend tier.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Mahaswayam Rojgar Portal (online)
Register and apply for internship vacancies at rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in.
Link: https://rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in

### District Skill Development, Employment & Entrepreneurship Guidance Centre (blockOffice)
Attend district job melas.

## Caveats
Candidate must register on the Mahaswayam employment portal and accept an apprenticeship contract.

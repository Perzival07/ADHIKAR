# Nagaland Coffee & Cardamom Mission Assistance

## Summary
Free shade tree saplings, high-grade Arabica coffee seedlings, cardamom suckers, pulping machines, and guaranteed buyback tie-ups with Coffee Board of India.

## Benefit
100% free Arabica coffee saplings and organic inputs + 50% capital subsidy on pulping units and solar poly-dryers + export market linkages to South Africa and Europe.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a landholding farmer in hill slopes of Nagaland cultivating coffee or large cardamom.

- Region: Nagaland
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Possession Certificate / Village Council Land Record** (Village Council / Land Resources Dept): Proves plantation plot ownership.
- **Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate (IIC)** (Deputy Commissioner): Confirms state domicile.
- **Bank Passbook in Nagaland** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### District Project Officer (DPO), Land Resources Department (blockOffice)
Submit application for nursery seedling allotment.

### Land Resources Nagaland Portal (online)
Track coffee mission guidelines at landresources.nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://landresources.nagaland.gov.in

## Caveats
Minimum 0.5 hectare suitable hill slope land required with natural canopy shade.

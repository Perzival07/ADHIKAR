# Kudumbashree Livelihood & Micro-Enterprise Mission (Kerala)

## Summary
Subsidized micro-credit, enterprise incubation, skill training, and guaranteed marketing linkages for 4.5 million organized women across Neighbourhood Groups (NHGs).

## Benefit
Linkage micro-loans up to ₹10 Lakh with interest subvention (loans up to 4% interest) + startup grants up to ₹2.5 Lakh for women collective micro-enterprises.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an active member of a registered Kudumbashree Neighbourhood Group (NHG) in Kerala.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 18–65
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Kudumbashree NHG Membership Passbook** (Area Development Society (ADS)): Proves active membership and thrift savings.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Individual Savings Account Passbook** (Bank Branch in Kerala): Required for micro-loan receipt.

## How to Apply
### Community Development Society (CDS) at Grama Panchayat / Municipality (panchayat)
Submit micro-enterprise project plan through CDS.

### Kudumbashree Official Portal (online)
Explore Kudumbashree Bazar e-commerce and micro-enterprise loans.
Link: https://kudumbashree.org

## Caveats
Applicant must participate actively in weekly NHG meetings and thrift savings.

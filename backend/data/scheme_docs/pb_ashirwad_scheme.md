# Punjab Ashirwad Scheme (Shagun Scheme)

## Summary
Direct marriage financial grant of ₹51,000 for the marriage of daughters belonging to SC, Backward Classes, Christian, and economically weaker families across Punjab.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹51,000 credited directly into the bride or mother’s bank account to support wedding expenses and family dignity.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a parent or guardian residing in Punjab arranging the marriage of an adult daughter from an eligible family with income under ₹32,790/yr.

- Region: Punjab
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Caste Certificate (SC/BC) & Income Certificate** (Tehsildar / Sewa Kendra): Income must be within prescribed limits.
- **Age Proof of Bride (18+) and Groom (21+)** (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Birth Certificate): Verifies legal marriage age.
- **Wedding Invitation Card / Marriage Certificate** (Family / Marriage Registrar): Proves solemnization details.

## How to Apply
### Punjab e-Sewa Portal / Ashirwad Section (online)
Apply online via connect.punjab.gov.in.
Link: https://socialjustice.punjab.gov.in

### Sewa Kendra Punjab (csc)
Submit original documents for scanning and verification.

## Caveats
Application can be submitted online up to 30 days before or within 30 days after solemnization of marriage.

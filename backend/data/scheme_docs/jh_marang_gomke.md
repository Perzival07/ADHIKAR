# Marang Gomke Jaipal Singh Munda Overseas Scholarship (Jharkhand)

## Summary
100% fully funded scholarship covering full tuition fees, annual living allowance (£9,000-£12,000), visa, and airfare for youth to pursue Master’s/MPhil degrees in UK & Ireland.

## Benefit
Full coverage of overseas university tuition fees + monthly stipend + health surcharge + economy return air tickets to Oxford, Cambridge, LSE, Imperial, and 30+ top universities.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an SC, ST, Minority, or OBC youth of Jharkhand under 35 years with 55%+ marks admitted to an eligible UK/Irish university.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 20–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Unconditional Offer of Admission from Empanelled UK/Irish University** (UK/Irish University): Mandatory for scholarship grant.
- **Caste and Domicile Certificate of Jharkhand** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): Validates priority category.
- **IELTS / TOEFL English Proficiency Scorecard** (British Council / IDP): Required for visa and admission.

## How to Apply
### Marang Gomke Overseas Scholarship Portal (online)
Submit digital application at overseasscholarship.jharkhand.gov.in.
Link: https://overseasscholarship.jharkhand.gov.in

### Tribal Welfare Commissioner Office, Ranchi (blockOffice)
Attend interview and document verification before High Level Committee.

## Caveats
Up to 25 scholars (10 ST, 5 SC, 3 Minorities, 7 BC) are selected per year. Family income must not exceed ₹12 Lakh/yr.

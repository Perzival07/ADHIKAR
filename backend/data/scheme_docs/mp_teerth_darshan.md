# Mukhyamantri Teerth Darshan Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

## Summary
100% free pilgrimage travel, special AC/sleeper train travel, hygienic boarding, lodging, and guided visits to premier pilgrimage destinations for senior citizens aged 60+.

## Benefit
All-inclusive free pilgrimage tour to Varanasi, Ayodhya, Rameshwaram, Tirupati, Puri, Dwarka, Haridwar, or Shirdi with dedicated medical team on board.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen aged 60 or above residing in Madhya Pradesh who does not pay income tax.

- Region: Madhya Pradesh
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Non-taxpayer)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Samagra Member ID** (Samagra Mission MP): Matches state citizen database.
- **Age Certificate showing 60+ (Aadhaar / Voter ID)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Verifies age criteria.
- **Medical Fitness Certificate by Government Doctor** (Medical Officer (PHC/CHC)): Mandatory to travel by rail.

## How to Apply
### Tehsildar Office / Janpad Panchayat Office (panchayat)
Submit application form with medical fitness certificate.

### Teerth Darshan MP Portal (online)
Check pilgrimage destination schedule at tirthdarshan.mp.gov.in.
Link: https://tirthdarshan.mp.gov.in

## Caveats
Senior citizens aged 65+ can take one younger attendant/caregiver with them for free.

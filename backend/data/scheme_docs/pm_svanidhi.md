# PM SVANidhi (Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi)

## Summary
Micro-credit facility offering working capital loans starting from ₹10,000 up to ₹50,000 with 7% interest subsidy and cashback incentives on digital transactions.

## Benefit
Tranche 1: ₹10,000 (12-month tenure), Tranche 2: ₹20,000 (18-month tenure), Tranche 3: ₹50,000 (36-month tenure) + 7% interest subsidy per annum + ₹1,200 annual digital cashback.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an urban or peri-urban street vendor holding a Certificate of Vending or ULB recommendation.

- Region: All India
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Certificate of Vending (CoV) / Letter of Recommendation (LoR)** (Urban Local Body (ULB) / Municipality): Proves vending activity in municipal limits.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Used for e-KYC.
- **Bank Passbook / UPI QR Code** (Commercial Bank): Required for loan disbursement and digital cashback.

## How to Apply
### PM SVANidhi Portal & Mobile App (online)
Apply directly or through municipal office with UPI QR code.
Link: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in

### Lending Bank Branch / Microfinance Institution (bankBranch)
Approach designated lending branch with ULB recommendation.

## Caveats
Letter of Recommendation (LoR) can be generated by ULB for vendors left out of vending surveys.

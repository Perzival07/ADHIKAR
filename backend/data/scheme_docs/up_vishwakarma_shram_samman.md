# Vishwakarma Shram Samman Yojana (VSSY UP)

## Summary
Free 6-day advanced skill training, free customized modern toolkits, and subsidized bank loan linkage (up to ₹10 Lakhs with margin money subsidy) for traditional artisans and craftspersons.

## Benefit
Free high-quality professional electric/hand toolkits + daily stipend during training + subsidized bank credit for business expansion.

## Eligibility
You are a traditional artisan (carpenter, tailor, potter, barber, blacksmith, etc.) in Uttar Pradesh.

- Region: Uttar Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card with UP Address** (UIDAI): Identity and residency verification.
- **Traditional Trade Certificate** (Gram Pradhan / Ward Member / DIC): Confirms active artisan practice.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank): For stipend and toolkit registration.

## How to Apply
### District Industries Centre (DIC) / General Manager Office (blockOffice)
Submit application and attend training batch.

### UP MSME Portal (DIUP) (online)
Apply online at diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in.
Link: https://diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must be minimum 18 years old and engaged in the specified traditional trade.

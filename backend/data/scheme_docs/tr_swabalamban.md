# Swabalamban Self-Employment Subsidy Scheme (Tripura)

## Summary
Self-employment scheme providing 35% back-ended capital subsidy on bank loans up to ₹5,00,000 for unemployed resident youth to set up manufacturing, retail trade, and service units.

## Benefit
35% capital subsidy (up to ₹1,75,000) adjusted against bank loan principal upon successful enterprise grounding.

## Eligibility
You are an unemployed resident of Tripura aged 18 to 45 years planning a micro-enterprise.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRTC)** (SDM Office): Proof of state residence.
- **Employment Exchange Registration Card** (Department of Labour): Proves unemployed status.
- **Project Profile & Equipment Estimate** (Self / DIC Guidance): Outlines trade and equipment costs.

## How to Apply
### General Manager, District Industries Centre (DIC) (blockOffice)
Submit project proposal for DLTFC bank forwarding.

### Tripura Industries Portal (online)
Download guidelines at industries.tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://industries.tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must be registered with the local Employment Exchange.

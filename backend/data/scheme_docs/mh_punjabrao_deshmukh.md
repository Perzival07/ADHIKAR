# Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta (Maharashtra)

## Summary
Annual hostel maintenance allowance of up to ₹30,000 per academic year for children of registered farmers, small landholders, and agricultural labourers in professional colleges.

## Benefit
Direct cash allowance of ₹30,000/year (in Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Aurangabad) and ₹20,000/year (other cities) directly credited to student’s bank account for boarding and mess.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student enrolled in a professional technical course in Maharashtra whose parent is a registered small farmer/labourer.

- Region: Maharashtra
- Age: 17–28
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Small/Marginal Farmer Certificate or 7/12 & 8-A Extract** (Tahsildar / Talathi): Proves agricultural family status.
- **Private Hostel / PG Rent Agreement and Warden Receipt** (Hostel Warden / Landlord): Proves paid hostel stay away from home.
- **CAP Admission Allotment Letter & College Bonafide** (DTE / Admitting College): Proves merit professional course admission.

## How to Apply
### MahaDBT Post-Matric Scholarship Portal (online)
Apply online under DTE schemes at mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.
Link: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

### College Scholarship Nodal Desk (panchayat)
Submit verification copy to college principal.

## Caveats
Family annual income must not exceed ₹8 Lakh. Student must not be staying in government-run free hostels.

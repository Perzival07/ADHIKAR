# Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana (Bihar)

## Summary
50% capital subsidy of up to ₹1 Lakh on purchase of new 4-seater to 10-seater commercial passenger vehicles (e-rickshaws, autos, vans) for rural SC/ST/EBC youth.

## Benefit
50% subsidy up to ₹1 Lakh credited directly to dealer/beneficiary account to promote rural passenger connectivity and self-employment.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a rural resident of Bihar belonging to SC, ST, or EBC category holding a commercial driving license.

- Region: Bihar
- Age: 21–50
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Caste Certificate (SC/ST/EBC)** (Circle Officer (CO)): Confirms priority reservation category.
- **Valid Commercial / Transport Driving License** (DTO Bihar): Mandatory for commercial driving.
- **Residential Proof of Gram Panchayat** (CO Bihar): Confirms residency in target panchayat.

## How to Apply
### Bihar Transport Department Portal (online)
Apply online under Gram Parivahan link.
Link: https://state.bihar.gov.in/transport

### Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) / Block Development Office (blockOffice)
Submit paper verification dossier for committee selection.

## Caveats
Up to 7 beneficiaries per Gram Panchayat (4 SC/ST and 3 EBC) are selected per phase.

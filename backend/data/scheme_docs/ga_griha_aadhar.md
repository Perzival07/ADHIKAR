# Griha Aadhar Scheme (Goa)

## Summary
Direct monthly financial assistance of ₹1,500 per month directly credited to bank accounts of married homemakers from low and middle-income families to cushion living costs.

## Benefit
₹1,500 per month DBT credited directly into the woman homemaker’s personal savings account every month.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a married woman residing in Goa for minimum 15 years with family income below ₹3 Lakh per annum.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **15-Year Goa Residence Certificate** (Mamlatdar Office Goa): Mandatory 15-year continuous stay proof.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹3 Lakh)** (Mamlatdar Office): Validates economic eligibility.
- **Marriage Certificate / Ration Card** (Civil Registrar / Civil Supplies): Proves marital status.

## How to Apply
### Goa Online e-Services Portal (online)
Apply online at goaonline.gov.in with digital documents.
Link: https://goaonline.gov.in

### Village Panchayat / Taluka Mamlatdar Office (panchayat)
Submit physical documents for local inquiry.

## Caveats
Husband and wife combined annual income must not exceed ₹3 Lakh. Beneficiary must not be in receipt of DSSS or regular government salary.

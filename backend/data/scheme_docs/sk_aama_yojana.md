# Sikkim Aama Yojana

## Summary
Annual financial assistance of ₹40,000 disbursed directly into bank accounts of non-working rural mothers to inculcate financial independence and household saving habits.

## Benefit
Direct benefit transfer of ₹40,000 per year directly to the verified bank account of eligible non-working mothers.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident non-working mother of Sikkim verified through the Ward Panchayat and Electoral Roll.

- Region: Sikkim
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Sikkim Subject Certificate / Certificate of Identification (COI)** (District Collectorate): Mandatory state residency proof.
- **Aadhaar Card of Mother** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Scheduled Commercial Bank): Required for direct cash transfer.

## How to Apply
### Ward Panchayat Office / Block Administrative Centre (BAC) (panchayat)
Submit application form with COI and bank passbook verification.

### Sikkim State Portal (online)
Download guidelines and verify notice at sikkim.gov.in.
Link: https://sikkim.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must not be in regular government or organized public sector employment.

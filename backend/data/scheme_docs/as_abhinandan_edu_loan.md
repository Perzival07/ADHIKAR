# Abhinandan Education Loan Subsidy Scheme (Assam)

## Summary
One-time government capital subsidy of ₹50,000 directly credited against commercial education loans taken by students for pursuing higher education.

## Benefit
Direct subsidy of ₹50,000 credited to the student’s bank education loan account to reduce debt burden and interest liabilities.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a student from Assam with an education loan of at least ₹1 Lakh sanctioned by a commercial/scheduled bank.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 17–32
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Education Loan Sanction Letter (₹1 Lakh+)** (Scheduled Commercial Bank): Proves active sanctioned education loan.
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) of Assam** (Deputy Commissioner / Circle Officer): Confirms state domicile.
- **Loan Account Statement with IFSC** (Lending Bank Branch): Account for subsidy credit.

## How to Apply
### Abhinandan Online Scheme Portal (online)
Apply online with loan sanction details.
Link: https://assam.gov.in

### Lending Bank Branch (bankBranch)
Bank confirms loan authenticity to state Finance Department.

## Caveats
Loan account must be standard and active (not non-performing/NPA).

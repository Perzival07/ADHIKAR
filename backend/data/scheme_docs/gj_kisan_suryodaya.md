# Kisan Suryodaya Yojana (Gujarat)

## Summary
Guaranteed 3-phase high-voltage daytime agricultural electricity supply (5 AM to 9 PM) for farm tubewells and pumps, ending nighttime irrigation dangers for farmers.

## Benefit
Reliable 8 hours of uninterrupted 3-phase daytime electricity for agricultural pumps, saving farmers from nighttime snake bites and wild animal risks.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an agricultural electricity consumer holding a valid farm connection with DISCOMs in Gujarat.

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Agricultural Electricity Consumer Number** (DISCOM Sub-Division): Validates feeder connectivity.
- **7/12 & 8-A Land Records** (AnyRoR Gujarat): Proves agricultural farm location.

## How to Apply
### Local Electricity DISCOM Sub-Division Office (blockOffice)
Verify daytime feeder schedule for your village.

### GUVNL / Agriculture Gujarat Portal (online)
Check village feeder energization status.
Link: https://agri.gujarat.gov.in

## Caveats
Connection must be registered under agricultural tariff category.

# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana)

## Summary
Comprehensive cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalisation coverage up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year across 29,000+ empanelled hospitals.

## Benefit
Cashless inpatient medical treatment up to ₹5 Lakhs per family per year covering 1,949 medical procedures, surgeries, ICU, and diagnostics.

## Eligibility
You qualify under SECC occupational/deprivation criteria, active ration card, or universal senior citizen (70+) coverage.

- Region: All India
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Required for biometric e-KYC.
- **Ration Card / Family ID** (Food & Supplies Dept): Helps match family records in NHA database.

## How to Apply
### NHA Beneficiary Portal & Ayushman App (online)
Check eligibility and download Ayushman Card at beneficiary.nha.gov.in.
Link: https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in

### Ayushman Arogya Mandir / Government Hospital Helpdesk (csc)
Visit hospital desk with Aadhaar card to generate instant PVC Ayushman card.

## Caveats
Universal coverage available for all citizens aged 70+ regardless of family income. Check eligibility via beneficiary portal.

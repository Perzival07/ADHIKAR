# Uttarakhand Indira Gandhi / State Old Age Pension Scheme

## Summary
Monthly financial pension of ₹1,500 credited directly to senior citizens aged 60 years and above living below the poverty line.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹1,500 per month (disbursed bi-monthly/monthly via DBT) directly to the pensioner’s bank account.

## Eligibility
You are a resident of Uttarakhand aged 60+ whose monthly family income is under ₹4,000 (or BPL card holder).

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 48,000 / year (or BPL)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity and age verification.
- **BPL Ration Card / Income Certificate (< ₹4,000/mo)** (Food Dept / Tahsildar): Income eligibility.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank): For direct monthly remittance.

## How to Apply
### CSC Jan Seva Kendra / Assistant Social Welfare Officer (Block) (csc)
Submit online form with BPL and age proof.

### Social Security Pension Portal (SSP Uttarakhand) (online)
Apply online at ssp.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://ssp.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary must submit annual digital life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan).

# Chief Minister's Rozgar Yojana (CMRY Goa)

## Summary
Concessional self-employment loans up to ₹25 Lakh with 50% government capital subsidy on project cost and 6% to 8% interest rebate for Goan resident youth.

## Benefit
Loans up to ₹25 Lakh with only 2% effective interest rate for women/SC/ST (4% for others) and upfront capital subsidy of up to 50% of fixed capital investment.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Goa for minimum 15 years aged 18 to 45 starting a new business venture.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **15-Year Residence Certificate in Goa** (Mamlatdar Office): Mandatory domicile.
- **Detailed Project Proposal and Quotations** (Self / Chartered Accountant): Outlines capital costs and viability.
- **Educational Qualification Marksheet (8th Pass+)** (School / College Board): Verifies qualification.

## How to Apply
### EDC Goa Official Portal (online)
Download application and check scheme details at edcgoa.com.
Link: https://edcgoa.com

### EDC House, Dr. Atmaram Borkar Road, Panaji (blockOffice)
Submit application and attend Task Force Committee appraisal.

## Caveats
Applicant must have passed minimum 8th standard and have 15 years domicile certificate in Goa.

# Mukhya Mantri Sahara Yojana (Himachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Direct financial relief of ₹3,000 per month credited to bank accounts of patients suffering from specific chronic debilitating diseases like cancer, paralysis, and renal failure.

## Benefit
₹3,000 per month DBT credited directly into the patient or caregiver’s bank account to support continuous palliative medical care and nutrition.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Himachal Pradesh diagnosed with one of the notified debilitating chronic conditions with family income under ₹4 Lakh.

- Region: Himachal Pradesh
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Medical Certificate by District Medical Board (CMO)** (Chief Medical Officer (CMO)): Certifies specified debilitating disease.
- **Income Certificate (under ₹4 Lakh)** (Tehsildar): Income criteria validation.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Direct monthly credit of ₹3,000.

## How to Apply
### Block Medical Officer (BMO) / Chief Medical Officer (CMO) (blockOffice)
Submit application with diagnostic reports.

### e-District Himachal Portal (online)
Apply online via edistrict.hp.gov.in under Health services.
Link: https://edistrict.hp.gov.in

## Caveats
Medical certificate must be signed by the District Medical Board headed by the Chief Medical Officer.

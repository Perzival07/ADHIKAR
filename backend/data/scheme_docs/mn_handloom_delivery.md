# Manipur Handloom & Handicraft Delivery Scheme

## Summary
Free distribution of modern fly-shuttle frame looms, jacquard machines, warped yarns, and ₹10,000 working capital to traditional women weavers at their doorstep.

## Benefit
100% subsidized delivery of modern frame loom and weaving accessories (worth ₹35,000) directly to the weaver’s residence + ₹10,000 yarn purchase grant.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing woman handloom weaver or cane/bamboo artisan residing in Manipur.

- Region: Manipur
- Age: 18–60
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Pehchan Weaver Card / Artisan Card** (Office of DC Handlooms / Textiles Dept): Proves genuine artisan status.
- **Resident Certificate of Manipur** (SDO / Deputy Commissioner): State domicile proof.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch in Manipur): Required for DBT of yarn grant.

## How to Apply
### Project Manager, District Handlooms & Textiles Office (blockOffice)
Submit application with weaver card.

### Manipur Textiles Portal (online)
Track delivery and procurement at textiles.mn.gov.in.
Link: https://textiles.mn.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must operate an existing traditional loom and possess an artisan/weaver card.

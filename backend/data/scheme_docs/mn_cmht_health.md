# Chief Minister-gi Hakshelgi Tengbang (CMHT Manipur)

## Summary
Cashless hospitalization cover of ₹5 Lakh per year for AAY, poor, disabled, and media persons across empanelled hospitals in Manipur and top national centers.

## Benefit
100% cashless hospitalization, critical surgeries, and diagnostics up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year, plus travel allowance of up to ₹300 per visit.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an Antyodaya, poor family, disabled individual, or local media person residing in Manipur.

- Region: Manipur
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) Ration Card / Income Certificate** (CAF&PD Dept / DC Office Manipur): Mandatory poverty proof.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) of Manipur** (Deputy Commissioner / SDO): State domicile proof.

## How to Apply
### CMHT Manipur Official Portal (online)
Register online and check empanelled hospitals at cmhtmanipur.gov.in.
Link: https://cmhtmanipur.gov.in

### CMHT Kiosk at District Hospital / JNIMS / RIMS Imphal (csc)
Approach CMHT Arogya Mitra for instant card issuance.

## Caveats
Card covers up to 5 family members and is recognized across network hospitals in Imphal, Guwahati, and New Delhi.

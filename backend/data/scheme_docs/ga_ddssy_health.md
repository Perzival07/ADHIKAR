# Deen Dayal Swasthya Seva Yojana (DDSSY Goa)

## Summary
Universal cashless hospitalisation cover of ₹2.5 Lakh per year for families up to 3 members, and ₹4 Lakh per year for families of 4 or more members.

## Benefit
100% cashless hospitalization, ICU, surgery, and diagnostics across 447 medical procedures in empanelled private and government tertiary care hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Goa who has resided in the state for 5 or more years.

- Region: Goa
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **5-Year Residence Certificate** (Mamlatdar Office): Mandatory 5-year domicile proof.
- **Aadhaar Card of all family members** (UIDAI): Required for family smart card generation.
- **Ration Card of Goa** (Civil Supplies Dept): Helps match family roster.

## How to Apply
### Goa Online DDSSY Portal (online)
Apply or renew DDSSY card online at goaonline.gov.in.
Link: https://goaonline.gov.in

### Goa Electronic Limited (GEL) DDSSY Kiosk (csc)
Collect smart chip health card across any taluka center.

## Caveats
Open to all residents of Goa for 5+ years regardless of income (nominal annual renewal fee).

# Swasthya Sathi Scheme (West Bengal Cashless Health Cover)

## Summary
Universal secondary and tertiary cashless hospitalization insurance providing coverage up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year, issued smart cards exclusively in the name of the eldest female member of the household.

## Benefit
Cashless hospitalization coverage up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year covering 2,100+ surgical, medical, and diagnostic procedures across 2,400+ hospitals.

## Eligibility
You are a resident family of West Bengal enrolled under a Swasthya Sathi Smart Card.

- Region: West Bengal
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Universal)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Swasthya Sathi Smart Card / URN Slip** (Health Dept WB): Contains biometric and family entitlement details.
- **Aadhaar Card of Patient** (UIDAI): Identity verification at hospital admission.

## How to Apply
### Duare Sarkar Camp / BDO Office / Municipality (blockOffice)
Biometric enrollment and instant smart card printing.

### Swasthya Sathi Official Portal (online)
Search empanelled hospital list and treatment history at swasthyasathi.gov.in.
Link: https://swasthyasathi.gov.in

## Caveats
Card is issued in the name of the eldest female family head; covers parents and in-laws.

# Haryana Old Age Samman Bhatta (Auto-Enrolment via PPP)

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹3,000 per month automatically sanctioned and credited to bank accounts of senior citizens aged 60+ via Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).

## Benefit
₹3,000 per month DBT credited on 10th of every month directly to the elderly citizen’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Haryana aged 60 or above with combined annual family income below ₹3 Lakh verified in Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 60+
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP / Family ID)** (Citizen Resource Information Dept (CRID) Haryana): Mandatory for auto-entitlement.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Haryana** (Bank Branch): Direct monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) Portal (online)
Verify age and income in Family ID at meraparivar.haryana.gov.in.
Link: https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in

### Antyodaya Saral Kendra / Atal Seva Kendra (csc)
Update family record or give pension consent.

## Caveats
Family income verified in PPP database must be less than ₹3 Lakh per annum.

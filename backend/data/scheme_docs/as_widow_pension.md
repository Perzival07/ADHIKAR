# Indira Miri Universal Widow Pension (Assam)

## Summary
One-time lump sum rehabilitation grant of ₹25,000 + monthly pension of ₹300 per month directly credited to bank accounts of young widowed women aged 18 to 45.

## Benefit
One-time ₹25,000 direct bank transfer to overcome immediate crisis upon husband demise + continuing monthly maintenance pension.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a widowed woman aged 18 to 45 residing in Assam.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Husband's Official Death Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths): Mandatory proof of widowhood.
- **Age Proof showing 18 to 45 years** (Aadhaar / Voter ID): Proves age eligibility.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): For lump sum and monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Gaon Panchayat / CDPO Office / Urban Local Body (panchayat)
Submit application form to CDPO.

### Social Welfare Assam Portal (online)
Check pension list and circulars.
Link: https://socialwelfare.assam.gov.in

## Caveats
Beneficiary is transitioned to standard widow pension or Orunodoi upon crossing 45 years.

# Gruha Jyothi Scheme (Karnataka)

## Summary
Up to 200 units of free domestic electricity per month for all residential households across Karnataka (calculated as average consumption + 10%).

## Benefit
Zero electricity bill for households whose monthly electricity consumption remains within their entitled average (up to 200 units maximum limit).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a domestic residential electricity consumer residing in Karnataka with a linked Aadhaar.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Electricity Consumer Account ID (Account ID on Bill)** (Electricity Supply Company (ESCOM)): Required to link meter.
- **Aadhaar Card of Consumer / Tenant** (UIDAI): Identity linkage.
- **Rental Agreement / Lease Deed (for tenants)** (Sub-Registrar / Notary): Required if applicant is a tenant.

## How to Apply
### Seva Sindhu Gruha Jyothi Portal (online)
Register Account ID and Aadhaar online at sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in.
Link: https://sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in

### Gram One / Karnataka One / ESCOM Sub-Division (csc)
Visit with electricity bill for instant free linking.

## Caveats
Applies strictly to domestic residential meters. Tenants can apply using their rental agreement and consumer ID.

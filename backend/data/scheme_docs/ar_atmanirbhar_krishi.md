# Atma Nirbhar Krishi Yojana (Arunachal Pradesh)

## Summary
Front-ended financial model providing 45% government subsidy, 45% bank loan, and only 10% farmer equity for farm mechanization, power tillers, and polyhouses.

## Benefit
Direct capital subsidy of 45% on high-quality farm equipment without requiring collateral mortgage security up to specified limits.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an individual farmer, SHG, or Farmers Producers Organization (FPO) in Arunachal Pradesh.

- Region: Arunachal Pradesh
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Possession Certificate (LPC)** (Circle Officer): Proves active agricultural land holdings.
- **APST Certificate** (DC Office): Validates state domicile.
- **Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch): Required for loan processing.

## How to Apply
### Atma Nirbhar Yojana Portal (online)
Submit online application at dedicated state portal.
Link: https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in

### District Agriculture Officer (DAO) (blockOffice)
Submit physical copy for mechanical feasibility check.

## Caveats
Selected farmers must undergo equipment orientation training before machinery hand-over.

# Mamta Scheme (Goa)

## Summary
Direct cash financial incentive of ₹10,000 delivered in installments to mothers giving birth to a girl child in registered government hospitals or health centres.

## Benefit
₹10,000 financial support transferred directly into the mother’s bank account to celebrate birth of girl child and encourage institutional delivery.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a mother residing in Goa for 3+ years who delivered a female infant in a government hospital.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Girl Child's Birth Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths Goa): Mandatory proof of girl child delivery.
- **Hospital Discharge Card / Proof of Institutional Delivery** (Goa Medical College / District Hospital / CHC): Confirms delivery in government facility.
- **Mother's Aadhaar-linked Bank Account** (Bank Branch in Goa): Direct DBT transfer.

## How to Apply
### Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) / Anganwadi Worker (blockOffice)
Submit form within 45 days of child delivery.

### Goa Online Portal (Mamta Scheme) (online)
Submit online application at goaonline.gov.in.
Link: https://goaonline.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicable up to first two living girl children per mother.

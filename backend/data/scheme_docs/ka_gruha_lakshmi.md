# Gruha Lakshmi Scheme (Karnataka)

## Summary
Direct monthly financial assistance of ₹2,000 per month (₹24,000 per year) directly credited into the bank accounts of female heads of Antyodaya, BPL, and APL families.

## Benefit
₹2,000 per month DBT credited directly into the woman head’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account with zero deductions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as the designated female head of the family on an active ration card in Karnataka.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Non-taxpayer)
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Ration Card (Antyodaya / BPL / APL Card)** (Food, Civil Supplies Dept Karnataka): Must name woman as family head (Yajamani).
- **Aadhaar Card of Woman and Husband** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct monthly credit of ₹2,000.

## How to Apply
### Gram One / Karnataka One / Bangalore One Centre (csc)
Apply with ration card and Aadhaar for instant biometric registration.

### Seva Sindhu Guarantee Schemes Portal (online)
Check registration and monthly payment status.
Link: https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in

## Caveats
Woman or her husband must not be an income tax payer or GST filer.

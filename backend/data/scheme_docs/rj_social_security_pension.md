# Rajasthan Social Security Old Age Pension (Guaranteed Minimum Pension)

## Summary
Statutory guaranteed minimum pension of ₹1,150 per month (with mandatory automatic 15% annual increase every July) for elderly citizens aged 58+ (women) and 60+ (men).

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹1,150 credited directly into the elderly citizen’s Jan Aadhaar-seeded bank account with statutory annual indexation.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a senior citizen (58+ female, 60+ male) residing in Rajasthan with annual family income under ₹48,000.

- Region: Rajasthan
- Age: 58+
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jan Aadhaar Card** (Jan Aadhaar Authority): Validates family composition and bank linkage.
- **Age Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID showing 58+ female, 60+ male)** (Election Commission / UIDAI): Verifies age criteria.
- **Self-Declaration of Family Income under ₹48,000** (Self): Verified during e-Mitra application.

## How to Apply
### Any e-Mitra Kiosk across Rajasthan / SDO Office (csc)
Apply online through e-Mitra with Jan Aadhaar biometric e-KYC.

### RAJSSP Social Security Pension Portal (online)
Track pension sanction and monthly disbursement at ssp.rajasthan.gov.in.
Link: https://ssp.rajasthan.gov.in

## Caveats
Pension amount increases automatically by 15% every financial year in two installments (July and January).

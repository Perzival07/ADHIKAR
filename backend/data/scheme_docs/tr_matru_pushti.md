# Mukhyamantri Matru Pushti Uphaar Scheme

## Summary
Nutritional gift kits containing grocery provisions, iron-folic supplements, milk powder, Horlicks, mustard oil, and dates delivered during 4 antenatal checkups to pregnant and lactating mothers.

## Benefit
4 comprehensive nutrition kits (worth ₹500+ each) handed over during each periodic ANC checkup at local PHC/Anganwadi centers.

## Eligibility
You are a pregnant mother registered for Antenatal Care (ANC) at a Tripura PHC or Anganwadi Center.

- Region: Tripura
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (During ANC visits)

## Documents Required
- **Mother & Child Protection (MCP) Card** (Anganwadi Worker / PHC Doctor): Records scheduled antenatal checkups.
- **Aadhaar Card of Mother** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Village Anganwadi Centre / Primary Health Centre (PHC) (panchayat)
Collect nutrition kit immediately following ANC medical examination.

### Tripura Social Welfare Portal (online)
Review scheme details at tripura.gov.in.
Link: https://tripura.gov.in

## Caveats
Recipient must complete scheduled checkup and immunization records.

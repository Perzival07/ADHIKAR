# Pragyan Bharati Scheme (Assam)

## Summary
Free admission fees waiver, free textbooks, ₹1,000 annual mess dues subsidy, and free scooters for meritorious girl students in higher secondary and degree colleges.

## Benefit
100% waiver of college admission fees, ₹1,000/month mess fee subsidy, and two-wheelers awarded to top female achievers in Higher Secondary examinations.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an undergraduate or HS student enrolled in a provincialized college in Assam with family income under ₹2 Lakh.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 16–25
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Income Certificate (under ₹2 Lakh)** (Circle Officer Assam): Mandatory for fee waiver.
- **HS / Class 12th Marksheet and Admit Card** (AHSEC): Required for scooter / admission eligibility.
- **Photograph planting tree sapling** (Self): Required on admission portal.

## How to Apply
### Assam Samarth e-Gov Higher Education Portal (online)
Apply during online degree admission at assam.samarth.ac.in.
Link: https://directorateofhighereducation.assam.gov.in

### College Admission Helpdesk (panchayat)
Submit income certificate and fee waiver declaration.

## Caveats
Students must plant a sapling at home or college and upload its photo as part of the green initiative.

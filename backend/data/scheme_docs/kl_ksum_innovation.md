# Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM) Innovation Grant & Seed Fund

## Summary
Product development grants of up to ₹12 Lakh (Idea Grant ₹2L, Productization Grant ₹7L, Scale-up Grant ₹12L) for young technology startups and college innovators.

## Benefit
Direct non-equity grant of up to ₹12 Lakh + free incubation space, cloud credits, patent filing subsidies, and access to international tech summits.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an innovator, college student, or registered startup in Kerala with an innovative tech product proposal.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **KSUM Startup Registration Certificate** (Kerala Startup Mission): Generated on startupmission.kerala.gov.in.
- **Product Proposal & Working Prototype Demo Link** (Self): Technical architecture and market scope.
- **Current Bank Account of Startup in Kerala** (Bank Branch): Required for grant tranche release.

## How to Apply
### KSUM Innovation Grant Portal (online)
Apply online and upload pitch deck at startupmission.kerala.gov.in.
Link: https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in

### KSUM Innovation Centre (Technopark / Infopark / Maker Village) (blockOffice)
Present pitch before technical jury.

## Caveats
Startup must be incorporated as a Private Limited Company or LLP registered with KSUM in Kerala.

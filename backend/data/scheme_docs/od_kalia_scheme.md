# KALIA Scheme (Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation, Odisha)

## Summary
Direct financial assistance of ₹10,000 per year for small and marginal farmers (₹4,000 per season top-up), and ₹12,500 livelihood grant for landless agricultural households.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹10,000/year in 2 installments (Kharif and Rabi) + ₹12,500 livelihood package for landless labourers for goat rearing, poultry, and mushroom farming.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a small farmer, marginal farmer, or landless agricultural labourer residing in Odisha.

- Region: Odisha
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit (Tax Exempt)
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Krushak Odisha Farmer ID / Land Record (RoR)** (Agriculture Dept / Bhulekh Odisha): Proves agricultural cultivation or labour status.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook in Odisha** (Bank Branch / Cooperative Bank): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### KALIA Portal / Krushak Odisha (online)
Check inclusion and register at kalia.odisha.gov.in.
Link: https://kalia.odisha.gov.in

### Gram Panchayat Nodal Officer / Krushi Mitra Desk (panchayat)
Submit physical application for Gram Sabha social audit.

## Caveats
Beneficiaries verified through Krushak Odisha portal and integrated with PM-KISAN database.

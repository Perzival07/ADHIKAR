# Meghalaya MOTHER Scheme (Maternal Health Cash Incentive)

## Summary
Nutritional kits, emergency transit home shelter, and direct cash incentive of up to ₹5,000 for pregnant women completing institutional deliveries in Meghalaya.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹5,000 (combining Central JSY + State top-up) + free stay at transit mother shelters (Birthing Homes) for mothers from remote villages + free vehicle pick-up.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an expectant mother in Meghalaya registered with MOTHER app who delivers in a government health centre.

- Region: Meghalaya
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mother and Child Protection (MCP) Card** (Sub-Centre / ANM): Documents 4 ANC checkups and immunizations.
- **Hospital Delivery and Discharge Certificate** (Primary Health Centre / CHC / Civil Hospital): Confirms institutional delivery.
- **Mother's Aadhaar-linked Bank Account** (Bank Branch / Post Office): Direct payment of incentive.

## How to Apply
### Village ASHA Worker / Sub-Centre / Birthing Home (panchayat)
Register pregnancy early on the MOTHER app with your ASHA.

### Meghalaya Health Department Portal (online)
Track maternal health dashboard at meghealth.gov.in.
Link: https://meghealth.gov.in

## Caveats
Mother must be registered on the MOTHER mobile tracking app by the local ASHA or ANM worker.

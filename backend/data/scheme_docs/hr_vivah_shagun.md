# Mukhyamantri Vivah Shagun Yojana (Haryana)

## Summary
Direct marriage financial grant of up to ₹71,000 for daughters of BPL, SC, destitute, and widowed families to ensure dignified wedding ceremonies.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹71,000 (SC/BPL), ₹51,000 (Backward Classes/EWS), and ₹41,000 (general poor families) directly into the beneficiary bride/mother’s account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a parent or guardian residing in Haryana arranging the marriage of an adult daughter from an eligible low-income family.

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 18–40
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Wedding Invitation Card (Lagan Patrika)** (Family / Priest): Details date and venue of wedding.
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Validates BPL / income criteria.
- **Age Proof of Bride (18+) and Groom (21+)** (Aadhaar / Voter ID / 10th Marksheet): Verifies legal marriageable age.

## How to Apply
### Antyodaya Saral Portal (Vivah Shagun) (online)
Apply online via saralharyana.gov.in.
Link: https://saralharyana.gov.in

### District Welfare Officer (DWO) Haryana (blockOffice)
Submit verification copy.

## Caveats
Application must be submitted online at least 30 days before the wedding date.

# Samagra Shiksha Kerala Scheme

## Summary
Free school uniforms, modern bilingual learning modules, smart digital classroom infrastructure, and talent nurturing programs in government and aided schools.

## Benefit
Free textbooks, two sets of uniforms, free school bags, and ₹10,000 cash reward for top achievers in Class 10 and 12 board examinations.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an enrolled student in a government or aided school across Kerala.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 6–18
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **School Admission Record / Student Bonafide** (School Headmaster): Enrolment record.
- **Resident Certificate of Child or Parent** (Village Officer): Eligibility proof.

## How to Apply
### Government Primary / High School Headmaster Office (panchayat)
Entitlements issued on admission.
Link: https://samagra.kite.kerala.gov.in

### Samagra KITE Education Portal (online)
Access digital learning repositories and interactive question banks.
Link: https://samagra.kite.kerala.gov.in

## Caveats
Students must maintain 75% attendance in classes.

# Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana (Unemployment Allowance Jharkhand)

## Summary
Annual financial allowance of ₹5,000 for general educated unemployed youth, and ₹7,500 for women and disabled persons to support competitive exam preparations.

## Benefit
Direct transfer of ₹5,000 to ₹7,500 per year credited into the youth’s bank account to purchase books, application forms, and competitive exam fees.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an unemployed technical graduate/degree holder aged 18 to 35 registered on Jharkhand Rojgar portal.

- Region: Jharkhand
- Age: 18–35
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Jharkhand Rojgar Live Registration Card** (District Employment Exchange): Must be registered on rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in.
- **Graduate / Technical Degree Certificate** (Recognized University): Educational qualification proof.
- **Student Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Jharkhand Rojgar Portal (online)
Register online and submit allowance claim at rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in.
Link: https://rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in

### District Employment Exchange / Shram Kalyan Bhawan (blockOffice)
Submit hard copy with attested degree.

## Caveats
Family must not have any member in regular government service or paying income tax.

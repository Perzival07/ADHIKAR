# Kanya Dhan Scheme (Goa)

## Summary
Direct cash award of ₹25,000 for girl students belonging to SC, ST, and backward communities upon passing Class 10th or 12th standard examinations.

## Benefit
Direct bank credit of ₹25,000 credited into the girl student’s bank account to support collegiate enrollment and prevent premature dropouts.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an SC/ST/OBC girl student who passed SSC (10th) or HSSC (12th) from Goa Board.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 15–22
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **SSC / HSSC Passing Certificate and Marksheet** (Goa Board of Secondary & Higher Secondary Education (GBSHSE)): Proves passing credentials.
- **Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC)** (Deputy Collector / Sub-Divisional Magistrate): Mandatory category verification.
- **Student Bank Account Passbook in Goa** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT.

## How to Apply
### Goa Social Welfare Portal (online)
Submit application online at socialwelfare.goa.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.goa.gov.in

### Taluka Social Welfare Office (blockOffice)
Submit hard copy with attested marksheet.

## Caveats
Family annual income must not exceed ₹1.5 Lakh per annum.

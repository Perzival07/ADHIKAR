# Nagaland State Disability Pension Scheme

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹500 per month and free assistive mobility devices for persons with benchmark physical or intellectual disabilities (40%+).

## Benefit
Monthly direct benefit transfer of ₹500 credited directly into the disabled citizen’s bank account + free wheelchairs, hearing aids, and artificial limbs.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a person residing in Nagaland holding a valid disability certificate showing 40%+ disability.

- Region: Nagaland
- Income ceiling: < 1.5 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Disability Certificate (40%+) / UDID Card** (District Medical Board Naga Hospital Kohima): Mandatory medical certification.
- **Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate (IIC) of Nagaland** (Deputy Commissioner): Proves state residency.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Account Details** (Bank Branch in Nagaland): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### District Social Welfare Office (DSWO) Nagaland (blockOffice)
Submit application with medical board certificate.

### Social Welfare Portal Nagaland (online)
Track pension disbursement at socialwelfare.nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://socialwelfare.nagaland.gov.in

## Caveats
UDID (Unique Disability ID) card is accepted for fast-track processing.

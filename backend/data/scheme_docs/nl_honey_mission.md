# Nagaland Honey & Bee Keeping Mission Support

## Summary
Free scientifically designed modern bee boxes, protective smoker gear, honey extractors, and buyback marketing assistance for rural beekeepers in honey villages.

## Benefit
100% free distribution of scientific bee boxes and colonies + training in stingless bee and Apis cerana honey management + premium buyback by Nagaland Honey brand.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a traditional beekeeper or rural youth group in a declared Honey Village in Nagaland.

- Region: Nagaland
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Village Council Certificate confirming Beekeeping Practice** (Village Council Chairman): Confirms active beekeeping.
- **Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate and Aadhaar Card** (DC Office / UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook in Nagaland** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT of subsidies.

## How to Apply
### NBHM District Team / District Agriculture Officer (blockOffice)
Register with NBHM field facilitator for bee box distribution.

### Nagaland State Portal (online)
Track NBHM announcements at nagaland.gov.in.
Link: https://nagaland.gov.in

## Caveats
Candidate must undergo the mandatory 3-day honey harvesting orientation conducted by NBHM team.

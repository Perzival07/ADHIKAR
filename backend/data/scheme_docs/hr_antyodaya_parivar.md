# Mukhyamantri Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana (MMAPUY Haryana)

## Summary
Targeted livelihood intervention packages, subsidized micro-loans up to ₹5 Lakh, and skill development to elevate the annual income of poorest families above ₹1.80 Lakh.

## Benefit
Zero or low-interest bank loans with 25% to 50% state capital subsidy for dairy, animal husbandry, poultry, small retail, or e-rickshaw ventures.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident family of Haryana with verified annual income below ₹1 Lakh in Parivar Pehchan Patra.

- Region: Haryana
- Age: 18–55
- Income ceiling: < 1 Lakh
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)** (CRID Haryana): Validates lowest income bracket.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Bank Passbook** (Bank Branch): Required for loan disbursement.

## How to Apply
### Block Antyodaya Utthan Mela / BDO Office (blockOffice)
Attend block fair with Family ID to select loan/skill scheme on spot.

### Antyodaya Saral Portal (online)
Track loan sanction and subsidy release.
Link: https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in

## Caveats
Identified families are invited directly via SMS/call to block-level Antyodaya Utthan Melas.

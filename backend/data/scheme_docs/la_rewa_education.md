# Rewa Educational Assistance Scheme (Ladakh)

## Summary
Financial assistance of up to ₹1 Lakh to meritorious and needy Ladakhi students for undergraduate coaching, NEET, JEE, and civil services preparations.

## Benefit
Reimbursement of professional coaching institution fees up to ₹1 Lakh + residential living support for higher competitive examinations.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident student of Ladakh enrolled in competitive professional exam coaching.

- Region: Ladakh
- Age: 17–30
- Income ceiling: < 5 Lakh
- Education level: Graduate / Degree
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Resident Certificate of Ladakh / ST Certificate** (Tehsildar Leh / Kargil): Proves indigenous resident status.
- **Coaching Institution Admission and Fee Receipt** (Registered Coaching Institute): Required for fee reimbursement.
- **Class 12th Marksheet** (JKBOSE / CBSE): Demonstrates academic eligibility.

## How to Apply
### UT Ladakh Official Portal (online)
Submit application online at ladakh.gov.in.
Link: https://ladakh.gov.in

### Office of the Assistant Commissioner / LAHDC (blockOffice)
Submit hard copy with fee receipts.

## Caveats
Candidate must have passed 10th and 12th board examinations from schools located in UT of Ladakh.

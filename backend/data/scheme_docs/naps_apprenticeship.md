# National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)

## Summary
Direct government co-funding of 25% of prescribed monthly stipend up to ₹1,500/month directly to apprentices undergoing on-the-job industrial training.

## Benefit
25% of stipend (up to ₹1,500/month) transferred directly to apprentice’s bank account via DBT + National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) recognized industry-wide.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a candidate aged 14+ (18+ for hazardous industries) with minimum 5th to 12th/ITI/Diploma pass undergoing apprenticeship.

- Region: All India
- Age: 14–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Educational Certificate (10th/12th/ITI/Diploma/Degree)** (Recognised Board / Institution): Determines eligible designated/optional trades.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Mandatory for digital registration and contract signing.
- **Aadhaar-linked Bank Account** (Bank Branch): Required for DBT stipend receipt.

## How to Apply
### Apprenticeship India Portal (online)
Register, browse verified company vacancies, and apply online.
Link: https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in

### Government ITI / Industrial Training Wing (blockOffice)
Visit local ITI apprenticeship advisor for counseling and campus drives.

## Caveats
Candidate must register on the Apprenticeship India portal and sign an approved apprenticeship contract with an establishment.

# Vidyakiranam Digital Education Project (Kerala)

## Summary
Free distribution of modern digital laptops and tablets with pre-loaded educational content for underprivileged school students in Classes 1 to 12.

## Benefit
Free high-speed laptop/tablet with warranty, digital textbooks, and high-speed broadband internet connectivity through K-FON network.

## Eligibility
You qualify as an underprivileged student enrolled in a government or aided school in Kerala.

- Region: Kerala
- Age: 6–18
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Education level: School
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Student Sampoorna School ID** (School Headmaster): Enrolment record in Kerala school system.
- **Priority Ration Card / Coastal / Tribal Certificate** (Civil Supplies Dept): Validates economic priority.

## How to Apply
### Government School Headmaster / Class Teacher Desk (panchayat)
School PTA compiles list of digital-device deprived students.

### KITE Kerala Official Portal (online)
Track digital infrastructure in schools at kite.kerala.gov.in.
Link: https://kite.kerala.gov.in

## Caveats
Priority given to tribal, coastal, and single-parent student households.

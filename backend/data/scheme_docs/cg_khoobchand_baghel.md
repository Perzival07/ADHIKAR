# Dr. Khoobchand Baghel Swasthya Sahayata Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

## Summary
Cashless health insurance cover of ₹5 Lakh per year for Antyodaya and Priority Ration Card families, and ₹50,000 for other general ration card holders.

## Benefit
100% cashless hospital treatment covering medicines, diagnostics, surgical care, and ICU across 800+ empanelled government and private hospitals.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident family in Chhattisgarh holding an active state ration card.

- Region: Chhattisgarh
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Chhattisgarh Ration Card** (Food Dept CG): Determines ₹5 Lakh vs ₹50,000 coverage.
- **Aadhaar Card of Patient** (UIDAI): Mandatory for hospital authorization.

## How to Apply
### DKBSSY Health Portal (online)
Check hospital empanelment and beneficiary coverage.
Link: https://dkbssy.cg.nic.in

### Hospital Arogya Mitra Helpdesk (csc)
Show ration card and Aadhaar for instant treatment approval.

## Caveats
Both state ration card and Aadhaar are required for biometric e-KYC hospital desk clearance.

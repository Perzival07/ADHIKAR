# Manipur Organic Mission Agency (MOMA) Value Chain Scheme

## Summary
Free organic seeds, bio-fertilizers, 100% free organic certification, and direct buyback marketing support for farmers cultivating organic ginger, turmeric, and black rice (Chakhao).

## Benefit
Free organic certification for 3 years + bio-inputs subsidy up to ₹25,000 per hectare + export market linkages through specialized air-cargo flights.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a practicing farmer or member of a Farmer Producer Company (FPC) in Manipur cultivating organic crops.

- Region: Manipur
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Land Revenue Record / Village Chief Land Allotment Slip** (Revenue Dept / Village Authority): Proves agricultural cultivation plot.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.
- **Farmer Producer Company (FPC) Membership Passbook** (Local Registered FPC): Confirms organic cluster membership.

## How to Apply
### District Horticulture Officer (DHO) / MOMA Field Office (blockOffice)
Register for organic cluster mobilization.

### MOMA Manipur Portal (online)
Track organic certification status at moma.mn.gov.in.
Link: https://moma.mn.gov.in

## Caveats
Farmer must sign commitment to zero synthetic chemical pesticide/fertilizer usage on the registered plot.

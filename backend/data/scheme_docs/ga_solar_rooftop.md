# Goa Solar Rooftop Subsidy Scheme

## Summary
Additional state top-up capital subsidy of up to 50% over and above the Central PM Surya Ghar subsidy for residential and housing society solar installations.

## Benefit
State subsidy covering up to 50% of benchmark cost + net metering benefits resulting in zero or negative domestic electricity bills.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a domestic electricity consumer in Goa with clear rooftop ownership.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Latest Paid Domestic Electricity Bill of Goa** (Electricity Department Goa): Consumer number must be clear.
- **House Tax Receipt / Title Ownership Proof** (Village Panchayat / Municipality): Confirms rooftop ownership.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### GEDA Rooftop Solar Portal (online)
Register consumer number and select vendor at geda.goa.gov.in.
Link: https://geda.goa.gov.in

### Assistant Engineer, Electricity Department Sub-Division (blockOffice)
Submit technical feasibility inspection form.

## Caveats
Installation must be performed by empanelled GEDA solar vendors.

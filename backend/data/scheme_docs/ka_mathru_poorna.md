# Mathru Poorna Scheme (Karnataka)

## Summary
One hot cooked wholesome meal daily containing rice, sambar, boiled egg or sprouted gram, green vegetables, milk, and iron-folic acid tablets for pregnant and lactating mothers.

## Benefit
Daily wholesome hot meal providing 40-45% of daily calorie and protein requirements for pregnant women and nursing mothers for 15 months (conception to 6 months post-delivery).

## Eligibility
You qualify as a pregnant woman or lactating mother residing in Karnataka registered at the local Anganwadi center.

- Region: Karnataka
- Age: 18–45
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Thayi Card (Mother & Child Protection Card)** (Primary Health Centre / ANM): Documents pregnancy and ANC milestones.
- **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI): Identity verification.

## How to Apply
### Local Anganwadi Centre (AWC) (panchayat)
Register with Anganwadi worker upon conception to receive daily meal.

### WCD Karnataka Portal (online)
Track maternal nutrition circulars.
Link: https://dwcd.karnataka.gov.in

## Caveats
Meal is consumed at the Anganwadi centre to ensure direct nutritional intake by the mother.

# Goa Yuva Samvad Yojana

## Summary
Free monthly telecom benefit providing 3 GB high-speed mobile internet data + 100 free voice call minutes every month for youth aged 16 to 35 residing in Goa.

## Benefit
Free SIM card and recurring monthly digital data recharge (3 GB data + 100 voice call minutes) directly credited to registered mobile number.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a resident of Goa aged 16 to 35 holding a 5-year residence certificate in the state.

- Region: Goa
- Age: 16–35
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Aadhaar Card showing DOB** (UIDAI): Verifies age between 16 and 35.
- **5-Year Residence Certificate in Goa** (Mamlatdar Office): Confirms state residency.

## How to Apply
### Goa Online Yuva Samvad Portal (online)
Apply online via goaonline.gov.in.
Link: https://goaonline.gov.in

### Authorized Telecom Operator Store in Goa (csc)
Complete biometric e-KYC to collect active SIM card.

## Caveats
Limited to one active free telecom benefit per eligible youth.

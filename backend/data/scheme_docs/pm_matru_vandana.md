# Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

## Summary
Direct maternity cash benefit of ₹5,000 for first child and ₹6,000 for second girl child to compensate wage loss and ensure maternal and infant nutrition.

## Benefit
₹5,000 in two installments (₹3,000 at antenatal registration + ₹2,000 after child birth & vaccination) for 1st child, and ₹6,000 in single installment for 2nd girl child directly into bank account.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a pregnant woman or lactating mother from an eligible priority household.

- Region: All India
- Age: 19–50
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Mother and Child Protection (MCP) Card** (Health Center / ANM): Documents ANC checkup and child immunizations.
- **Mother's Aadhaar Card linked to bank account** (UIDAI): Mandatory for direct DBT credit.
- **Ration Card / EWS / BPL proof** (Food Dept): Confirms eligibility criteria.

## How to Apply
### PMMVY Citizen Login Portal (online)
Register online at pmmvy.wcd.gov.in with MCP card details.
Link: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in

### Anganwadi Centre (AWC) / Primary Health Centre (PHC) (panchayat)
Submit Form 1-A to Anganwadi Worker or ASHA worker.

## Caveats
Women in regular central/state government employment or PSUs are not eligible.

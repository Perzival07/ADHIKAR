# Ganga Swarupa Yojana (Widow Pension Gujarat)

## Summary
Direct monthly social pension of ₹1,250 per month credited directly to the bank accounts of widowed women with income up to ₹1.20 Lakh (rural) / ₹1.50 Lakh (urban).

## Benefit
₹1,250 per month DBT credited directly into the post office or bank savings account of the widowed woman on the 1st week of every month.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a widowed woman residing in Gujarat with annual income under ₹1.20 Lakh (rural) or ₹1.50 Lakh (urban).

- Region: Gujarat
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Husband's Death Certificate** (Registrar of Births & Deaths / Talati): Mandatory proof of widowhood.
- **Income Certificate** (Mamlatdar Office): Within prescribed limits.
- **Bank Passbook / Post Office Account** (Bank / Post Office): Direct monthly pension credit.

## How to Apply
### Digital Gujarat Portal (Ganga Swarupa) (online)
Apply online via digitalgujarat.gov.in.
Link: https://digitalgujarat.gov.in

### Mamlatdar Office / Talati-cum-Mantri (panchayat)
Submit application with husband death certificate.

## Caveats
Pension continues uninterrupted even if the beneficiary’s son attains 21 years of age (historic reform).

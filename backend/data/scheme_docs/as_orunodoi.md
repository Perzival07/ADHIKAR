# Orunodoi 3.0 Scheme (Assam)

## Summary
Direct monthly financial assistance of ₹1,250 transferred on the 10th of every month to the bank accounts of nominated female heads of economically vulnerable families.

## Benefit
₹1,250 per month DBT credited on 10th of every month to purchase nutrition, medicines, and essential family provisions.

## Eligibility
You qualify as a female resident of Assam from a priority family with annual income below ₹2 Lakh.

- Region: Assam
- Age: 18–65
- Income ceiling: < 2.5 Lakh
- Gender: Female
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **National Food Security Act (NFSA) Ration Card** (Food, Civil Supplies Dept Assam): Mandatory for Orunodoi eligibility.
- **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook of Woman Head** (Bank Branch): Required for monthly DBT.

## How to Apply
### Gaon Panchayat / VCDC / Ward Office Orunodoi Sahayak (panchayat)
Submit household verification form through Gaon Panchayat.
Link: https://orunodoi.assam.gov.in

### Orunodoi Portal Assam (online)
Track status and verify beneficiary list.
Link: https://orunodoi.assam.gov.in

## Caveats
Households having members in regular government service or owning four-wheelers/tractors are excluded.

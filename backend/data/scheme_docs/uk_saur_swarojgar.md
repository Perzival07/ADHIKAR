# Mukhyamantri Saur Swarojgar Yojana (MSSY Solar Livelihood)

## Summary
Allotment of 20 kW to 25 kW grid-connected solar power plants to unemployed youth, farmers, and reverse migrants with guaranteed 25-year power purchase agreements (PPA) with UPCL power corporation.

## Benefit
Guaranteed steady monthly income by selling generated solar power to UPCL at tariff fixed by UERC for 25 consecutive years.

## Eligibility
You are a resident of Uttarakhand with own/leased land looking to generate income via solar power.

- Region: Uttarakhand
- Age: 18+
- Income ceiling: No Limit
- Application status: Open / Ongoing (Ongoing)

## Documents Required
- **Domicile Certificate (Mool Niwas Praman Patra)** (Tahsildar / SDM): Residency proof.
- **Land Revenue Record (Khatoni) or 25-yr Lease Agreement** (Revenue Department): Proof of suitable plot.
- **Electricity Transformer Distance Certificate** (UPCL Junior Engineer): Confirms grid connectivity within feasible distance.

## How to Apply
### Project Officer, UREDA District Office / DIC Office (blockOffice)
Submit land documents and technical feasibility report.

### UREDA Official Web Portal (online)
Apply online at ureda.uk.gov.in.
Link: https://ureda.uk.gov.in

## Caveats
Applicant must possess minimum 1.5 to 2 Nali (approx 300-400 sq. meters) shadow-free land near grid transmission line.
